# Book 2 — Lesson 14: Percentages in Context — Practice Sets

**Source content file**, paired with `concepts.md`. Sets build: basic percent
change → multiplier method → successive changes (same %) → successive
changes (different %s) → mixed word problems → spot-the-error (additive

## Set 1 — Basic Percent Change

For each item below, find the percent increase or decrease described.

1. A shirt's price increases from $\$40$ to $\$52$. By what percent does the price increase?
2. A town's population decreases from $2{,}500$ to $2{,}125$. By what percent does the population decrease?
3. A student's test score increases from $60$ to $75$. By what percent does the score increase?
4. Monthly rent increases from $\$800$ to $\$880$. By what percent does the rent increase?
5. A stock's price decreases from $\$120$ to $\$90$. By what percent does the price decrease?
6. A number increases from $45$ to $54$. By what percent does the number increase?

## Set 2 — The Multiplier Method

For each item, use the multiplier method ($\times(1+r)$ or $\times(1-r)$) —
not the difference-then-add approach — to find the new value.

1. If $\$250$ is increased by $12\%$, what is the new value, in dollars?
2. If $\$360$ is decreased by $25\%$, what is the new value, in dollars?
3. If $80$ is increased by $35\%$, what is the new value?
4. If $500$ is decreased by $8\%$, what is the new value?
5. If $150$ is increased by $60\%$, what is the new value?
6. If $720$ is decreased by $45\%$, what is the new value?

## Set 3 — Successive Changes, Same Percent

For each item, find the final value **and** the net percent change from
the original. (None of these should come out to $0\%$ net change unless
you show exactly why — watch Items 1, 3, and 5 closely.)

1. If $\$200$ increases by $10\%$ and then decreases by $10\%$, what is the final value, and what is the net percent change from the original value?
2. If $\$400$ increases by $25\%$ and then decreases by $25\%$, what is the final value, and what is the net percent change from the original value?
3. If $150$ decreases by $20\%$ and then increases by $20\%$, what is the final value, and what is the net percent change from the original value?
4. If $\$600$ increases by $50\%$ and then decreases by $50\%$, what is the final value, and what is the net percent change from the original value?
5. If $1{,}000$ decreases by $10\%$ and then increases by $10\%$, what is the final value, and what is the net percent change from the original value?
6. If $240$ increases by $30\%$ and then decreases by $30\%$, what is the final value, and what is the net percent change from the original value?

## Set 4 — Successive Changes, Different Percents

For each item, find the final value and the net percent change from the
original.

1. If $\$300$ increases by $20\%$ and then increases by $10\%$, what is the final value, and what is the net percent change?
2. If $\$500$ increases by $20\%$ and then decreases by $10\%$, what is the final value, and what is the net percent change? (This is the
   Model Example from `concepts.md` — confirm you get the same answer.)
3. If $\$800$ decreases by $25\%$ and then increases by $20\%$, what is the final value, and what is the net percent change?
4. If $\$150$ increases by $40\%$ and then decreases by $30\%$, what is the final value, and what is the net percent change?
5. If $\$400$ decreases by $15\%$ and then increases by $25\%$, what is the final value, and what is the net percent change?
6. If $\$600$ increases by $15\%$ and then increases by another $15\%$, what is the final value, and what is the net percent change? (These are two equal
   compounding steps — not the same as a single $30\%$ increase; show why.)

## Set 5 — Mixed Word Problems

1. A $\$120$ jacket is discounted $25\%$, and then an $8\%$ sales tax is
   added to the *discounted* price. What is the final price of the jacket,
   in dollars, rounded to the nearest cent if necessary?
2. A town's population of $10{,}000$ grows $8\%$ in year one, then shrinks
   $5\%$ in year two. What is the population after two years?
3. A $\$2{,}000$ monthly salary is raised $10\%$ one year, then cut $10\%$
   the next year due to budget cuts. What is the new monthly salary, and
   what is the net percent change from the original salary?
4. A $\$50$ item is marked up $40\%$ for retail, then marked down $30\%$
   during a clearance sale. What is the sale price of the item, in dollars?
5. $\$5{,}000$ in a savings account earns $4\%$ interest in year one and
   $4\%$ interest in year two, compounded annually (each year's interest is
   calculated on the *previous year's* balance, not the original deposit).
   What is the balance in the account after two years, in dollars?
6. A company's revenue drops $30\%$ during a recession, then rises $30\%$
   the following year as the economy recovers. What is the net percent
   change from the original revenue, and why is it not $0\%$?

## Set 6 — Spot the Error

Each item below shows a solution that uses the additive/subtractive
shortcut instead of the multiplier method. What is the error, and what is
the correct net percent change?

1. *"A price rises $30\%$, then falls $20\%$. Net change is
   $30\%-20\%=10\%$ increase."*
2. *"A $\$200$ item is marked up $50\%$, then marked down $50\%$, so it's
   back to $\$200$ ($50\%-50\%=0\%$ net change)."*
3. *"Enrollment grows $10\%$, then grows another $15\%$ the next year, so
   total growth is $10\%+15\%=25\%$."*
4. *"A stock falls $40\%$, then rises $40\%$, so it's back to its original
   value."*
5. *"To find $20\%$ of a number, multiply the number by $20$."*

## Set 7 — Bonus / Challenge

1. A price rises $10\%$, then rises another $10\%$, then falls $20\%$. What
   is the net percent change?
2. After a $20\%$ discount, a shirt costs $\$64$. What was the original
   price of the shirt, in dollars? (Work backward from the final value.)
3. After two years of $10\%$ annual population growth, a town has
   $12{,}100$ people. What was the original population?
4. A price increases by $r\%$, then decreases by that same $r\%$, and ends
   up $4\%$ below the original price. What is the value of $r$? (Hint:
   $(1+r)(1-r)=1-r^2$, where $r$ is written as a decimal here — set this
   equal to $0.96$.)
5. After three successive $10\%$ increases, a value is $1{,}331$. What was
   the original value?

## Set 8 — Official SAT-Style Application

Multiple-choice (A–D), English, matching the real Digital SAT's format and pacing for this lesson's skill, *Percentages* (Problem-Solving and Data Analysis). Every item below is **original**. It was written by studying the method, trap structure and distractor style of real, officially released SAT Suite Question Bank items in the Percentages category, then building a new problem (new numbers, names and context) that needs the same solving steps. No real College Board question text, numbers or context is reproduced here or anywhere in this book; see the note at the end of this set.

15 Easy / 15 Medium / 15 Hard. Every item uses only the methods in `concepts.md`: percent change, the multiplier method, and successive changes.

### Easy

1. A laptop's price increases from \$480 to \$540. By what percent does the price increase?
   A) $11\tfrac{1}{9}\%$  B) $12.5\%$  C) $60\%$  D) $112.5\%$

2. A city bus fare of \$2.00 is increased by $15\%$. What is the new fare, in dollars?
   A) \$2.15  B) \$0.30  C) \$1.70  D) \$2.30

3. A jacket costs \$80. During a sale, it is discounted by $25\%$. What is the sale price, in dollars?
   A) \$60  B) \$20  C) \$55  D) \$105

4. A school's enrollment falls from $420$ students to $357$ students. By what percent does enrollment decrease?
   A) $15\%$  B) $\dfrac{300}{17}\%$  C) $85\%$  D) $63\%$

5. A club has $240$ members. Its membership increases by $5\%$. How many members does the club have now?
   A) $228$  B) $245$  C) $252$  D) $12$

6. A bicycle's price drops from \$300 to \$240. By what percent does the price decrease?
   A) $25\%$  B) $20\%$  C) $80\%$  D) $125\%$

7. An hourly wage of \$18 increases by $10\%$. What is the new hourly wage, in dollars?
   A) \$1.80  B) \$18.10  C) \$28.00  D) \$19.80

8. A plant grows from $40$ centimeters to $52$ centimeters in one month. By what percent did its height increase?
   A) $30\%$  B) $\dfrac{300}{13}\%$  C) $130\%$  D) $12\%$

9. A dinner bill of \$64 is increased by a $15\%$ tip. What is the total amount paid, in dollars?
   A) \$9.60  B) \$64.15  C) \$73.60  D) \$79.00

10. A phone that cost \$600 is reduced to \$465. By what percent did its price decrease?
   A) $\dfrac{900}{31}\%$  B) $22.5\%$  C) $77.5\%$  D) $135\%$

11. A recipe calls for $500$ grams of flour. The amount is reduced by $30\%$. How many grams of flour are now needed?
   A) $150$  B) $470$  C) $530$  D) $350$

12. A savings account holds \$1{,}000 and earns $3\%$ interest over one year, with no withdrawals. What is the balance at the end of the year, in dollars?
   A) \$30  B) \$1{,}030  C) \$1{,}003  D) \$1{,}300

13. What is $125\%$ of $80$?
   A) $80$  B) $105$  C) $100$  D) $205$

14. Which expression represents $40$ increased by $25\%$?
   A) $40(1.25)$  B) $40(0.25)$  C) $40(0.75)$  D) $40+25$

15. A company's revenue was \$2.4 million last year. This year, revenue increased by $5\%$. What is this year's revenue, in millions of dollars?
   A) \$0.12 million  B) \$2.28 million  C) \$2.45 million  D) \$2.52 million

### Medium

1. After a $35\%$ reduction, a quantity equals $p$ times its original value. What is the value of $p$?
   A) $0.35$  B) $1.35$  C) $0.65$  D) $6.5$

2. A printer's price is increased by $15\%$, and then the new price is decreased by $15\%$. The original price is \$1{,}200. What is the final price, in dollars?
   A) \$1{,}200  B) \$1{,}173  C) \$1{,}020  D) \$1{,}380

3. After a $20\%$ increase, a value is $186$. What was the original value?
   A) $148.80$  B) $37.20$  C) $223.20$  D) $155$

4. A DVD's price is reduced by $25\%$, and then the new price is increased by $40\%$. If the original price is \$80, what is the final price, in dollars?
   A) \$60  B) \$84  C) \$80  D) \$92

5. A savings account has a balance of \$2{,}000 and earns $6\%$ interest compounded annually. What is the balance after 2 years, in dollars?
   A) \$2{,}247.20  B) \$2{,}120  C) \$2{,}240  D) \$2{,}360

6. A tablet's price was reduced by $15\%$. The sale price is \$680. What was the price before the reduction, in dollars?
   A) \$578  B) \$782  C) \$800  D) \$102

7. A shop buys a bag for \$60, marks it up by $40\%$, and then discounts the marked-up price by $25\%$. What is the final price, in dollars?
   A) \$60  B) \$63  C) \$69  D) \$84

8. The number of visitors to a museum fell from $1{,}800$ to $1{,}350$ in one year. The next year, visitors rose by $20\%$. What is the net percent change from the original $1{,}800$ to the final year?
   A) $-10\%$  B) $-5\%$  C) $-25\%$  D) $+20\%$

9. A bakery's number of customers increased by $25\%$ in June and decreased by $20\%$ in July, each compared with the previous month. What is the net percent change in customers from May to July?
   A) $+5\%$  B) $-5\%$  C) $+25\%$  D) $0\%$

10. A monthly phone plan costs \$40. The price rises by $15\%$, and then the new price rises by another $10\%$. What is the final monthly price, in dollars?
   A) \$50.00  B) \$46.00  C) \$50.60  D) \$54.00

11. The population of a village decreases by $5\%$ each year. If the population is $40{,}000$ at the start, what is the population after 2 years?
   A) $36{,}000$  B) $36{,}100$  C) $38{,}000$  D) $34{,}200$

12. The amount of sugar in a recipe is reduced by $40\%$, and then the new amount is increased by $25\%$. What is the net percent change in sugar from the original amount?
   A) $-40\%$  B) $-15\%$  C) $-25\%$  D) $+25\%$

13. A tablet costs \$250 before a $12\%$ discount. After the discount, an $8\%$ sales tax is added to the discounted price. What is the final amount paid, in dollars?
   A) \$220.00  B) \$240.00  C) \$270.00  D) \$237.60

14. The price of a service rises by $20\%$. By what percent must the new price decrease to return to the original price?
   A) $16\tfrac{2}{3}\%$  B) $20\%$  C) $18\%$  D) $25\%$

15. A rectangle's length increases by $20\%$ and its width decreases by $20\%$. By what percent does the rectangle's area change?
   A) $0\%$  B) $+4\%$  C) $-4\%$  D) $-20\%$

### Hard

11. After a $30\%$ decrease and then a $50\%$ increase, the sales figure is \$37{,}800. What were the sales before the decrease, in dollars?

12. The population of a city increased by $30\%$ from 2010 to 2015 and decreased by $10\%$ from 2015 to 2020. The population in 2020 was $140{,}400$. What was the population in 2010?

13. Item A's price is increased by $10\%$, and item B's price is decreased by $10\%$. Both items had the same original price. By what percent is the final price of item A greater than the final price of item B?

14. A price rises by $r\%$ and then falls by $r\%$. The net change is $-1\%$. What is $r$?
