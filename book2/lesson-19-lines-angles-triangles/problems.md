## Set 3 — Parallel Lines Cut by a Transversal

Lines $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$ are parallel and are cut by a transversal $t$, which intersects $\ell_1$ at point $P$ and $\ell_2$ at point $Q$.

Here $\ell_1$ is the upper line and $\ell_2$ is the lower line. At each point, the four angles are named by position: upper-left, upper-right, lower-left, and lower-right.

1. The measure of $P$'s lower-right angle is $72°$. What is the measure of $Q$'s upper-left angle?
2. The measure of $P$'s lower-left angle is $96°$. What is the measure of $Q$'s upper-right angle?
3. The measure of $P$'s lower-right angle is $130°$. What is the measure of $Q$'s upper-right angle?
4. The measure of $P$'s upper-right angle is $108°$. What is the measure of $Q$'s upper-right angle?
5. The measure of $P$'s lower-left angle is $85°$. What is the measure of $Q$'s lower-left angle?
6. Lines $m$ and $n$ are cut by a transversal, but it is **not** stated or marked that $m \parallel n$. One angle formed measures $85°$, and the angle in the alternate interior position measures $95°$. Based on this information, can it be concluded that $m \parallel n$? What, if anything, can be concluded?

## Set 5 — Mixed: Angles + Similarity

3. In $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF$ by the SAS similarity criterion, $AB=8$, $DE=12$, $\angle A=\angle D=40°$, and $AC=10$. What is the value of $DF$?
4. In $\triangle ABC$, point $D$ lies on $AB$ and point $E$ lies on $AC$, with $DE \parallel BC$. If $AD=4$, $DB=6$, and $AE=6$, what is the value of $EC$? (Hint: since $DE\parallel BC$, $\triangle ADE \sim \triangle ABC$.)
5. A person who is $1.8$ meters tall casts a shadow that is $2.4$ meters long. At the same moment, a flagpole casts a shadow that is $9.6$ meters long. What is the height, in meters, of the flagpole?
6. Two triangles each have angle measures of $55°$, $60°$, and $65°$, though the angles are listed in different orders and no correspondence between vertices is given. What is the scale factor?

## Set 6 — Spot the Error

Each of the following presents a worked solution with exactly one error. Identify the error, state which rule it violates, and give the correct answer.

1. $\ell_1\parallel\ell_2$. The measure of $P$'s lower-right angle is $70°$. What is the measure of $Q$'s upper-left angle?
   *Incorrect solution:* "These are co-interior angles, so their measures sum to $180°$:
   $180-70=110°$."
2. A triangle's angles have measures of $2x$, $3x$, and $4x$. What is the value of $x$?
   *Incorrect solution:* "$2x+3x+4x=360$ (because the angle sum is $360°$), $9x=360$,
   $x=40$."
3. $\triangle ABC\sim\triangle DEF$, with $AB=6$, $BC=9$, $AC=12$, and $DE=8$. What is the value of $EF$?
   *Incorrect solution:* "$EF$ corresponds to $AC$, so $EF=AC\times\frac{DE}{AB}
   =12\times\frac{8}{6}=16$."
4. Two angles are complementary, and one of the angles measures $65°$. What is the measure of the other angle?
   *Incorrect solution:* "Complementary angles sum to $180°$, so the other angle is
   $180-65=115°$."
5. $\ell_1\parallel\ell_2$. The measure of $P$'s upper-right angle is $112°$ (corresponding angles). What is the measure of $Q$'s
   upper-right angle?
   *Incorrect solution:* "Corresponding angles sum to $180°$, so
   $180-112=68°$."
