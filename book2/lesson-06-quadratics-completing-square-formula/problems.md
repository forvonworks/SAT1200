## Set 6 — Spot the Error

Each solution below contains exactly one error. What is the error, and what
is the correct solution?

1. $x^2-6x+4=0$ — *"$x=\dfrac{-6\pm\sqrt{36-16}}{2}$" (used $b$ instead of $-b$)*
2. $x^2+2x-2=0$ — *"Discriminant $=4-8=-4$, so $x=\dfrac{-2\pm\sqrt{-4}}{2}$" (arithmetic slip: $4-4(1)(-2)=4+8=12$, not $-4$)*
3. $x^2-4x-1=0$ — *"$x=\dfrac{4+\sqrt{20}}{2}=2+\sqrt5$. The solution is $x=2+\sqrt5$." (stopped after one root)*

### Easy

1. What is one of the solutions to the equation $x^2-7x+10=0$?
   A) $-2$  B) 2  C) 7  D) 10

2. What is one of the solutions to the equation $x^2+3x-10=0$?
   A) $-5$  B) $-2$  C) 5  D) 10

3. Which equation is equivalent to $y=x^2-6x+8$ when written in vertex form?
   A) $y=(x-6)^2+8$  B) $y=(x+3)^2-1$  C) $y=(x-3)^2+1$  D) $y=(x-3)^2-1$

4. Which equation is equivalent to $y=x^2+4x-5$ when written in vertex form?
   A) $y=(x+4)^2-5$  B) $y=(x+2)^2+9$  C) $y=(x+2)^2-9$  D) $y=(x-2)^2-9$

5. What is the sum of the solutions to $x^2-5x+6=0$?
   A) $-5$  B) 1  C) 5  D) 6

6. What is the product of the solutions to $x^2-2x-15=0$?
   A) $-15$  B) $-2$  C) 2  D) 15

7. What is the solution set of $x^2-x-6=0$?
   A) $\{-2,-3\}$  B) $\{-2,3\}$  C) $\{2,-3\}$  D) $\{2,3\}$

8. What is one of the solutions to the equation $x^2+2x-1=0$?
   A) $-1+\sqrt2$  B) $1+\sqrt2$  C) $-2+\sqrt2$  D) $1+\sqrt3$

9. If $x^2+8x=5$, what is the value of $x^2+8x+3$?
   A) 2  B) 5  C) 8  D) 15

10. Which equation is equivalent to $y=2x^2+8x+5$ when written in vertex form?
    A) $y=2(x+2)^2-3$  B) $y=(x+2)^2-3$  C) $y=2(x+2)^2+5$  D) $y=2(x-2)^2-3$

11. Using the quadratic formula, what are the solutions to $x^2-4x-5=0$?
    A) $x=-1,5$  B) $x=4,-5$  C) $x=2,-2$  D) $x=1,-5$

12. What is the vertex of $y=x^2-8x+12$?
    A) $(8,12)$  B) $(-4,-4)$  C) $(4,4)$  D) $(4,-4)$

13. What is the vertex of $y=x^2+2x-3$?
    A) $(-2,-3)$  B) $(-1,4)$  C) $(-1,-4)$  D) $(1,-4)$

14. What are the solutions to $2x^2-5x-3=0$?
    A) $x=3,\frac12$  B) $x=-3,-\frac12$  C) $x=-3,\frac12$  D) $x=3,-\frac12$

15. If $x^2-x-20=0$, what is the greater of the two solutions?
    A) $-5$  B) $-4$  C) 4  D) 5

### Hard

1. One solution to $3x^2-12x+7=0$ can be written as $\dfrac{12+\sqrt{k}}{6}$, where $k$ is a constant. What is the value of $k$?

    A) $\sqrt7$  B) $2\sqrt7$  C) $4\sqrt7$  D) $6$

13. One solution to $5x^2+4x-3=0$ can be written as $\dfrac{-4-\sqrt{k}}{10}$, where $k$ is a constant. What is the value of $k$?
