# Book 2 — Lesson 13: Ratios, Rates & Proportions

**Source content file.** First lesson of the Problem-Solving & Data Analysis
domain (~15% of SAT Math, `research/01-official-sat-math-spec.md` /
`research/02-topic-difficulty-taxonomy.md`). Structure mirrors the pattern
established across Book 2's Algebra and Advanced Math lessons: Definition →
Model Example → Your-turn → Common Mistake box → Vocabulary.

**Domain framing:** Problem-Solving & Data Analysis questions are mostly
"formula-light" — the real skill is *correct setup*, not heavy algebra. This
lesson's content is more rigorous and systematic than anything a student has
likely seen taught explicitly in school (most people "just know" ratios
informally), but it is still core-SAT depth, not Book 3 territory.

**Research grounding:** `research/02-topic-difficulty-taxonomy.md` names
"ratios, rates, proportions, unit conversion" as easy-medium PSDA content
that's "mostly about correct setup." `research/04-common-student-mistakes.md`
("Ratios, Percentages & Proportions" section) documents, as a genuine
**conceptual gap** (not just a careless slip): confusing a part-to-part ratio
with a part-to-whole fraction (e.g. reading 3:2 as 3/2 of the total, when the
total is actually 5 parts). The same section also documents flipped
proportion order, mismatched units across a proportion, incorrect
cross-multiplication, and the fact that a ratio can shift over time in
"years from now" problems. Per the annotated TOC, this lesson also carries
the standing "Nima So'ralmoqda?" check — ratio problems very often ask for
one specific quantity or a *difference*, not the ratio itself or a single
part in isolation.

## Concept 1 — Nisbat: Qism-Qismga va Qism-Butunga Nisbat (Ratio: Part-to-Part vs. Part-to-Whole)

### Definition

*Ikki yoki undan ortiq miqdorning nisbiy kattaligini solishtiruvchi ifoda
nisbat deb ataladi va $a:b$ yoki $\frac{a}{b}$ ko'rinishida yoziladi. Nisbat
ikki xil narsani bildirishi mumkin: **qism-qismga nisbat** (bir qismni
boshqa qismga solishtiradi) yoki **qism-butunga nisbat** (bir qismni
umumiy miqdorga solishtiradi) — va bu ikkisi bir xil son emas.*

<small>(A ratio compares the relative size of two or more quantities,
written $a:b$ or $\frac{a}{b}$. A ratio can mean either a **part-to-part**
comparison or a **part-to-whole** comparison — and these are not the same
number.)</small>

**Bu nega chalkashtiriladi:** agar o'g'il bolalar:qiz bolalar nisbati $2:3$
bo'lsa, ko'pchilik o'g'il bolalar "umumiy sonning $\frac{2}{3}$ qismi" deb
o'ylaydi. Bu **noto'g'ri**. $2:3$ degani — har $2$ ta o'g'il bolaga $3$ ta
qiz bola to'g'ri keladi, ya'ni jamida $2+3=5$ "qism" bor. Demak o'g'il
bolalar — butunning $\frac{2}{5}$ qismi, qiz bolalar — $\frac{3}{5}$ qismi.
**Nisbatdagi ikki son qo'shilganda, butunning necha "qism"dan iborat
ekanligi topiladi — bu qadamni hech qachon tashlab ketmang.**

| | Qism-qismga ($2:3$) | Qism-butunga |
|---|---|---|
| O'g'il bolalar | $2$ qism | $\dfrac{2}{5}$ (butunning) |
| Qiz bolalar | $3$ qism | $\dfrac{3}{5}$ (butunning) |
| Jami qismlar | $2+3=5$ | — |

### Model Example (1-misol)

Sinfda o'g'il bolalar:qiz bolalar nisbati $2:3$. Sinfda jami $20$ o'quvchi
bor. Nechta o'g'il bola va nechta qiz bola bor?

**Yechish:** Jami qismlar sonini topamiz: $2+3=5$ qism. Har bir qism nechta
o'quvchiga teng: $20 \div 5 = 4$. O'g'il bolalar: $2$ qism $\times 4 = 8$.
Qiz bolalar: $3$ qism $\times 4 = 12$. Tekshiruv: $8+12=20$ ✓.

**Javob:** $8$ o'g'il bola, $12$ qiz bola.

### Your-Turn Example (O'zingiz bajaring)

Bir idishda mushuklar:itlar nisbati $3:4$. Jami $28$ ta hayvon bor. Nechta
mushuk va nechta it bor? Mushuklar $= \underline{\hspace{15mm}}$, Itlar
$= \underline{\hspace{15mm}}$

### Common Mistake Box

**Noto'g'ri:** $2:3$ nisbatini ko'rib, o'g'il bolalarni to'g'ridan-to'g'ri
butunning $\frac{2}{3}$ qismi deb hisoblash (masalan $20 \times \frac{2}{3}
\approx 13.3$ — bu butunlay noto'g'ri).

**To'g'ri:** Avval ikki sonni qo'shib, jami nechta qism borligini aniqlang
($2+3=5$), so'ng har bir tomonni **jami qismlar soniga** nisbatan oling
($\frac{2}{5}$, $\frac{3}{5}$), $\frac{2}{3}$ emas. *(Bu — tadqiqotda
qayd etilgan eng keng tarqalgan kontseptual xato, `research/04`.)*

## Concept 2 — Tezlik (Rate) va Birlik Tezlik (Unit Rate)

### Definition

*Ikki xil birlikdagi miqdorlarning nisbati tezlik (rate) deb ataladi
(masalan, masofa/vaqt, narx/dona). Agar maxraj $1$ ga tenglashtirilsa, bu
birlik tezlik (unit rate) deyiladi — masalan "soatiga necha kilometr"
yoki "bir dona uchun necha so'm."*

<small>(A rate compares two quantities in different units. A unit rate is a
rate scaled so the denominator is 1 — "miles **per** hour," "dollars
**per** item.")</small>

### Model Example (2-misol)

Mashina $3$ soatda $180$ kilometr yuradi. Birlik tezlikni (soatiga necha
km) toping.

**Yechish:** $180 \text{ km} \div 3 \text{ soat} = 60$ km/soat.

**Javob:** $60$ km/soat.

### Your-Turn Example (O'zingiz bajaring)

Fabrika $5$ soatda $250$ dona mahsulot ishlab chiqaradi. Birlik tezlikni
(soatiga nechta dona) toping: $\underline{\hspace{25mm}}$

### Common Mistake Box

**Noto'g'ri:** Tezlikni hisoblashda, savol qaysi birlikni so'rayotganini
tekshirmasdan, ixtiyoriy tartibda bo'lish (masalan narxni dona ga bo'lish
o'rniga, donani narxga bo'lish — natijada butunlay boshqa birlik chiqadi,
masalan "dona/so'm" o'rniga "so'm/dona" so'ralgan bo'lsa).

**To'g'ri:** Har doim savolni diqqat bilan o'qing — "narxi qancha" deyilsa,
pulni miqdorga bo'ling (so'm/dona); "nechta sig'adi" deyilsa, miqdorni
vaqtga yoki hajmga bo'ling. Birlikning o'zi javobning qaysi son ekanligini
ko'rsatadi.

## Concept 3 — Proportsiyani Yechish va Nisbatni Masshtablash (Solving Proportions & Scaling Ratios)

### Definition

*Ikkita nisbatning tengligini bildiruvchi tenglik ($\frac{a}{b}=\frac{c}{d}$)
proportsiya deb ataladi. Noma'lumni topish uchun **ko'ndalang ko'paytirish**
(cross-multiplication) ishlatiladi: $a \times d = b \times c$.*

<small>(A proportion is an equation stating two ratios are equal. Solving
for an unknown uses cross-multiplication: $a \times d = b \times c$.)</small>

### Model Example (3-misol)

$$\frac{x}{5} = \frac{12}{20}$$

**Yechish:** Ko'ndalang ko'paytiramiz: $20x = 5 \times 12 = 60$. Ikkala
tomonni $20$ ga bo'lamiz: $x = 3$.

**Javob:** $x = 3$

**Nisbatni masshtablash misoli:** Retsept un:shakar nisbati $3:5$. Agar $18$
stakan un ishlatilsa, nechta stakan shakar kerak?

**Yechish:** Proportsiya quramiz — **ikki tomonda ham bir xil tartib**
saqlanishi shart (un ustida, shakar ostida, ikkala tomonda ham):
$\frac{3}{5} = \frac{18}{s}$. Ko'ndalang ko'paytiramiz: $3s = 90$, $s = 30$.

**Javob:** $30$ stakan shakar.

### Your-Turn Example (O'zingiz bajaring)

$$\frac{8}{x} = \frac{6}{15} \;\Rightarrow\; x = \underline{\hspace{20mm}}$$

Xarita masshtabi $1$ dyuym $= 15$ milya. $4.5$ dyuym necha milyaga teng?
$\underline{\hspace{20mm}}$

### Common Mistake Box

**Birinchi xato — proportsiya tartibini aralashtirish:** chap tomonda
"qalam:qalamlar soni" tartibida yozib, o'ng tomonda "qalamlar soni:qalam"
tartibida yozish. **To'g'ri:** har ikki tomonda bir xil miqdor yuqorida,
bir xil miqdor pastda turishi shart.

**Ikkinchi xato — noto'g'ri ko'ndalang ko'paytirish:** numerator×numerator
va denominator×denominator ko'paytirish (masalan $\frac{x}{6}=\frac{10}{15}$
da $x \times 10 = 6 \times 15$ deb yozish). **To'g'ri:** ko'ndalang
ko'paytirish — qarama-qarshi burchaklarni ko'paytirish: $15x = 6 \times 10$.

### Notation Callout — Birlik Mos Kelishi (Matching Units Across a Proportion)

Proportsiya qurishda ikki tomondagi birliklar **mos kelishi** shart —
masalan bir tomonda "km", ikkinchi tomonda "metr" bo'lsa, proportsiya
noto'g'ri bo'ladi. Avval ikkala miqdorni bir xil birlikka aylantiring,
so'ng proportsiya tuzing.

## Notes for the problems file (Sets 1–7)

1. Ratio notation & simplification (write/simplify, no part-to-whole yet).
2. Part-to-part vs. part-to-whole drill — the named conceptual gap, given
   real dedicated weight: find one part, find a fraction-of-whole, find a
   difference between parts.
3. Rates and unit rates.
4. Solving proportions via cross-multiplication, including scaling a ratio
   (recipe/map-scale style).
5. Mixed word problems combining ratios/rates/proportions, including a
   "what's actually being asked" item (asks for a *difference*, not a part).
6. Spot the error — part-to-whole confusion, flipped proportion order,
   mismatched units in a rate, wrong cross-multiplication pairing, and an
   inverted unit rate.
7. Bonus/Challenge (timed) — multi-part ratio, rate-to-time conversion,
   map-scale, a ratio-shifts-over-time age problem (per `research/04`), and
   an average-rate problem (average of two rates ≠ simple mean unless times
   are equal — ties to the Statistics domain's analogous mistake).
