## Set 1 — Ratio Notation & Simplification

1. Write the ratio $10:15$ in simplest form.
2. Write the ratio $21:14$ in simplest form.
3. Write the ratio $16:24:40$ in simplest form.
4. A box contains $9$ red marbles and $12$ blue marbles. Write the ratio of red to blue marbles in simplest form.
5. Write the ratio of $24$ to $36$ in simplest form.
6. A ratio is $4:9$. What is the total number of parts represented?

## Set 2 — Part-to-Part vs. Part-to-Whole Ratios

*This set reinforces the core idea from Concept 1 — in every item, start by finding the total number of parts.*

1. In a class, the ratio of boys to girls is $3:5$, and there are $40$ students total. How many boys are in the class?
2. In the class above (a $3:5$ ratio, $40$ students total), what fraction of the whole class do the girls represent?
3. The ratio of cats to dogs at a shelter is $2:7$, with $27$ animals total. How many dogs are at the shelter?
4. A box contains red and blue marbles in a ratio of $4:5$, with $36$ marbles total. What fraction of the whole do the red marbles represent (relative to the whole, not to the blue marbles)?
5. A basket contains apples and oranges in a ratio of $5:3$, with $32$ pieces of fruit total. How many more apples are there than oranges?
6. A recipe calls for flour and sugar in a ratio of $5:2$. If the flour and sugar together total $70$ cups, how many cups of sugar are needed?

## Set 3 — Rates & Unit Rates

1. A car travels $180$ kilometers in $3$ hours at a constant speed. What is the car's speed, in kilometers per hour?
2. A factory produces $250$ units in $5$ hours at a constant rate. How many units per hour does the factory produce?
3. If $15$ kg of rice costs $\$45$ and the price per kilogram is constant, what is the price per kilogram?
4. A printer prints $48$ pages in $4$ minutes at a constant rate. How many pages per minute does it print?
5. Which represents a greater rate: $120$ miles in $2$ hours, or $150$ miles in $3$ hours?
6. A runner covers $400$ meters in $50$ seconds at a constant speed. How many meters per second does the runner cover?

## Set 4 — Solving Proportions & Scaling

1. Solve the equation $\dfrac{x}{5} = \dfrac{12}{20}$ for $x$.
2. Solve the equation $\dfrac{8}{x} = \dfrac{6}{15}$ for $x$.
3. Solve the equation $\dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{x}{28}$ for $x$.
4. A recipe calls for flour to sugar in a ratio of $3:5$. If $18$ cups of flour are used, how many cups of sugar are needed?
5. On a map, $1$ inch represents $15$ miles. How many miles does $4.5$ inches represent?
6. On a map, $2$ cm represents $25$ km. How many kilometers does $7$ cm represent?

## Set 5 — Mixed Word Problems

1. At an event, the ratio of men to women is $5:3$, with $96$ people total. How many more men are there than women?
2. A car uses $1$ gallon of gas every $28$ miles at a constant rate. How many gallons are needed for a $182$-mile trip?
3. At a school, the ratio of teachers to students is $1:18$. If there are $36$ teachers, how many students are there?
4. A punch recipe calls for juice to soda in a ratio of $4:3$. To make $56$ cups of punch total, how many cups of soda are needed?
5. A machine, filling bottles at a constant rate, fills $60$ bottles in $5$ minutes. At that same rate, how many minutes are needed to fill $192$ bottles?
6. Aziz saves and spends his monthly income in a ratio of $2:5$ (savings to spending). If he spends $\$350$, how much does he save?

## Set 6 — Spot the Error

Each item below contains exactly one error. Identify the error and give the correct answer.

1. The ratio of red to blue marbles is $3:7$, with $50$ marbles total. *Incorrect solution:* "Red $=\frac{3}{7}\times50\approx21.43$."
2. $\dfrac{x}{6}=\dfrac{10}{15}$. *Incorrect solution:* "Cross-multiplying: $x\times10=6\times15$, so $10x=90$, $x=9$."
3. A train travels $120$ km in $2$ hours $30$ minutes. *Incorrect solution:* "Speed $=120\div2=60$ km/h."
4. The ratio of pencils to pens is $4:9$. If there are $36$ pens, how many pencils are there? *Incorrect solution:* "$\frac{4}{9}=\frac{36}{p}$, so cross-multiplying gives $4p=324$, $p=81$."
5. At a store, $5$ notebooks cost $\$20$ total. Find the price of one notebook. *Incorrect solution:* "Unit rate $=\frac{5}{20}=0.25$, so one notebook costs $\$0.25$."

## Set 7 — Bonus / Challenge (timed, hardest — 2+ steps)

1. A country's gold, silver, and bronze medal counts are in a ratio of $2:3:4$, with $54$ medals total. How many more bronze medals are there than gold medals?
2. A factory, operating at a constant rate, produces $180$ units in $4$ hours. At that same rate, how many hours are needed to produce $540$ units?
3. On a map, $3$ cm represents $40$ km. How many centimeters on the map represent $150$ km?
4. The ratio of Aziz's age to Bekzod's age is currently $3:4$. Five years ago, this ratio was $2:3$. Find their current ages.
5. A car travels at $60$ miles per hour for the first $2$ hours of a trip, then at $45$ miles per hour for the next $3$ hours. What is the car's average speed for the entire trip?

**Set 5:** 1) $24$ more  2) $6.5$ gallons  3) $648$ students  4) $24$ cups  5) $16$ minutes  6) $\$140$

**Set 6:**
1. Error — the red marbles were computed against the denominator alone ($7$) instead of the total number of parts ($3+7=10$), confusing part-to-part with part-to-whole. Correct: $\frac{3}{10}\times50=15$.
2. Error — the cross-multiplication paired the wrong terms (numerator×numerator instead of the correct cross terms). Correct: $15x=6\times10=60$, $x=4$.
3. Error — $2$ hours $30$ minutes was never converted to $2.5$ hours; the $30$ minutes was simply dropped. Correct: $120\div2.5=48$ km/h.
4. Error — the positions of pencils and pens in the proportion were swapped, placing $36$ on the wrong side. Correct: $\frac{4}{9}=\frac{p}{36}$, $9p=144$, $p=16$.
5. Error — the unit rate was computed inverted (notebooks per dollar instead of dollars per notebook) and labeled as the price. Correct: $20\div5=4$ dollars/notebook.

**Set 7:** 1) $12$ more  2) $12$ hours  3) $11.25$ cm  4) Aziz is $15$, Bekzod is $20$  5) $51$ mph
