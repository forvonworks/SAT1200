## Set 2 — Exponential Growth: Write the Equation, Then Evaluate

1. The population of a town is currently $200$ people and grows at a
   constant rate of $5\%$ per year. What is the population of the town
   after $4$ years?
2. An investment of $\$1000$ grows at a constant rate of $6\%$ per year.
   What is the value of the investment after $3$ years?
3. A culture of bacteria currently contains $50$ bacteria, and the number
   of bacteria doubles every hour. What is the number of bacteria in the
   culture after $5$ hours?
4. The population of a city is currently $8000$ people and grows at a
   constant rate of $3\%$ per year. What is the population of the city
   after $2$ years?
5. The price of an item is currently $\$20$ and increases at a constant
   rate of $10\%$ per year. What is the price of the item after $3$ years?
6. A quantity is currently $12$ and increases by a constant factor of
   $1.5$ with each step. What is the value of the quantity after $4$
   steps?

## Set 3 — Exponential Decay: Write the Equation, Then Evaluate

## Set 4 — Mixed: Determine the Model, Then Evaluate

For each situation below, first determine whether the relationship is
best modeled by a linear or an exponential function, then evaluate.

1. The height of a plant is currently $10$ cm, and the height increases by
   $3$ cm each week. What is the height of the plant after $6$ weeks?
2. A savings account currently has a balance of $\$500$ and earns interest
   at a constant rate of $4\%$ per year. What is the balance of the
   account after $3$ years?
3. The battery charge of a phone is currently $100\%$, and the charge
   decreases at a constant rate of $15\%$ per hour. What is the charge of
   the phone after $2$ hours?
4. A pool currently holds $2000$ liters of water, and water drains from
   the pool at a constant rate of $50$ liters per minute. What is the
   amount of water remaining in the pool after $10$ minutes?
5. A population of bacteria is currently $4$ and triples each day. What is
   the population of bacteria after $4$ days?
6. A runner has currently traveled a distance of $1$ mile, and the
   distance traveled increases by $2$ miles each day. What is the total
   distance traveled after $5$ days?

## Set 5 — Half-Life

1. A substance with a mass of $160$ grams has a half-life of $4$ years.
   What is the mass of the substance that remains after $12$ years?
2. A drug with a mass of $500$ mg has a half-life of $6$ hours. What mass
   of the drug remains in the body after $18$ hours?
3. An isotope with a mass of $80$ grams has a half-life of $10$ days. What
   mass of the isotope remains after $30$ days?
4. The population of a certain species is currently $6400$ and is modeled
   by a function that halves every $5$ years. What is the population
   after $20$ years?
5. A population of $1000$ bacteria decreases such that the population
   halves every $3$ hours. What is the population of bacteria after $9$
   hours?

## Set 6 — Identify the Error

1. The population of a city is currently $10000$ and grows at a constant
   rate of $7\%$ per year. A student was asked to find the population
   after $2$ years and wrote: *"$10000\times(1.7)^2=28900$."* What error
   did the student make?
2. A sample currently has a mass of $500$ grams and decreases at a
   constant rate of $20\%$ per year. A student was asked to find the mass
   of the sample after $2$ years and wrote: *"$500\times(1+0.2)^2=720$."*
   What error did the student make?
3. A function is defined by the table of values $y=2,4,8,16$ for
   $x=0,1,2,3$. A student claims: *"This function is linear with a
   common difference of $2$, so $y=2+2x$."* What error did the student
   make?
4. The value of a car is currently $\$20000$ and decreases at a constant
   rate of $\$1500$ per year. A student claims: *"This relationship is
   exponential: $y=20000\times(1-0.075)^t$."* What error did the student
   make?
5. A substance with a mass of $240$ grams decays, and a student was asked
   to find the mass that remains after $3$ half-lives. The student wrote:
   *"$240 - 3\times\frac{1}{2}\times240 = 240-360=-120$."* What error did
   the student make?

## Set 7 — Bonus / Challenge: Work Backward

1. The population of a town grows from $200$ to $242$ over $2$ years at a
   constant annual percent rate. What is the annual percent rate of
   growth?
2. A substance decays to $\frac14$ of its original mass after $10$ days.
   What is the half-life of the substance, in days?
3. An investment grows from $\$1000$ to $\$1331$ over $3$ years at a
   constant annual percent rate. What is the annual percent rate of
   growth?
4. The value of a car is currently $\$25000$ and depreciates to $\$20000$
   after $1$ year at a constant annual percent rate. What is the annual
   percent rate of depreciation, and what will the value of the car be
   after a total of $2$ years?
5. A quantity increases from $3$ to $48$ over $4$ equal steps. Is the
   quantity best modeled by a linear or an exponential function, and what
   is the equation that defines the function?

**Set 6:**
1. Error — $7\%$ was treated as $1.7$ instead of $1.07$ (misplaced the
   decimal in the growth factor). Correct: $10000\times1.07^2\approx11449$.
2. Error — used the growth form $(1+r)$ for a decaying quantity instead of
   $(1-r)$. Correct: $500\times0.8^2=320$.
3. Error — the differences $2,4,8$ are NOT constant, so it isn't linear;
   the ratios $2,2,2$ ARE constant, so it's exponential. Correct:
   $y=2\times2^x$.
4. Error — a constant DOLLAR amount per year ($\$1500$) is linear, not
   exponential, regardless of the word "rate." Correct: $y=20000-1500t$.
5. Error — treated repeated halving as repeated subtraction (additive)
   instead of repeated multiplication (multiplicative) — the negative
   result itself is a giveaway something's wrong, since an amount can
   never go below zero this way. Correct: $240\times(\frac12)^3=30$ g.

**Set 7:**
1. $r=10\%$
2. $h=5$ days
3. $r=10\%$
4. $r=20\%$; after $2$ years, $\$16000$
5. Exponential, $y=3\times2^x$
