# Book 2 — Lesson 4: Word-Problem Translation & Modeling — Problem Sets

**Source content file**, paired with `concepts.md`. Set 1 deliberately asks
for translation only — no solving — to check the translation skill on its
own, the way `concepts.md` argues it should be taught. All solved answers
verified by a `sympy` script, not hand-computed.

---

## Set 1 — Translate Only (do not solve)

Write the expression, equation, or inequality. Do not find a numeric answer.

1. The sum of a number $x$ and 9.
2. 7 less than a number $n$.
3. A number $y$ decreased by 12.
4. Twice a number $x$, increased by 5.
5. The product of a number $n$ and 4, decreased by 3.
6. 5 more than three times a number $x$.
7. A number $n$ is at least 10.
8. A number $x$, decreased by 6, is no more than 20.

## Set 2 — Translate & Solve (one-step)

1. 7 less than a number $n$ is 15. Find $n$.
2. A number $x$ increased by 9 is 23. Find $x$.
3. Twice a number $y$ is 36. Find $y$.
4. A number decreased by 8 is 15. Find the number.
5. 15 less than a number $m$ is 7. Find $m$.
6. The quotient of a number $k$ and 4 is 9. Find $k$.

## Set 3 — Translate & Solve (two-step)

1. 5 more than twice a number $x$ is 17. Find $x$.
2. 8 less than three times a number $n$ is 19. Find $n$.
3. Twice the sum of a number $y$ and 4 is 26. Find $y$.
4. 7 less than half a number $k$ is 3. Find $k$.
5. Three less than four times a number $m$ equals twice the number,
   increased by 9. Find $m$.
6. A number $x$, decreased by 6, is doubled to get 28. Find $x$.

## Set 4 — Full Word Problems (modeling)

1. A rectangle's length is 2 more than three times its width. The
   perimeter is 44. Find the width.
2. The sum of two consecutive integers is 45. Find the smaller integer.
3. Maria is 4 years older than twice her brother's age. The sum of their
   ages is 31. Find her brother's age.
4. A shirt costs \$8 more than twice the cost of a hat. Together they cost
   \$38. Find the cost of the hat.
5. A number is increased by 20% of itself to get 72. Find the original
   number.

## Set 5 — Inequality Translation & Solve

1. A number decreased by 5 is at least 10. Find the possible values of
   the number.
2. Three times a number is no more than 21. Find the possible values.
3. 5 more than a number is less than 12. Find the possible values.
4. Twice a number, decreased by 4, is at least 10. Find the possible
   values.
5. Translate only: a number $x$ is more than 8.

## Set 6 — Spot the Error (the reverse-order trap, every time)

Each item is mistranslated using the Concept 2 trap. Name the error and
give the correct translation (and solution, where one is asked for).

1. Translate: "9 less than a number $x$." — *shown as* $9-x$.
2. "15 less than a number $n$ equals 20. Find $n$." — *shown as*
   $15-n=20$.
3. Translate: "6 subtracted from a number $y$." — *shown as* $6-y$.
4. Translate: "A number $x$ is less than 10." — *shown as* $10-x$.
5. "Twice the difference of a number and 3 is 10. Find the number." —
   *shown as* $2n-3=10$.

## Set 7 — Bonus / Challenge (timed, multi-step)

1. The sum of three consecutive even integers is 90. Find the **largest**
   integer.
2. A rope is cut into two pieces. One piece is 5 meters longer than twice
   the other. The total length is 35 meters. Find the length of the
   **shorter** piece.
3. John has \$15 more than twice as much money as Maria. Together they
   have \$120. How much money does **John** have?
4. A number increased by 30% of itself, then decreased by 5, equals 47.
   Find the original number.
5. Twice the sum of a number and 7 is 10 less than five times the number.
   Find the number.

## Answer Key

**Set 1:**
1. $x+9$
2. $n-7$
3. $y-12$
4. $2x+5$
5. $4n-3$
6. $3x+5$
7. $n\geq10$
8. $x-6\leq20$

**Set 2:** 1) $n=22$  2) $x=14$  3) $y=18$  4) $x=23$  5) $m=22$  6) $k=36$

**Set 3:** 1) $x=6$  2) $n=9$  3) $y=9$  4) $k=20$  5) $m=6$  6) $x=20$

**Set 4:**
1. width $=5$ (length $=17$)
2. smaller integer $=22$
3. brother's age $=9$
4. hat $=\$10$
5. original number $=60$

**Set 5:** 1) $x\geq15$  2) $x\leq7$  3) $x<7$  4) $x\geq7$  5) $x>8$

**Set 6:**
1. Error — read left to right instead of reversing order for "less than."
   Correct: $x-9$.
2. Error — same reversal, applied to an equation. Correct: $n-15=20$,
   so $n=35$ (not $n=-5$).
3. Error — "subtracted from" reverses order the same way "less than"
   does: "$A$ subtracted from $B$" means $B-A$. Correct: $y-6$.
4. Error — "is less than" is a *comparison* (Concept 3), not the
   reverse-order subtraction phrase (Concept 2); no subtraction happens
   at all. Correct: $x<10$.
5. Error — "the difference of a number and 3" must be grouped in
   parentheses before doubling; only the $n$ was doubled, not the whole
   difference. Correct: $2(n-3)=10$, so $n=8$.

**Set 7:** 1) largest $=32$  2) shorter piece $=10$ m  3) John $=\$85$
4) original number $=40$  5) the number $=8$
