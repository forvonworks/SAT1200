# Book 2 — Lesson 4: Word-Problem Translation & Modeling — Problem Sets

**Source content file**, paired with `concepts.md`. Set 1 deliberately asks
for translation only — no solving — to check the translation skill on its
own, the way `concepts.md` argues it should be taught. All solved answers
verified by a `sympy` script, not hand-computed.

---

## Set 1 — Translate Only (do not solve)

For each situation, identify the expression, equation, or inequality that
represents it. Do not solve for a numeric value.

1. Which expression represents the sum of a number $x$ and $9$?
2. Which expression represents a value $7$ less than a number $n$?
3. Which expression represents a number $y$ decreased by $12$?
4. Which expression represents twice a number $x$, increased by $5$?
5. Which expression represents the product of a number $n$ and $4$,
   decreased by $3$?
6. Which expression represents a value $5$ more than three times a
   number $x$?
7. Which inequality represents the statement: a number $n$ is at
   least $10$?
8. Which inequality represents the statement: a number $x$, decreased
   by $6$, is no more than $20$?

## Set 2 — Translate & Solve (one-step)

1. Seven less than a number $n$ is $15$. What is the value of $n$?
2. A number $x$ increased by $9$ is $23$. What is the value of $x$?
3. Twice a number $y$ is $36$. What is the value of $y$?
4. A number decreased by $8$ is $15$. What is the number?
5. Fifteen less than a number $m$ is $7$. What is the value of $m$?
6. The quotient of a number $k$ and $4$ is $9$. What is the value of $k$?

## Set 3 — Translate & Solve (two-step)

1. Five more than twice a number $x$ is $17$. What is the value of $x$?
2. Eight less than three times a number $n$ is $19$. What is the
   value of $n$?
3. Twice the sum of a number $y$ and $4$ is $26$. What is the value of $y$?
4. Seven less than half a number $k$ is $3$. What is the value of $k$?
5. Three less than four times a number $m$ equals twice the number,
   increased by $9$. What is the value of $m$?
6. A number $x$, decreased by $6$, is doubled to get $28$. What is the
   value of $x$?

## Set 4 — Full Word Problems (modeling)

1. A rectangle's length is $2$ more than three times its width. The
   perimeter is $44$. What is the width of the rectangle?
2. The sum of two consecutive integers is $45$. What is the smaller
   of the two integers?
3. Maria is $4$ years older than twice her brother's age. The sum of
   their ages is $31$. What is her brother's age?
4. A shirt costs \$8 more than twice the cost of a hat. Together they
   cost \$38. What is the cost of the hat?
5. A number is increased by $20\%$ of itself to get $72$. What is the
   original number?

## Set 5 — Inequality Translation & Solve

1. A number decreased by $5$ is at least $10$. What are the possible
   values of the number?
2. Three times a number is no more than $21$. What are the possible
   values of the number?
3. Five more than a number is less than $12$. What are the possible
   values of the number?
4. Twice a number, decreased by $4$, is at least $10$. What are the
   possible values of the number?
5. Which inequality represents the statement: a number $x$ is more
   than $8$?

## Set 6 — Spot the Error (the reverse-order trap, every time)

Each item is mistranslated using the Concept 2 trap. Name the error and
give the correct translation (and solution, where one is asked for).

1. Which expression represents "$9$ less than a number $x$"? — *shown
   as* $9-x$.
2. "Fifteen less than a number $n$ equals $20$. What is the value
   of $n$?" — *shown as* $15-n=20$.
3. Which expression represents "$6$ subtracted from a number $y$"? —
   *shown as* $6-y$.
4. Which inequality represents "a number $x$ is less than $10$"? —
   *shown as* $10-x$.
5. "Twice the difference of a number and $3$ is $10$. What is the
   number?" — *shown as* $2n-3=10$.

## Set 7 — Bonus / Challenge (timed, multi-step)

1. The sum of three consecutive even integers is $90$. What is the
   **largest** of the three integers?
2. A rope is cut into two pieces. One piece is $5$ meters longer than
   twice the other. The total length is $35$ meters. What is the
   length, in meters, of the **shorter** piece?
3. John has \$15 more than twice as much money as Maria. Together they
   have \$120. How much money does **John** have?
4. A number increased by $30\%$ of itself, then decreased by $5$,
   equals $47$. What is the original number?
5. Twice the sum of a number and $7$ is $10$ less than five times the
   number. What is the number?

## Set 8 — Official SAT-Style Application

Multiple-choice (A–D), matching the real Digital SAT's format and this
lesson's two official skill categories (*Linear equations in one variable*
and *Linear inequalities in one or two variables*), with a bias toward the
word-problem translation/modeling items specifically — this lesson's own
focus, not bare equation-solving. Every item is original, written by
studying the method of real, officially-released SAT Suite Question Bank
items, then building a new problem with new numbers/names/context. 15
Easy / 15 Medium / 15 Hard. No real College Board question is reproduced
— see the sourcing note at the end of this set.

### Easy

1. Bananas cost \$0.60 per pound. How many dollars will $b$ pounds of bananas cost?
   A) $0.60b$  B) $0.60+b$  C) $0.60/b$  D) $b/0.60$

2. If a number $x$ increased by $11$ is $29$, what is $x$?
   A) 7  B) 18  C) 29  D) 40

3. A water tank already holds 20 gallons. A hose adds 5 gallons per minute for $m$ minutes. Which equation gives the total gallons $g$ after $m$ minutes?
   A) $g=20+5m$  B) $g=5+20m$  C) $g=20m+5$  D) $g=25m$

4. Seven less than a number $y$ is $24$. What is $y$?
   A) 17  B) 24  C) 31  D) 38

5. Twice a number $n$ is $48$. What is $n$?
   A) 12  B) 24  C) 46  D) 96

6. Which expression represents $9$ more than a number $k$?
   A) $k+9$  B) $9-k$  C) $9k$  D) $k-9$

7. A number $x$ decreased by $15$ is $6$. What is $x$?
   A) 6  B) 9  C) 21  D) 90

8. Which inequality represents: a number $p$ is no more than $40$?
   A) $p\leq40$  B) $p\geq40$  C) $p<40$  D) $p>40$

9. A taxi charges a \$4 flat fee plus \$2 per mile. Which expression gives the total fare for driving $m$ miles?
   A) $4+2m$  B) $2+4m$  C) $4m+2$  D) $6m$

10. The sum of a number and $14$ is $30$. What is the number?
    A) 14  B) 16  C) 30  D) 44

11. A number $m$, decreased by $9$, is $13$. What is $m$?
    A) 4  B) 13  C) 22  D) 31

12. Which expression represents the quotient of a number $z$ and $6$?
    A) $z/6$  B) $6/z$  C) $6z$  D) $z-6$

13. Three times a number $w$ is $51$. What is $w$?
    A) 17  B) 48  C) 54  D) 153

14. A gym membership costs \$25 to join and \$15 per month. Which expression gives the total cost after $t$ months?
    A) $25+15t$  B) $15+25t$  C) $40t$  D) $25t+15$

15. Twelve less than a number $x$ is $5$. What is $x$?
    A) $-7$  B) 7  C) 17  D) 60

### Medium

1. Five more than twice a number $x$ is $23$. What is $x$?
   A) 4  B) 9  C) 14  D) 18

2. Nine less than four times a number $n$ is $23$. What is $n$?
   A) 3.5  B) 8  C) 14  D) 32

3. A model estimates that a tortoise travels between 48 and 55 meters per hour. Which inequality represents the estimated total number of meters, $x$, the tortoise could travel in 6 hours?
   A) $48(6)\leq x\leq55(6)$  B) $48+6\leq x\leq55+6$  C) $48\leq6+x\leq55$  D) $48\leq6x\leq55$

4. A triangle has three sides. The second side is $3$ cm longer than the first side, and the third side is twice the length of the first side. The perimeter is $35$ cm. What is the length, in cm, of the **longest** side?
   A) 8  B) 11  C) 16  D) 19

5. A parking garage charges \$6 for the first hour and \$3 for each additional hour. Which equation gives the total charge $c$ for $h$ additional hours after the first?
   A) $c=6+3h$  B) $c=3+6h$  C) $c=9h$  D) $c=6h+3$

6. Fourteen more than three times a number is equal to twice the number, increased by $20$. What is the number?
   A) 3  B) 6  C) 9  D) 14

7. Three more than twice a number $x$ is at most $19$. What are the possible values of $x$?
   A) $x\leq8$  B) $x\leq11$  C) $x\leq38$  D) $x\geq8$

8. A plumber charges a \$45 service fee plus \$35 per hour. If the total charge was \$185, which equation can be used to find the number of hours $h$ the plumber worked?
   A) $45+35h=185$  B) $35+45h=185$  C) $80h=185$  D) $45h+35=185$

9. The sum of two consecutive odd integers is $56$. What is the larger integer?
   A) 27  B) 28  C) 29  D) 31

10. A number increased by $25\%$ of itself is $60$. What is the original number?
    A) 15  B) 45  C) 48  D) 75

11. Rashid is $5$ years younger than three times his cousin's age. The sum of their ages is $35$. What is his cousin's age?
    A) 5  B) 10  C) 25  D) 30

12. A rectangle's length is $3$ less than twice its width. The perimeter is $54$. What is the length of the rectangle?
    A) 10  B) 13  C) 17  D) 20

13. A club needs to raise at least \$540 for a trip. It already has \$120 and plans to sell raffle tickets for \$15 each. What is the least number of raffle tickets the club must sell?
   A) 24  B) 28  C) 36  D) 420

14. A bakery packs cookies and brownies into boxes, with no more than $10$ items per box. If $c$ is the number of cookies and $b$ is the number of brownies in a box, which inequality represents this situation?
    A) $c+b\leq10$  B) $c+b\geq10$  C) $c-b\leq10$  D) $c-b\geq10$

15. Eight less than five times a number is the same as three times the number, increased by $10$. What is the number?
    A) 1  B) 3  C) 9  D) 18

### Hard

1. Print shop A charges a \$30 setup fee plus \$18 per poster. Print shop B charges \$24 per poster, but takes \$12 off the total order. For how many posters $p$ do the two shops charge the same total? Which equation can be used to find $p$?
   A) $30+18p=24p-12$  B) $30+18p=24p+12$  C) $30+24p=18p-12$  D) $30p+18=24p-12$

2. In the equation $x(r-7)+5=15x+32$, $r$ is a positive integer. If the equation has exactly one solution, what CANNOT be the value of $r$?
   A) 15  B) 20  C) 22  D) 30

3. If $\dfrac{x-5}{9}=\dfrac{x-5}{14}$, the value of $x-5$ is between which of the following pairs of values?
   A) $-10$ and $-6$  B) $-4$ and $4$  C) $6$ and $10$  D) $12$ and $15$

4. If $\dfrac{x+2}{3}-\dfrac{x+2}{5}=4$, what is the value of $x+2$?
   A) 12  B) 28  C) 30  D) 60

5. A book club has \$80 to spend on books. Shipping costs \$7.50 per order plus \$4.25 for each book. What is the greatest number of books the club can order without exceeding its \$80 budget?
   A) 16  B) 17  C) 18  D) 19

6. A museum keeps its number of weekly visitors $v$ within two limits. Four times $v$, decreased by $150$, is at most $1000$. Also, $v$ decreased by $40$ is at least $2v$ decreased by $330$. What is the greatest whole number of visitors that satisfies both conditions?
   A) 240  B) 287  C) 288  D) 290

7. Laylo is $7$ years older than three times her nephew's age. In $4$ years, the sum of their ages will be $59$. How old is Laylo right now?
   A) 11  B) 33  C) 40  D) 44

8. A warehouse stores boxes of three sizes. The number of large boxes is $7$ times the number $m$ of medium boxes, and the number of small boxes is $34$. The warehouse stores $250$ boxes total. Which equation represents this situation?
   A) $34(7m)+m+34=250$  B) $7m+7(34)=250$  C) $m+7m+34m=250$  D) $8m+34=250$

9. A number is increased by $40\%$ of itself, then decreased by $8$, to get $97$. What is the original number?
   A) 65  B) 69  C) 75  D) 89

10. The sum of four consecutive integers is $10$ more than three times the smallest integer. What is the largest of the four integers?
    A) 4  B) 5  C) 6  D) 7

11. A board is cut into two pieces. One piece is $9$ cm longer than three times the other. The total length is $73$ cm. What is the length, in cm, of the longer piece?
    A) 16  B) 48  C) 57  D) 64

12. In the equation $2(kx-5)=\dfrac{7}{3}x-12$, $k$ is a constant. If the equation has no solution, what is the value of $k$?
    A) $\dfrac{7}{6}$  B) $-\dfrac{7}{6}$  C) $-6$  D) $-5$

13. A number $x$ satisfies $3x-8=2x+19$. What is the value of $x+15$?
    A) 19  B) 27  C) 35  D) 42

14. Zarina has \$12 more than twice as much money as Umida. Together they have \$168. How much money does Zarina have?
    A) 52  B) 104  C) 116  D) 156

15. A number decreased by $9$ is more than $4$, but the same number increased by $3$ is at most $22$. What is the largest possible integer value of the number?
    A) 13  B) 14  C) 18  D) 19

   parentheses before doubling; only the $n$ was doubled, not the whole
