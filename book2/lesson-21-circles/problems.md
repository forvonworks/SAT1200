## Set 4 — Reading Center & Radius from Standard Form

## Set 6 — Spot the Error

Har bir item aynan bitta xato qadamni ko'rsatadi. Xatoni nomlang, qaysi
qoidani buzganini aytng, va to'g'ri javobni yozing.

1. Masala: radiusi $9$ sm, markaziy burchak $40°$ bo'lgan yoyning
   uzunligini toping. *Noto'g'ri yechim:* "Yoy uzunligi $=40$ sm."
2. Masala: radiusi $10$ sm, markaziy burchak $90°$ bo'lgan sektorning
   yuzini toping. *Noto'g'ri yechim:* "Sektor yuzi
   $=\dfrac{90}{360}\times2\pi\times10=5\pi$ sm²."
3. Masala: $x^2+y^2-10x+4y-7=0$ tenglamasining markazi va radiusini
   toping. *Noto'g'ri yechim:* "$(x^2-10x)+(y^2+4y)=-7$, so'ng
   $(x-5)^2+(y+2)^2=-7+25+4=22$."
4. Masala: $(x+6)^2+(y-3)^2=49$ tenglamasining markazini toping.
   *Noto'g'ri yechim:* "Markaz $(6,3)$."
5. Masala: radiusi $16$ sm bo'lgan doirada bir yoyning uzunligi $8\pi$ sm.
   Markaziy burchakni toping. *Noto'g'ri yechim:*
   "$8\pi=\dfrac{\theta}{360}\times\pi\times16^2$, bundan $\theta$ ni
   topamiz."

## Set 7 — Bonus / Challenge

1. Bir sektorning yuzi $24\pi$ sm², markaziy burchagi $60°$. Doiraning
   radiusini toping.
2. Bir yoyning uzunligi $10\pi$ sm, markaziy burchagi $100°$. Avval
   radiusni, so'ngra xuddi shu burchak uchun sektor yuzini toping.
3. $x^2+y^2-14x-8y+40=0$ tenglamasi berilgan. Markaz va radiusni toping,
   so'ngra $(7,4)$ nuqtasi doira ichida, tashqarisida, yoki aylananing
   ustida ekanligini aniqlang.
4. Bir sektorning yuzi to'liq doira yuzining aynan $\dfrac{1}{8}$ qismini
   tashkil qiladi. Markaziy burchakni toping. Agar radius $16$ sm bo'lsa,
   shu sektorning yoy uzunligini toping.
5. $x^2+y^2+4x-10y-20=0$ tenglamasi berilgan. Markaz va radiusni toping.
   So'ngra, markaziy burchagi $72°$ bo'lgan sektorning yoy uzunligi va
   yuzini toping.

**Set 6:**
1. Xato — yoy o'lchovini (daraja) yoy uzunligi (masofa) deb yozgan; bu
   ikki xil kattalik, formuladan foydalanmagan. To'g'ri:
   $\dfrac{40}{360}\times2\pi\times9=2\pi$ sm.
2. Xato — yuza formulasi $\pi r^2$ o'rniga uzunlik formulasi $2\pi r$
   qo'llanilgan. To'g'ri: $\dfrac{90}{360}\times\pi\times10^2=25\pi$ sm².
3. Xato — erkin hadni o'ng tomonga ko'chirishda ishorasi o'zgartirilmagan
   ($-7$ qoldirilgan, $+7$ bo'lishi kerak edi). To'g'ri:
   $(x^2-10x)+(y^2+4y)=7$, $(x-5)^2+(y+2)^2=7+25+4=36$; markaz $(5,-2)$,
   $r=6$.
4. Xato — $(x+6)^2$ ni $(x-6)^2$ kabi o'qib, markazning $x$ koordinatasi
   ishorasini teskari olgan. To'g'ri: $(x+6)^2=(x-(-6))^2$, markaz
   $(-6,3)$.
5. Xato — yoy uzunligi formulasi $2\pi r$ o'rniga sektor yuzi formulasi
   $\pi r^2$ qo'llanilgan. To'g'ri: $8\pi=\dfrac{\theta}{360}\times2\pi\times16$,
   bundan $\theta=90°$.

**Set 7:**
1. $r=12$ sm
2. $r=18$ sm; sektor yuzi $=90\pi$ sm²
3. Markaz $(7,4)$, $r=5$; $(7,4)$ nuqtasi aynan markazning o'zi, demak
   doira **ichida** (markazgacha masofa $0<5$).
4. $\theta=45°$; yoy uzunligi $=4\pi$ sm (radius $16$ sm bilan)
5. Markaz $(-2,5)$, $r=7$; yoy uzunligi $=\dfrac{14\pi}{5}$ sm, sektor yuzi
   $=\dfrac{49\pi}{5}$ sm²
