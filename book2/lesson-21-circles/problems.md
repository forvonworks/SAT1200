## Set 1 — Arc Length & Arc Measure

1. A circle has a radius of $9$ cm and a central angle of $40°$. What is the length of the arc intercepted by the central angle?
2. A circle has a radius of $15$ cm and a central angle of $72°$. What is the length of the intercepted arc?
3. A circle has a radius of $8$ cm and a central angle of $270°$. What is the length of the intercepted arc?
4. A circle has a radius of $6$ cm. An arc of the circle has a length of $4\pi$ cm. What is the measure, in degrees, of the central angle that intercepts this arc?
5. A circle has a radius of $10$ cm. An arc of the circle has a length of $5\pi$ cm. What is the measure of the central angle?
6. An arc of a circle has a central angle of $50°$. What is the degree measure of this arc? What additional information would be needed to find the length of the arc?

## Set 3 — Mixed: Arc Length + Sector Area (Same Circle)

Each pair of problems refers to the same circle and the same central angle — one question asks for the arc length, the other for the sector area. Don't mix up the two.

1. A circle has a radius of $6$ cm and a central angle of $90°$. What is the length of the intercepted arc?
2. Using the same circle and the same central angle, what is the area of the sector?
3. A circle has a radius of $9$ cm and a central angle of $200°$. What is the length of the intercepted arc?
4. Using the same circle and the same central angle, what is the area of the sector?
5. A sprinkler with a radius of $18$ feet waters a section of a field that forms a central angle of $50°$. What is the length of the arc traced by the tip of the sprinkler?
6. What is the area of the sector of the field watered by the same sprinkler?

## Set 5 — Circle Equation via Completing the Square

1. The equation $x^2+y^2-4x-6y-12=0$ represents a circle in the xy-plane. Written in the form $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$, what are the center and radius of the circle?
2. The equation $x^2+y^2+10x-4y+13=0$ represents a circle in the xy-plane. Written in the form $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$, what are the center and radius of the circle?
3. The equation $x^2+y^2-2x+8y+1=0$ represents a circle in the xy-plane. Written in the form $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$, what are the center and radius of the circle?
4. The equation $x^2+y^2+6x+6y+9=0$ represents a circle in the xy-plane. Written in the form $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$, what are the center and radius of the circle?
5. The equation $x^2+y^2-8x+2y-8=0$ represents a circle in the xy-plane. Written in the form $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$, what are the center and radius of the circle?
6. The equation $x^2+y^2+2x-12y+12=0$ represents a circle in the xy-plane. Written in the form $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$, what are the center and radius of the circle?

## Set 6 — Spot the Error

Each item below contains exactly one flawed step. Identify the error, state which rule was broken, and give the correct answer.

1. Problem: A circle has a radius of $9$ cm and a central angle of $40°$. What is the length of the intercepted arc? *Incorrect solution:* "The arc length is $40$ cm."
2. Problem: A circle has a radius of $10$ cm and a central angle of $90°$. What is the area of the sector? *Incorrect solution:* "The sector area is $\dfrac{90}{360}\times2\pi\times10=5\pi$ cm²."
3. Problem: What are the center and radius of the circle represented by the equation $x^2+y^2-10x+4y-7=0$? *Incorrect solution:* "$(x^2-10x)+(y^2+4y)=-7$, so $(x-5)^2+(y+2)^2=-7+25+4=22$."
4. Problem: What is the center of the circle represented by the equation $(x+6)^2+(y-3)^2=49$? *Incorrect solution:* "The center is $(6,3)$."
5. Problem: A circle has a radius of $16$ cm. An arc of the circle has a length of $8\pi$ cm. What is the measure of the central angle? *Incorrect solution:* "$8\pi=\dfrac{\theta}{360}\times\pi\times16^2$, which gives $\theta$."

## Set 7 — Bonus / Challenge

1. A sector of a circle has an area of $24\pi$ cm² and a central angle of $60°$. What is the radius of the circle?
2. An arc of a circle has a length of $10\pi$ cm and a central angle of $100°$. What is the radius of the circle? Then, what is the area of the sector for the same central angle?
3. A circle is represented by the equation $x^2+y^2-14x-8y+40=0$. What are the center and radius of the circle? Then determine whether the point $(7,4)$ lies inside the circle, outside the circle, or on the circle.
4. The area of a sector is exactly $\dfrac{1}{8}$ of the area of the full circle. What is the measure of the central angle? If the radius of the circle is $16$ cm, what is the length of the arc of this sector?
5. A circle is represented by the equation $x^2+y^2+4x-10y-20=0$. What are the center and radius of the circle? Then, for a central angle of $72°$, what are the arc length and the sector area?

## Answer Key

**Set 1:** 1) $2\pi$ cm  2) $6\pi$ cm  3) $12\pi$ cm  4) $120°$  5) $90°$
6) The arc measure is $50°$; the **radius** is needed to find the arc length.

**Set 2:** 1) $6\pi$ cm²  2) $60\pi$ cm²  3) $9\pi$ cm²  4) $20\pi$ cm²
5) $54°$  6) $30°$

**Set 3:** 1) $3\pi$ ft  2) $9\pi$ cm²  3) $10\pi$ cm  4) $45\pi$ cm²
5) $5\pi$ ft  6) $45\pi$ ft²

**Set 4:** 1) center $(2,5)$, $r=7$  2) center $(-3,1)$, $r=4$
3) center $(7,-6)$, $r=9$  4) center $(0,4)$, $r=5$
5) center $(-1,-1)$, $r=\sqrt2$  6) center $(5,0)$, $r=5$

**Set 5:** 1) center $(2,3)$, $r=5$  2) center $(-5,2)$, $r=4$
3) center $(1,-4)$, $r=4$  4) center $(-3,-3)$, $r=3$
5) center $(4,-1)$, $r=5$  6) center $(-1,6)$, $r=5$

**Set 6:**
1. Error — the arc measure (in degrees) was written as if it were the arc length (a distance); these are two different quantities, and the arc-length formula was never applied. Correct:
   $\dfrac{40}{360}\times2\pi\times9=2\pi$ cm.
2. Error — the arc-length formula $2\pi r$ was used instead of the area formula $\pi r^2$. Correct: $\dfrac{90}{360}\times\pi\times10^2=25\pi$ cm².
3. Error — the sign was not flipped when moving the constant term to the other side ($-7$ was kept instead of becoming $+7$). Correct:
   $(x^2-10x)+(y^2+4y)=7$, $(x-5)^2+(y+2)^2=7+25+4=36$; center $(5,-2)$,
   $r=6$.
4. Error — $(x+6)^2$ was read as $(x-6)^2$, flipping the sign of the center's $x$-coordinate. Correct: $(x+6)^2=(x-(-6))^2$, so the center is
   $(-6,3)$.
5. Error — the sector-area formula $\pi r^2$ was used instead of the arc-length formula $2\pi r$. Correct: $8\pi=\dfrac{\theta}{360}\times2\pi\times16$,
   which gives $\theta=90°$.

**Set 7:**
1. $r=12$ cm
2. $r=18$ cm; sector area $=90\pi$ cm²
3. Center $(7,4)$, $r=5$; the point $(7,4)$ is exactly the center itself, so it lies **inside** the circle (distance to center is $0<5$).
4. $\theta=45°$; arc length $=4\pi$ cm (with radius $16$ cm)
5. Center $(-2,5)$, $r=7$; arc length $=\dfrac{14\pi}{5}$ cm, sector area
   $=\dfrac{49\pi}{5}$ cm²
