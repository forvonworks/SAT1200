## Set 6 — Spot the Error

For each item, a student's work contains exactly one error. Identify the error, then give the correct answer.

1. A student is asked for the slope of the line through $(2,5)$ and $(6,13)$ and writes: *"Solution: $m=\frac{6-2}{13-5}=\frac{4}{8}=\frac12$."* What error did the student make, and what is the correct slope?
2. For the line $y=5x-3$, a student writes: *"The slope is $-3$ and the $y$-intercept is $5$."* What error did the student make, and what are the correct slope and $y$-intercept?
3. A student is asked for the slope of a line perpendicular to $y=2x+1$ and writes: *"The perpendicular line also has a slope of $2$, since perpendicular lines intersect."* What error did the student make, and what is the correct slope?
4. A student is asked for an equation of the line through $(3,4)$ with slope $-2$ and writes: *"$y-4=-2(x-3) \Rightarrow y-4=-2x-6 \Rightarrow y=-2x-2$."* What error did the student make, and what is the correct equation?
5. For the function $V(x)=-4x+40$, a student writes: *"The tank is full when $x=10$, since $V(10)=0$."* What error did the student make?

### Easy

1. The graph of linear function $f$ passes through the points $(0,-6)$ and $(3,0)$. Which equation defines $f$?
   A) $f(x)=2x-6$  B) $f(x)=-2x-6$  C) $f(x)=2x+6$  D) $f(x)=-2x+6$

2. If $f(x)=3x-2$, what is $f(7)$?
   A) 5  B) 12  C) 19  D) 26

3. The function $P(t)=400+15t$ models the number of trees planted in a park, where $P(t)$ is the number of trees $t$ years after 2010. What is the best interpretation of $P(20)=700$?
   A) 700 trees were planted in 2010.  B) 700 trees are predicted in 2020.  C) 700 trees are predicted in 2030.  D) 20 trees are predicted in 2030.

4. A line defined by $g(x)=mx+b$ has slope 5 and $y$-intercept $-3$. What is $g(2)$?
   A) 2  B) 7  C) 10  D) 13

5. For linear function $h$, $h(0)=9$ and $h(4)=21$. What is the slope of the graph of $y=h(x)$?
   A) 3  B) 4  C) 9  D) 12

6. A company's cost, in dollars, to produce $x$ items is modeled by $C(x)=18x+400$. What does 400 represent in this context?
   A) the cost per item  B) the fixed cost before producing any items  C) the total cost for 400 items  D) the number of items produced

7. The table shows three values of $x$ and their corresponding values of $y$ for a linear relationship: $(2,11)$, $(4,19)$, $(6,27)$. What is the slope of the line?
   A) 2  B) 4  C) 8  D) 11

8. If $f(x)=-4x+9$, for what value of $x$ does $f(x)=1$?
   A) $-2$  B) 1  C) 2  D) 4

9. The graph of $y=f(x)$ is a line with slope $-3$ passing through $(2,5)$. What is $f(0)$?
   A) $-1$  B) 5  C) 8  D) 11

10. A candle's height, in centimeters, after burning for $m$ minutes is given by $H(m)=20-0.05m$. What is the height after burning for 100 minutes?
    A) 5  B) 10  C) 15  D) 20

11. For linear function $k$, the graph of $y=k(x)$ has a $y$-intercept of 7 and passes through $(4,19)$. What is the slope?
    A) 3  B) 4  C) 7  D) 12

12. If $f(x)=2x+c$ and $f(5)=17$, what is the value of $c$?
    A) 2  B) 5  C) 7  D) 17

13. A line has slope 6 and passes through $(0,-4)$. Which equation defines this line?
    A) $y=6x-4$  B) $y=-6x+4$  C) $y=6x+4$  D) $y=4x-6$

14. The function $w$ gives a plant's weight, in grams, $x$ days after planting: $w(x)=5x+2$. What is $w(0)$?

    A) $-2$  B) 2  C) 4  D) 8
