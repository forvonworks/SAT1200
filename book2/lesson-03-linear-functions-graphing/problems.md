## Set 5 — Context (Interpreting Slope & Intercept)

1. The function $C(x)=2000x+5000$ gives the cost, in som, of a taxi ride of $x$ kilometers. What is the value of $C(10)$?
2. Using the function $C$ from the previous item, for what value of $x$ does $C(x)=35000$?
3. A tank is draining, and its volume, in liters, $x$ minutes after draining begins is given by $V(x)=-5x+60$. What is the value of $V(8)$?
4. Using the function $V$ from the previous item, for what value of $x$ is the tank empty, that is, $V(x)=0$?
5. In the xy-plane, a line has a slope of $4$ and a $y$-intercept of $-7$. What is the $y$-value of the point on the line where $x=3$?
6. A plant's height, in centimeters, is a linear function of time, in weeks, whose graph passes through the points $(2,10)$ and $(5,19)$. What was the plant's height at week $0$?

## Set 6 — Spot the Error

For each item, a student's work contains exactly one error. Identify the error, then give the correct answer.

1. A student is asked for the slope of the line through $(2,5)$ and $(6,13)$ and writes: *"Solution: $m=\frac{6-2}{13-5}=\frac{4}{8}=\frac12$."* What error did the student make, and what is the correct slope?
2. For the line $y=5x-3$, a student writes: *"The slope is $-3$ and the $y$-intercept is $5$."* What error did the student make, and what are the correct slope and $y$-intercept?
3. A student is asked for the slope of a line perpendicular to $y=2x+1$ and writes: *"The perpendicular line also has a slope of $2$, since perpendicular lines intersect."* What error did the student make, and what is the correct slope?
4. A student is asked for an equation of the line through $(3,4)$ with slope $-2$ and writes: *"$y-4=-2(x-3) \Rightarrow y-4=-2x-6 \Rightarrow y=-2x-2$."* What error did the student make, and what is the correct equation?
5. For the function $V(x)=-4x+40$, a student writes: *"The tank is full when $x=10$, since $V(10)=0$."* What error did the student make?

## Set 7 — Bonus / Challenge

1. In the xy-plane, line $k$ passes through the point $(1,2)$ and is parallel to the line that passes through $(0,0)$ and $(3,9)$. What is an equation of line $k$?
2. A line in the xy-plane passes through the point $(-2,5)$ and is perpendicular to a line with slope $\frac23$. What is an equation of the line?
3. In the xy-plane, the line $y=kx+4$ passes through the point $(3,13)$. What is the value of $k$?
4. In the xy-plane, the equations $y=mx+5$ and $y=3x+b$ represent the same line, which passes through the point $(2,11)$. What are the values of $m$ and $b$?
5. A line in the xy-plane is parallel to the line $y=-2x+1$ and has the same $y$-intercept as the line $y=5x-7$. What is an equation of the line?

## Answer Key

**Set 1:** 1) $3$  2) $-3$  3) $0$  4) $2$  5) $3$  6) $-2$

**Set 2:** 1) $\frac34$  2) $-\frac32$  3) $-1$  4) $\frac54$  5) $-\frac23$  6) $2$

**Set 3:** 1) $y=4x-1$  2) $y=-2x+5$  3) $y=3x-5$  4) $y=-2x+2$  5) $y=\frac12x-3$  6) $y=x-3$

**Set 4:** 1) $y=3x+5$  2) $y=-4x+5$  3) $y=-\frac12x+3$  4) $y=3x+6$  5) $y=-2$  6) $y=7$

**Set 5:** 1) $25000$  2) $15$  3) $20$  4) $12$  5) $5$  6) $4$

**Set 6:**
1. Error — inverted the formula ($\Delta x/\Delta y$ instead of
   $\Delta y/\Delta x$). Correct: $m=2$.
2. Error — swapped slope and $y$-intercept. Correct: slope $=5$, $y$-intercept $=-3$.
3. Error — used the same slope instead of the negative reciprocal. Correct:
   slope $=-\frac12$.
4. Error — sign mistake distributing $-2(x-3)$: it's $-2x+6$, not $-2x-6$.
   Correct: $y=-2x+10$.
5. Error — $V(x)=0$ means the tank is **empty**, not full; the sign/context
   meaning was misread, not a computation error.

**Set 7:** 1) $y=3x-1$  2) $y=-\frac32x+2$  3) $k=3$  4) $m=3$, $b=5$  5) $y=-2x-7$
