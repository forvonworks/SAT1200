## Page 1: Concepts

**Factor - Bo'luvchi:** agar a soni b soniga qoldiqsiz bo'linsa, b soni a ning bo'luvchisi bo'ladi.
*Factor: a number that divides another number with no remainder.*
ex: 12 ning bo'luvchilari: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12

**Multiple - Karrali:** sonni 1, 2, 3, 4, ... ga ko'paytirganda hosil bo'lgan sonlar. Karralilar cheksiz ko'p.
*Multiple: the number you get when you multiply a number by 1, 2, 3, ...*
ex: 5 ning karralilari: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, ...

**Prime Number - Tub son:** faqat ikkita bo'luvchisi bor son: 1 va o'zi. 2 yagona juft tub son.
*Prime number: a whole number greater than 1 whose only factors are 1 and itself.*
ex: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19

**Composite Number - Murakkab son:** ikkitadan ko'p bo'luvchisi bor son. 1 soni na tub, na murakkab.
*Composite number: a whole number greater than 1 that has more than two factors.*
ex: 4, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15

**Divisibility Rules - Bo'linish belgilari:**
- 2 ga: oxirgi raqami juft (0, 2, 4, 6, 8)
- 5 ga: oxirgi raqami 0 yoki 5
- 10 ga: oxirgi raqami 0
- 3 ga: raqamlari yig'indisi 3 ga bo'linadi
- 9 ga: raqamlari yig'indisi 9 ga bo'linadi

*Divisible by: can be divided by a number with no remainder.*
ex: 522 → 5 + 2 + 2 = 9 → 522 soni 2 ga, 3 ga va 9 ga bo'linadi.

**Prime Factorization - Tub ko'paytuvchilarga ajratish:** sonni faqat tub sonlarning ko'paytmasi shaklida yozish. Sonni eng kichik tub sonlarga (2, 3, 5, ...) ketma-ket bo'lib boring.
*Prime factorization: writing a number as a product of prime numbers.*
ex: 60 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 5 = 2² × 3 × 5

## Page 2: Concepts (continued), SAT phrases, Vocabulary

**Greatest Common Factor (GCF) - EKUB:** berilgan sonlarning har birini qoldiqsiz bo'ladigan eng katta son.
*GCF: the largest number that divides all the given numbers.*
ex: 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12;  18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18  →  EKUB(12, 18) = 6

**Least Common Multiple (LCM) - EKUK:** berilgan sonlarning har biriga qoldiqsiz bo'linadigan eng kichik son.
*LCM: the smallest number that is a multiple of all the given numbers.*
ex: 4: 4, 8, 12, 16, ...;  6: 6, 12, 18, ...  →  EKUK(4, 6) = 12

**Remainder - Qoldiq:** bo'lishdan keyin ortib qolgan son. Qoldiq har doim bo'luvchidan kichik.
*Remainder: the amount left over after dividing.*
ex: 17 ÷ 5 = 3, qoldiq 2   (5 × 3 + 2 = 17)
