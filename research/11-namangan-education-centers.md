# Namangan Region — Education/Tutoring Center Sales Leads (District-by-District)

Compiled for business development around a SAT Math prep book series. Scope: every district (tuman) of Namangan viloyat, not just Namangan city — Namangan city, Chortoq (Chartak/"Charkat"), Yangiqo'rg'on, Uychi, Chust, Kosonsoy, Mingbuloq, Namangan tumani (the rural district surrounding the city), Norin, Pop, To'raqo'rg'on, and Uchqo'rg'on.

**Methodology:** web search in Uzbek (Latin and Cyrillic), Russian and English, covering business directories (Golden Pages UZ, Yellow Pages UZ, top.uz, prom.uz), the British Council's own IELTS partner/registration list, OLX classifieds, government (gov.uz) district-hokimiyat news (which cover small, newly-opened centers funded under the "Ko'mak" youth-entrepreneurship loan program — exactly the "small, recently-started" profile this list is after), and local press (uza.uz, kun.uz, talimxabarlari.uz). **Direct fetches of `.uz` domains failed in this session (DNS/network policy blocked goldenpages.uz, yellowpages.uz, britishcouncil.uz, prom.uz directly)**, so every entry below is reconstructed from search-engine result snippets/cached text, not live pages — treat every phone number and address as a starting point to verify by phone/social media before outreach, not as confirmed-current.

**Per the brief, the two large known chains (SATashkent, Everest Learning Center) are deliberately excluded** from this list even where they turned up in Namangan-region searches, since they are out-of-scope established competitors covered elsewhere. Everything below is a smaller/independent/local center, or a plausible institutional buyer, regardless of whether SAT is explicitly advertised.

**Total: 14 leads found**, concentrated almost entirely in Namangan city, plus one strong lead in Chortoq district and a handful in the rural district(s) immediately around the city. **7 of the 12 target districts (Yangiqo'rg'on, Uychi, Chust, Kosonsoy, Mingbuloq, Norin, Uchqo'rg'on) returned no identifiable center at all** — see Research Limitations.

## Research Limitations

- **Network/access constraint:** this session could not directly load any `.uz` website (DNS/policy block on goldenpages.uz, yellowpages.uz, britishcouncil.uz, prom.uz, olx.uz, and gov.uz subdomains all failed on direct fetch). Every entry above comes from search-engine snippets and cached directory text, not a live page read. **Every phone number, address and claimed service should be re-verified by phone or via the center's own Telegram/Instagram before outreach** — several numbers that surfaced are identical across multiple unrelated directory listings (a likely Golden Pages template/placeholder artifact, flagged in each affected row).
- **Districts that returned essentially nothing, despite searches in Uzbek Latin, Uzbek Cyrillic, Russian, and English, combined with "ta'lim markazi," "o'quv markazi," "til markazi," "repetitor," "fan markazi," "SAT," and "IELTS":**
  - **Yangiqo'rg'on tumani** — no center found. The only loosely relevant hit was a preschool-renovation news item naming the district hokim, unrelated to tutoring.
  - **Uychi tumani** — no center found. The only nearby hits were a vocational technicum in neighboring Namangan tumani and the (unrelated-district) state foreign-languages institute.
  - **Chust tumani** — no center found, despite being the region's second-largest town by population. This is a genuine gap, not just a search-term issue — multiple phrasing variants all returned zero local results.
  - **Kosonsoy (Kasansay) tumani** — no center found, beyond a passing mention that the town participated in a 2022 regional "digital literacy" school course (not a private center).
  - **Mingbuloq tumani** — no center found. Only a gas-utility branch and the district statistics office appeared in directories.
  - **Norin tumani** — no center found. Only hokimiyat administrative pages and an unrelated town-services-center news item appeared.
  - **Uchqo'rg'on tumani** — no *named* center found, though a Golden Pages city listing claims 34 organizations are registered in Uchqo'rg'on city; this session could not open that page directly to see which (if any) are education-related. **This is the single best next step to run again with working `.uz` access** — treat the district as "likely has something, not yet identified" rather than confirmed-empty.
  - **Pop tumani** — the only hit was a 2022 IT-skills training center opened in the Uyg'ursoy settlement; it is government/IT-focused, the source is 4 years old, and no name or contact info could be found, so it was not included as a row above.
- **"Namangan tumani" vs. "Yangi Namangan tumani" ambiguity:** Uzbekistan's district boundaries around Namangan city have shifted in recent years (a 2022 Senate item discusses moving ~472 hectares between To'raqo'rg'on district and the city's Davlatobod district for a "Yangi Namangan shaharchasi" project), and gov.uz now runs a distinct "Yangi Namangan tumani" hokimiyat news feed. It was not possible in this session to confirm definitively whether "Yangi Namangan tumani" **is** the renamed/reorganized "Namangan tumani" the brief asked for, or a separate newly-split unit. The three leads attributed to "Yangi Namangan district" above should be treated as provisionally matching "Namangan tumani (rural)" pending on-the-ground confirmation.
- **Namangan city itself is heavily over-represented relative to every rural district.** This likely reflects genuine reality (most private tutoring/language businesses cluster in the regional capital) more than a search-methodology gap, but it means the "broad net" goal of this research is only lightly fulfilled for the rural districts — see above.
- **Small, informal tutors are almost certainly invisible to this methodology.** As with the prior nationwide research (see `10-uzbekistan-sat-centers-leads.md`), the great majority of actual small-town tutoring in Uzbekistan likely happens through individual teachers, closed Telegram/WhatsApp groups, and word-of-mouth that never gets indexed by Google. For the 7 districts with zero hits, this is the most probable explanation, not that these districts have literally no tutoring activity at all. On-the-ground inquiry (calling district school directors, visiting district bazaars/town centers, asking the "Ko'mak" youth-entrepreneurship loan program office which centers it has funded locally) would likely surface real leads that search cannot.
- **Golden Pages' "Preparation for the SAT" and "SAT" category tags should not be over-trusted.** Several Namangan city listings (Learning Skills Lab, Oxford School) carry an explicit SAT tag, but given how often this directory reuses identical phone-number blocks and boilerplate across unrelated listings, it's plausible the SAT category tag itself is applied generically to a cluster of listings rather than reflecting a real, taught SAT curriculum at each one. Treat the SAT tag as a soft signal worth asking about directly, not confirmation.
