# Andijan Region (Viloyat) — Small/Independent Education & Tutoring Centers Lead List

**Purpose:** Sales leads for a SAT Math prep book series. Target = small, recently-started, or otherwise-independent education/tutoring/language centers across Andijan region — NOT the large established chains already known as competitors (SATashkent, Everest Learning Center, etc., which are out of scope). The goal is breadth: any center with at least a little local footprint (operating history, a directory/press listing, social presence, or named branch) is included as a lead worth an outreach call, whether or not SAT is explicitly advertised.

**Method:** Web search (Uzbek, Russian, English) combining each district/town name with "ta'lim markazi," "o'quv markazi," "til markazi," "repetitor," "SAT," "IELTS," "fan markazi," plus directory sites (Golden Pages UZ), OLX, Telegram/Instagram mentions, and local news. Golden Pages UZ (goldenpages.uz) could not be fetched directly in this session (network policy blocks the domain), so all Golden Pages data below is as reported in search-engine result snippets/summaries, not independently re-verified against the live page — phone numbers and addresses should be confirmed by phone before outreach.

**Important caveat found during research:** Several directory ("o'quv markazi") listings that appear to sit in individual districts (Asaka, Buloqboshi x2, Xo'jaobod, plus comparable ones found for Andijan city and for many Tashkent districts) share **identical boilerplate text and identical phone numbers** ("milliy potok"/national-stream line (+998 97) 744-91-70 / 748-55-56, "yevropa potok"/European-stream line (+998 90) 318-27-19 / (+998 95) 170-07-06) despite having completely different brand names (LTC Group, Cosmos Academia, Venus Edu, Mastermind Language Centre, My School, Target Education, Robbit, Maydan Ta'lim, Yunior Kod, Umid Gullari, etc.). This strongly suggests a mass-registered national directory/franchise listing (likely tied to a national university-entrance-exam-prep enrollment hotline) rather than independently verified, distinct local businesses. These are flagged below as **low-confidence / unverified** — still worth a courtesy call since a real physical branch may exist at the stated location, but treat the "reputation" signal as weak until confirmed by phone.

## Research Limitations

- **Districts/towns that returned essentially nothing** (no distinct private education/tutoring center found, templated or otherwise): **Andijan tumani (rural district)**, **Bo'z**, **Izboskan**, **Jalaquduq**, **Qo'rg'ontepa**, **Marhamat**, **Paxtaobod**, **Shahrixon**, **Ulug'nor**. Searches for these returned only district-government (hokimiyat/gov.uz) pages, public-services-center listings, or unrelated news. No Instagram/Telegram handles, OLX ads, or press mentions of private tutoring/language centers were found for any of them in this session.
- **Districts with only weak/low-confidence leads:** **Asaka**, **Buloqboshi**, **Xo'jaobod** — each returned exactly one Golden Pages "o'quv markazi" listing, but all three (plus a second Buloqboshi listing) use identical template text and largely the same enrollment phone numbers as listings found for many unrelated districts and Tashkent brand names, so they read as a mass-registered national directory entry rather than a confirmed, independently-reputable local business. Still worth a phone call to see if a real branch answers.
- **Districts with only state-run (non-sales-fit) centers:** **Oltinko'l**, **Baliqchi**, **Xonobod** — only government IT/digital-skills centers surfaced; these are free, state-run programs and a poor fit for a commercial book pitch, though the Xonobod location does explicitly plan English instruction and could be worth a lower-priority inquiry.
- **Andijan city** had by far the best coverage — it is the only part of the region with multiple distinct, named, directly-contactable private centers (Proper English School, Confidence Education Center, Registon Learning Center, Andijan Development Center, Korean School, Language City Education, Edu Action/Edu-Action CD test centers).
- **Golden Pages (goldenpages.uz) could not be fetched directly** in this session — the outbound network proxy returned a 403 on every connection attempt to that domain. All Golden Pages details above come from web-search snippets/AI summaries of that site's pages, not a direct page read, so addresses, hours, and phone numbers should be independently re-confirmed (e.g., by phone) before being relied on for outreach.
- **No OLX.uz classified ads, Instagram accounts, or Telegram channels specific to Andijan region tutoring were found** despite multiple targeted queries; OLX results consistently redirected to Tashkent, Samarkand, Bukhara, or Fergana listings instead. This likely reflects genuinely thin indexed online presence for small-town Andijan tutoring businesses rather than their total absence — follow-up via direct OLX region filtering, 2GIS/Google Maps browsing, or in-person/phone canvassing of district hokimiyats would likely surface more than a web search can.
- Overall: **confirmed, individually-distinct leads = roughly 8** (concentrated in Andijan city), plus **4 low-confidence templated directory leads** (Asaka, Buloqboshi x2, Xo'jaobod) and **3 state-run centers** of marginal fit. Coverage of the rural/small-town districts is genuinely thin — this reflects the limits of what's indexed online for these towns, not a lack of search effort.
