# Fergana Region (Viloyat) — Small/Independent Education Centers — Sales Lead List

Compiled for business development around a SAT Math prep book series, covering every district and town of Fergana region, not just Fergana city: Fergana city, Marg'ilon, Qo'qon (Kokand), and the 13 rural districts (Fergana tumani, Oltiariq, Bag'dod, Beshariq, Buvayda, Dang'ara, Furqat, Qo'shtepa, Quva, Rishton, So'x, Toshloq, Uchko'prik, Yozyovon). Built entirely through web search (business directories — Golden Pages UZ, Yellow Pages UZ, top.uz — OLX classifieds, Yandex Maps review snippets, gov.uz/local-press coverage, and ielts.org's official test-centre and receiving-organisation lists) in Uzbek, Russian and English. **Direct access to most `.uz` websites, Instagram and Telegram was blocked by this session's network policy**, so every entry below is reconstructed from search-result snippets rather than live pages — treat all contact details as a starting point to verify, not as confirmed-current. Per the brief, known big chains (SATashkent, Everest Learning Center) are explicitly excluded even where they surfaced in results — including SATashkent's Kokand hiring-page listing, which is out of scope as instructed.

**21 distinct center entries found, all concentrated in the three cities (Fergana city, Marg'ilon, Kokand).** Every one of the 13 rural districts, plus Fergana tumani (the rural district distinct from Fergana city), returned **zero** center-specific results — see Research Limitations.

A recurring data-quality problem: a large cluster of Fergana-city Golden Pages listings (Nurik's Academy, both IELTS Zone branches, Opus, Avangard, Fergana School, Master SAT, Wegweiser) all share the *exact same two pairs* of phone numbers — (+998 97) 744-91-70 / 748-55-56 and (+998 90) 318-27-19 / (+998 95) 170-07-06. This is almost certainly a directory-service placeholder rather than each business's real line (the same pattern was already flagged for other regions in `10-uzbekistan-sat-centers-leads.md`). These numbers are included below for completeness but flagged inline — do not expect them to reach the specific business without independent verification.

## Research Limitations

- **Network access constraint:** this session could not directly load most `.uz` websites, Instagram, or Telegram (DNS/policy blocks on the sandboxed network), so every entry above is reconstructed from search-engine result snippets and cached directory/review text rather than live pages. Contact numbers, addresses, and current course offerings should be re-verified by calling, visiting the listing directly, or checking the center's live social media before outreach.
- **Duplicated directory phone numbers (major caveat):** a cluster of Fergana-city Golden Pages listings — Nurik's Academy, both IELTS Zone branches, Opus, Avangard, Fergana School, Master SAT, and Wegweiser — all return the *identical* two phone-number pairs. This is the same pattern flagged in earlier regional research (`10-uzbekistan-sat-centers-leads.md`) and is very likely a shared directory/admin number rather than each business's real line. Do not expect to reach a specific center by dialing these without first confirming through another channel (visiting in person, a distinct listing, or social media).
- **Zero results for all 13 rural districts, plus Fergana tumani (rural):** Oltiariq, Bag'dod, Beshariq, Buvayda, Dang'ara, Furqat, Qo'shtepa, Quva, Rishton, So'x, Toshloq, Uchko'prik, and Yozyovon — as well as the Fergana district (tumani) distinct from Fergana city — returned **no center-specific results at all** across Uzbek, Russian, and English query variants against business directories (Golden Pages, Yellow Pages, top.uz), OLX classifieds, Instagram/Telegram search, and local press. OLX.uz does have per-district "obrazovanie" (education-services) category pages for some of these towns (e.g., Rishtan), but the ones checked returned zero active listings. This strongly suggests either (a) genuinely thin commercial tutoring-center supply in these smaller districts, or (b) a web/social presence too localized (closed Telegram/WhatsApp groups, word-of-mouth, unlisted storefronts) for search engines to surface — on-the-ground inquiry (district hokimiyat education department, local mahalla contacts, school counselors) would likely be far more productive than further web search for these areas.
- **Notable indirect signal from rural districts:** gov.uz's own regional news site reported an 11th-grade student at a *specialized public school* in Fergana tumani (rural district) scoring 1460 on the international SAT (Sept. 2026), and talimxabarlari.uz reported an 11th-grader at secondary school №49 in Rishton district scoring 1550 SAT. These show SAT awareness and self-study activity reaching into rural districts, but both are public schools, not commercial tutoring centers — not actionable as sales leads, but useful context that demand/awareness exists even where no center could be found.
- **Small, informal tutors are almost certainly under-represented.** Individual tutors, closed Telegram groups, and word-of-mouth arrangements — especially common in smaller towns — are largely invisible to a web-search-based methodology.
- **Possible duplicate entities:** "Intellect" and "Intellekt Magistr" in Kokand may be the same business counted twice (different directory sources, similar name, both in Kokand) — verify before treating as two separate leads. Likewise, "My School"'s Marg'ilon branch shares a name with several Tashkent "My School" branches that are a different, larger chain-like operation; the Marg'ilon branch itself (per the gov.uz article) appears to be a genuinely regional operation, but this should be confirmed on contact.
- **SAT-explicit vs. SAT-adjacent:** only Nurik's Academy (Fergana city) explicitly lists "Подготовка к SAT" as a service. Master SAT O'quv Markazi's name signals SAT but its description text is generic entrance-exam language. All other entries are general English/IELTS/university-entrance-prep centers included per the brief's instruction to cast a broad net — each should be asked directly (per the stated business plan) whether they currently teach SAT and what materials they use.
- **Out-of-scope exclusions applied:** SATashkent and Everest Learning Center results that surfaced during this search (including SATashkent's Kokand-hiring job postings) were excluded per the brief, since both are already-known large chains out of scope for this list.
