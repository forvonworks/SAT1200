# Uzbekistan SAT Prep Centers — Sales Lead List

Compiled for business development around a SAT Math prep book series. This list was built entirely through web search (Google-indexed pages, business directories — Yellow Pages UZ, Golden Pages UZ, top.uz, kursi24.uz — OLX classifieds, job-board postings on hh.uz/hh.ru, and local press/listings coverage) in English, Uzbek and Russian. **Direct access to most `.uz` websites was blocked by this session's network policy**, so entries are built from search-result snippets and cached directory text rather than live pages — contact details in particular should be treated as a starting point to verify, not as confirmed-current. Where multiple directory listings for different businesses returned the *identical* phone numbers, this is flagged explicitly, since it most likely means those are a directory's own placeholder/admin numbers rather than the business's real line.

23 distinct center/branch entries are listed below across 6 cities (Tashkent, Samarkand, Bukhara, Fergana, Namangan, Andijan, plus an unconfirmed Kokand lead). No verifiable SAT-specific centers were found for Nukus, Urgench/Khiva, Chartak, or the smaller regional capitals (Qarshi, Termez, Jizzakh, Navoiy, Guliston) — see Research Limitations.

## Research Limitations

- **Network access constraint:** this session could not directly load most Uzbek (`.uz`) websites (DNS/policy blocks on the sandboxed network), so every entry above is reconstructed from search-engine result snippets and cached directory text rather than live pages. Contact numbers, prices, and addresses should be re-verified by opening the source links directly, calling, or checking the center's live social media before outreach.
- **Duplicated directory phone numbers:** several unrelated listings across different cities (Namangan's Learning Skills Lab and Oxford School, Andijan's Proper English School, Fergana's Master SAT, Samarkand's SAT Samarkand) returned the *exact same* two phone-number pairs. This is very likely a quirk of the Golden Pages directory (e.g., a shared admin/listing-service number) rather than each business's real line — do not dial these expecting to reach the specific center without first confirming via another channel.
- **Small, informal tutors are almost certainly under-represented.** A large share of actual SAT tutoring in Uzbekistan likely happens through individual tutors, closed Telegram groups/channels, and word-of-mouth arrangements that never appear in Google-indexed directories, OLX ads, or press coverage — these are invisible to a web-search-based methodology like this one.
- **Regional coverage gaps:** no SAT-specific center could be confirmed for Nukus/Karakalpakstan, Urgench or Khiva (Khorezm), Chartak (a smaller town in Namangan region), or the regional capitals of Qarshi (Kashkadarya), Termez (Surkhandarya), Jizzakh, Navoiy, or Guliston (Sirdarya). This may reflect genuinely thinner SAT-prep supply in these regions, weaker web/social presence of any centers that do exist there, or both — on-the-ground inquiry (local Telegram channels, school counselors, word of mouth) would likely surface centers that search engines do not.
- **Chain branch counts are self-reported and sometimes inconsistent.** SATashkent's own materials give a "4 branches" figure in one place while its job-recruiting page lists additional cities (Fergana, Namangan, Kokand) hiring staff — these may be newly opening locations, online-only roles, or recruiting ahead of a confirmed opening; treat any city not in the "4 branches" claim as unconfirmed until verified directly.
- **"SAT offering" confidence varies by row.** Some centers (SATashkent, Texnik School, Aridian School, New Uzbekistan University, "Пять с плюсом") explicitly and unambiguously advertise SAT courses. Others (Everest Learning Center, BUNKER SCHOOL) advertise general "entrance exam preparation" or "university admission prep" without SAT named explicitly in the sources found — these are included per the brief's instruction to include general-SAT-adjacent prep providers, but should be confirmed as actually teaching SAT content before being treated as fully qualified leads.
