# Book 1 — Lesson 2: Fractions

**Source content file.** Structure follows `research/14-design-spec.md` §6:
Definition → Model example → Your-turn → common-mistake box → Vocabulary,
repeated per concept.

**Research grounding:** `research/02-topic-difficulty-taxonomy.md` names
fraction operations (add/subtract/multiply/divide, mixed numbers, simplifying)
as "repeatedly named as the #1 pre-college math gap" — the gap is procedural
fluency, not conceptual understanding (Uzbek curriculum already teaches what
a fraction is). This lesson is built around that: a brief terminology pass,
then heavy focus on the four operations and mixed-number conversion, each
with a documented, named error pattern — not generic "be careful" advice.

## Concept 1 — Kasr va Uning Qismlari; Soddalashtirish

### Definition

*Butunning bir yoki bir necha teng bo'lagini ifodalovchi son kasr deb
ataladi; u ikki qismdan — surat va maxrajdan — iborat bo'lib, surat/maxraj
ko'rinishida yoziladi. Maxraj butun nechta teng bo'lakka bo'linganini, surat
esa shu bo'laklardan nechtasi olinganini ko'rsatadi.*

<small>(A fraction represents one or more equal parts of a whole; it has two
parts — a numerator and a denominator — written as numerator/denominator.
The denominator shows how many equal parts the whole is divided into; the
numerator shows how many of those parts are taken.)</small>

*Agar surat va maxrajning umumiy bo'luvchisi 1 dan katta bo'lsa, kasrni shu
son bilan qisqartirish mumkin; natijada kasrning qiymati o'zgarmaydi, faqat
ko'rinishi soddalashadi. Surat va maxraji faqat 1 ni umumiy bo'luvchi qilib
olgan kasr eng oddiy (qisqarmas) kasr deb ataladi.*

<small>(If the numerator and denominator share a common factor greater than
1, the fraction can be reduced by that factor without changing its value —
only its form gets simpler. A fraction whose numerator and denominator share
no common factor but 1 is fully simplified.)</small>

### Model Example (1-misol)

$$\frac{24}{36} \text{ ni eng oddiy ko'rinishga keltiring.}$$

**Yechish:** 24 va 36 ning eng katta umumiy bo'luvchisi (EKUB) — 12.
$24 \div 12 = 2$, $36 \div 12 = 3$.

**Javob:** $\dfrac{2}{3}$

### Your-Turn Example (O'zingiz bajaring)

$$\frac{18}{30} \text{ ni eng oddiy ko'rinishga keltiring} = \underline{\hspace{20mm}}$$

## Concept 2 — Kasrlarni Qo'shish va Ayirish

### Definition

*Maxrajlari har xil bo'lgan kasrlarni qo'shish yoki ayirish uchun, avval
ularni umumiy maxrajga — odatda maxrajlarning eng kichik umumiy karralisiga
(EKUK) — keltirish kerak; faqat shundan keyin suratlar bilan amal bajariladi,
maxraj esa o'zgarmaydi.*

<small>(To add or subtract fractions with different denominators, they must
first be brought to a common denominator — usually the least common multiple
(LCM) of the denominators; only then are the numerators combined, while the
denominator stays the same.)</small>

### Model Example (2-misol)

$$\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{6} = ?$$

**Yechish:** 4 va 6 ning EKUKi — 12. $\dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{3}{12}$,
$\dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{2}{12}$. $\dfrac{3}{12} + \dfrac{2}{12} = \dfrac{5}{12}$.

**Javob:** $\dfrac{5}{12}$

### Your-Turn Example (O'zingiz bajaring)

$$\frac{2}{3} - \frac{1}{5} = \underline{\hspace{20mm}}$$

### Common Mistake Box (conditional — documented error, include)

**Noto'g'ri:** suratlarni o'zaro, maxrajlarni o'zaro qo'shib yuborish —
$\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{4}$ ni $\dfrac{1+1}{3+4} = \dfrac{2}{7}$ deb
hisoblash.

**To'g'ri:** avval umumiy maxrajga keltirish shart. $\dfrac{1}{3} =
\dfrac{4}{12}$, $\dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{3}{12}$, demak $\dfrac{1}{3} +
\dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{4}{12} + \dfrac{3}{12} = \dfrac{7}{12}$ —
$\dfrac{2}{7}$ emas.

*(Research basis: this is one of the most robustly documented fraction
misconceptions — treating numerator and denominator as two independent
whole-number sums. An NFER analysis of English students found only 54%
answered correctly when denominators differed, with "add straight across"
the dominant wrong answer; even with matching denominators, ~10% of
lower-achieving students still added both parts. Documented back to Carpenter
(1976) and Howard (1991); a 2022 study of Colombian 7th-graders found the
identical pattern. See session research notes.)*

## Concept 3 — Kasrlarni Ko'paytirish

### Definition

*Ikki kasrni ko'paytirish uchun suratlar o'zaro, maxrajlar o'zaro
ko'paytiriladi; umumiy maxrajga keltirish talab qilinmaydi.*

<small>(To multiply two fractions, multiply the numerators together and the
denominators together; no common denominator is needed.)</small>

### Model Example (3-misol)

$$\frac{4}{9} \times \frac{3}{8} = ?$$

**Yechish:** Ko'paytirishdan oldin umumiy ko'paytuvchilarni qisqartirish
hisoblashni osonlashtiradi: 4 va 8 ning umumiy ko'paytuvchisi — 4; 3 va 9
ning umumiy ko'paytuvchisi — 3. Qisqartirgandan so'ng:
$\dfrac{1}{3} \times \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{1}{6}$.

**Javob:** $\dfrac{1}{6}$

### Your-Turn Example (O'zingiz bajaring)

$$\frac{5}{6} \times \frac{3}{10} = \underline{\hspace{20mm}}$$

## Concept 4 — Kasrlarni Bo'lish

### Definition

*Bir kasrni ikkinchisiga bo'lish uchun, bo'luvchi kasrning teskarisi
(surat va maxraji almashtirilgan ko'rinishi) bilan ko'paytiriladi; bu usul
"teskarisiga ko'paytirish" deb ataladi.*

<small>(To divide one fraction by another, multiply by the reciprocal of
the divisor — the fraction with numerator and denominator swapped; this
method is called "multiplying by the reciprocal.")</small>

### Model Example (4-misol)

$$\frac{3}{4} \div \frac{2}{5} = ?$$

**Yechish:** $\dfrac{3}{4} \div \dfrac{2}{5} = \dfrac{3}{4} \times
\dfrac{5}{2} = \dfrac{15}{8}$.

**Javob:** $\dfrac{15}{8}$

### Your-Turn Example (O'zingiz bajaring)

$$\frac{5}{8} \div \frac{3}{4} = \underline{\hspace{20mm}}$$

### Common Mistake Box (conditional — documented error, include)

**Noto'g'ri:** teskarisini noto'g'ri kasrga qo'llash — $\dfrac{3}{4} \div
\dfrac{2}{5}$ ni $\dfrac{4}{3} \times \dfrac{2}{5}$ deb hisoblash (birinchi
kasr teskari qilingan, ikkinchisi emas).

**To'g'ri:** faqat ikkinchi kasr (bo'luvchi) teskari qilinadi, birinchisi
o'zgarishsiz qoladi: $\dfrac{3}{4} \div \dfrac{2}{5} = \dfrac{3}{4} \times
\dfrac{5}{2} = \dfrac{15}{8}$ — $\dfrac{4}{3} \times \dfrac{2}{5}$ emas.

## Concept 5 — Aralash Son va Noto'g'ri Kasr

### Definition

*Butun son va kasrning yig'indisidan iborat son aralash son deb ataladi
(masalan, $2\tfrac{1}{2}$); surati maxrajidan katta yoki unga teng bo'lgan
kasr esa noto'g'ri kasr deb ataladi. Aralash sonni noto'g'ri kasrga
aylantirish uchun, butun son maxrajga ko'paytirilib, suratga qo'shiladi,
maxraj esa o'zgarmaydi.*

<small>(A number made of a whole number plus a fraction is a mixed number
(e.g., $2\tfrac{1}{2}$); a fraction whose numerator is greater than or equal
to its denominator is an improper fraction. To convert a mixed number to an
improper fraction, multiply the whole number by the denominator and add the
numerator, keeping the same denominator.)</small>

### Model Example (5-misol)

$$3\tfrac{2}{5} \text{ ni noto'g'ri kasrga aylantiring.}$$

**Yechish:** $3 \times 5 = 15$, $15 + 2 = 17$.

**Javob:** $\dfrac{17}{5}$

*(Teskari yo'nalish: $\dfrac{23}{6}$ ni aralash songa aylantirish uchun
$23 \div 6 = 3$ qoldiq $5$, demak $\dfrac{23}{6} = 3\tfrac{5}{6}$.)*

### Your-Turn Example (O'zingiz bajaring)

$$4\tfrac{1}{3} \text{ ni noto'g'ri kasrga} = \underline{\hspace{15mm}}
\qquad\qquad \frac{29}{8} \text{ ni aralash songa} = \underline{\hspace{15mm}}$$

### Common Mistake Box (conditional — documented error, include)

**Noto'g'ri:** butun sonni maxrajga ko'paytirmasdan, to'g'ridan-to'g'ri
suratga qo'shib yuborish — $3\tfrac{2}{5}$ ni $\dfrac{3+2}{5} = \dfrac{5}{5}$
deb hisoblash.

**To'g'ri:** butun son avval maxrajga ko'paytiriladi: $3 \times 5 = 15$,
keyin suratga qo'shiladi: $15 + 2 = 17$, demak $3\tfrac{2}{5} =
\dfrac{17}{5}$ — $\dfrac{5}{5}$ emas.

## Notes for the problems file (Sets 1–7)

- Set 1: simplifying fractions to lowest terms.
- Set 2: adding/subtracting fractions with unlike denominators.
- Set 3: multiplying fractions (including ones that reward cross-cancellation).
- Set 4: dividing fractions.
- Set 5: mixed number ↔ improper fraction conversion, both directions, plus
  one each of add/subtract/multiply on mixed numbers.
- Set 6: "spot the error" — straight-across addition, divide-without-flipping,
  incomplete simplification, and mixed-number-conversion errors, each tied to
  a Concept above.
- Set 7 (bonus/challenge): multi-step expressions combining two or more
  fraction operations in one problem.
