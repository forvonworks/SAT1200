## Concept 1 — Musbat va Manfiy Sonlar (Positive and Negative Numbers)

### Definition

*Sonlar o'qida markaz — ya'ni 0 — dan o'ngda joylashgan barcha sonlar musbat
sonlar, chapda joylashgan barcha sonlar esa manfiy sonlar deb ataladi. Sonlar
o'qida 0 dan bir xil masofada, lekin qarama-qarshi tomonlarda joylashgan ikki
son qarama-qarshi sonlar deb ataladi (masalan, $5$ va $-5$). Biror sonning
sonlar o'qida $0$ gacha bo'lgan masofasi shu sonning moduli deb ataladi va
$|\ |$ belgisi bilan yoziladi.*

<small>(Numbers to the right of 0 on the number line are positive, numbers to
the left are negative. Two numbers the same distance from 0 but on opposite
sides are called opposite numbers (e.g. 5 and −5). A number's distance from 0
on the number line is called its absolute value, written with $|\ |$.)</small>

**Why this matters for the SAT:** absolute value notation shows up constantly
in Digital SAT algebra items (inequalities, equation-solving with two cases),
not just as the grouping symbol covered in Lesson 1 — students need the
*distance* meaning solid, not just "makes negative numbers positive."

### Quick reference table

| Son (Number) | Modul (Absolute value) | Qarama-qarshi son (Opposite) |
|---|---|---|
| $7$ | $|7|=7$ | $-7$ |
| $-7$ | $|-7|=7$ | $7$ |
| $-12$ | $|-12|=12$ | $12$ |
| $0$ | $|0|=0$ | $0$ |

*(No model/your-turn pair here — this is a fluency check on notation, not new
material. Moving straight to the lesson's real content.)*

## Concept 2 — Ishorali Sonlarni Qo'shish va Ayirish (Adding and Subtracting Signed Numbers)

### Definition

*Ishoralari bir xil bo'lgan ikki son qo'shilganda, ularning modullari
qo'shiladi va yig'indi oldiga shu umumiy ishora qo'yiladi. Ishoralari har xil
bo'lgan ikki son qo'shilganda esa, kattaroq modulli sondan kichik modulli son
ayiriladi va natija oldiga kattaroq modulli sonning ishorasi qo'yiladi. Biror
sondan manfiy sonni ayirish, o'sha sonning musbatini qo'shishga teng —
ya'ni $a-(-b)=a+b$.*

<small>(Adding two numbers with the same sign: add the absolute values, keep
that sign. Adding two numbers with different signs: subtract the smaller
absolute value from the larger, take the sign of the larger. Subtracting a
negative number is the same as adding its positive — $a-(-b)=a+b$.)</small>

### Model Example (1-misol)

$$(-7) + 12 - (-5) = ?$$

**Yechish:** Avval ayirish-manfiy qoidasini qo'llaymiz: $-(-5)=+5$, shuning
uchun ifoda $(-7)+12+5$ ko'rinishiga keladi. Chapdan o'ngga hisoblaymiz:
$-7+12=5$, so'ngra $5+5=10$.

**Javob:** $10$

### Your-Turn Example (O'zingiz bajaring)

$$(-9) - (-14) + (-3) = \underline{\hspace{25mm}}$$

### Common Mistake Box (conditional — documented error, include)

**Noto'g'ri yondashuv:** $8-(-3)$ ifodasini $8-3=5$ deb hisoblash — ya'ni
ayirish belgisi oldidagi manfiy ishorani e'tiborsiz qoldirish.

**To'g'ri qoida:** Manfiy sonni ayirish — musbatini qo'shish bilan bir xil:
$8-(-3)=8+3=11$. Ikkita ketma-ket ishora ($-$ va $-$) uchrasa, ular doim
$+$ ga almashadi.

*(Research basis: subtracting a negative number is independently documented
as the single highest-error-rate step in integer arithmetic across multiple
studies — one study of preservice elementary teachers found 53% made a
concept-level error specifically on this operation, more than any other
integer-operation type tested.)*

### Vocabulary

| English | O'zbekcha |
|---|---|
| Positive / Negative number | Musbat / Manfiy son |
| Opposite numbers | Qarama-qarshi sonlar |
| Absolute value | Modul |
| Number line | Sonlar o'qi |
| Sign | Ishora |
| Same sign / Different sign | Bir xil ishora / Har xil ishora |

## Concept 3 — Ishorali Sonlarni Ko'paytirish va Bo'lish (Multiplying and Dividing Signed Numbers)

### Definition

*Ishoralari bir xil bo'lgan ikki sonning ko'paytmasi (yoki bo'linmasi) musbat
bo'ladi; ishoralari har xil bo'lgan ikki sonning ko'paytmasi (yoki bo'linmasi)
esa manfiy bo'ladi.*

<small>(Two numbers with the same sign multiply or divide to a positive
result; two numbers with different signs multiply or divide to a negative
result.)</small>

### Model Example (1-misol)

$$(-4) \times (-3) \div (-2) = ?$$

**Yechish:** Chapdan o'ngga bajaramiz. Avval $(-4) \times (-3)$: ishoralar bir
xil (ikkalasi ham manfiy), shuning uchun natija musbat — $12$. Keyin
$12 \div (-2)$: ishoralar har xil, shuning uchun natija manfiy — $-6$.

**Javob:** $-6$

### Your-Turn Example (O'zingiz bajaring)

$$(-18) \div (-3) \times (-2) = \underline{\hspace{25mm}}$$

### Common Mistake Box (conditional — documented error, include)

**Noto'g'ri yondashuv:** $(-5) \times (-6)$ ni $-30$ deb hisoblash — ikkala
ko'paytuvchi ham manfiy bo'lgani uchun natija ham manfiy bo'ladi deb o'ylash.

**To'g'ri qoida:** Ikkala ko'paytuvchining ishorasi bir xil (ikkalasi ham
manfiy) bo'lgani uchun natija **musbat**: $(-5) \times (-6) = 30$. Uzun
ko'paytma yoki bo'linmada bir nechta manfiy son bo'lsa, ularning sonini
sanash foydali: manfiy sonlar soni **juft** bo'lsa natija musbat, **toq**
bo'lsa natija manfiy bo'ladi.

### Vocabulary

| English | O'zbekcha |
|---|---|
| Multiply / Divide | Ko'paytirish / Bo'lish |
| Even / Odd (count) | Juft / Toq (son) |
| Result | Natija |

## Concept 4 — Qavslarni Ochish Qoidalari (Opening Brackets / Distributing a Sign)

### Definition

*Qavs oldida turgan ishora qavs ichidagi har bir hadga ta'sir qiladi: qavs
oldida manfiy ishora bo'lsa, qavs ichidagi har bir hadning ishorasi teskarisiga
almashtiriladi; qavs oldida musbat ishora bo'lsa, hadlarning ishoralari
o'zgarmay qoladi.*

<small>(The sign in front of a parenthesis applies to every term inside it: a
negative sign flips every term's sign; a positive sign leaves every term
unchanged.)</small>

$$-(+a) = -a \qquad -(-a) = +a \qquad +(-a) = -a \qquad +(+a) = +a$$

$$-(a+b) = -a-b \qquad -(a-b) = -a+b$$

### Model Example (1-misol)

$$-(7 - 4) + 3 = ?$$

**Yechish:** Qavs oldida manfiy ishora turibdi, shuning uchun qavs ichidagi
har bir had ishorasi teskarisiga almashadi: $-(7-4) = -7+4$. Ifoda
$-7+4+3$ ko'rinishiga keladi. Chapdan o'ngga: $-7+4=-3$, so'ngra $-3+3=0$.

**Javob:** $0$

### Your-Turn Example (O'zingiz bajaring)

$$-(5 - 9) - 6 = \underline{\hspace{25mm}}$$

### Common Mistake Box (conditional — documented error, include)

**Noto'g'ri yondashuv:** $-(10-4)$ ifodasini $-10-4=-14$ deb hisoblash —
qavs ichidagi faqat birinchi hadga manfiy ishorani qo'llab, ikkinchi
hadning ishorasini o'zgartirmay qoldirish.

**To'g'ri qoida:** Qavs oldidagi manfiy ishora qavs ichidagi **har bir**
hadga ta'sir qiladi, faqat birinchisiga emas: $-(10-4) = -10+4 = -6$.

*(Research basis: `04-common-student-mistakes.md` names this exact
pattern — e.g. `-(x-4)` simplified to `-x-4` instead of `-x+4` — "the single
most common trap" in algebraic manipulation across multiple independent
sources.)*

### Vocabulary

| English | O'zbekcha |
|---|---|
| Opening brackets / Distributing | Qavslarni ochish |
| Term | Had |
| To flip (a sign) | Teskarisiga almashtirish |

## Notes for the problems file (Sets 1–7)

- Set 1: pure same-sign addition — build the "add magnitudes, keep the sign"
  habit before mixing signs.
- Set 2: mixed-sign addition and subtracting-a-negative — directly targets the
  single highest-documented-error-rate integer operation.
- Set 3: two-factor multiplication/division sign rules.
- Set 4: three-to-four-factor chains — practice counting negatives
  (even/odd) rather than tracking sign step by step.
- Set 5: opening-brackets / distributing-a-sign drills.
- Set 6: "spot the error" format — five items, each built from one of this
  lesson's three documented misconceptions (subtracting-a-negative ignored,
  double-negative-multiplication misjudged, negative-distributed-to-only-one-term).
- Set 7 (bonus/challenge): multi-step expressions combining all four concepts
  in one expression, timed.
