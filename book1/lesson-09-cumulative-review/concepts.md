# Book 1 — Lesson 9: Cumulative Review & Mixed Practice

**Source content file**, capping Book 1. This lesson is deliberately different from
Lessons 1-8: it introduces no new rules. Its job is to force Lessons 1-8 to work
*together* on the same problem, the way the actual SAT does — a real test question
never politely waits until you've finished "the fractions chapter" before mixing in
a negative exponent. Research backs this directly: interleaved/mixed practice
produces better retention than blocked, one-skill-at-a-time practice
(`06-concept-explanation-pedagogy.md`). If a student can do every Book 1 lesson in
isolation but freezes the moment two of them show up in the same problem, Book 1
isn't actually finished yet — that's exactly the gap this lesson is built to close.

## Mixed Example 1 — Fractions + Decimals + Equations

### Model Example (1-misol)

$$\frac{1}{2}(x-4) + 0.25x = 5$$

**Yechish:** Avval $0.25$ ni kasrga aylantiramiz: $0.25 = \frac{1}{4}$. Qavsni
ochamiz: $\frac{1}{2}x - 2 + \frac{1}{4}x = 5$. O'xshash hadlarni qo'shamiz
(umumiy maxraj 4): $\frac{2}{4}x + \frac{1}{4}x = \frac{3}{4}x$. Ifoda:
$\frac{3}{4}x - 2 = 5$. Ikkala tomonga $+2$: $\frac{3}{4}x = 7$. Ikkala tomonni
$\frac{3}{4}$ ga bo'lamiz (ya'ni $\frac{4}{3}$ ga ko'paytiramiz):
$x = 7 \times \frac{4}{3} = \frac{28}{3}$.

**Javob:** $x = \dfrac{28}{3}$

*(Bu javob butun son emas — va bu normal. Bu masala atayin shunday tuzilgan:
kasr va o'nli kasrni bitta tenglamada aralashtirib, aniq javobni toping, uni
o'nli kasrga aylantirib "tekislashga" urinmang.)*

## Mixed Example 2 — f(x) + Signed Numbers + Exponents

### Model Example (2-misol)

$f(x) = -2(x-1)^2 + 3$ berilgan. $f(-1)$ ni toping.

**Yechish:** $x$ o'rniga $-1$ ni qo'yamiz: $f(-1) = -2(-1-1)^2+3$. Avval qavs:
$-1-1=-2$. Daraja: $(-2)^2=4$. Endi: $-2\times4+3$. Ko'paytirish:
$-2\times4=-8$. Oxirida: $-8+3=-5$.

**Javob:** $f(-1) = -5$

*(E'tibor bering: bu yerda to'rtta ko'nikma ketma-ket ishlatildi — o'rniga
qo'yish (f(x)), qavs ichida ayirish (signed numbers), daraja (exponents), va
amallar bajarish tartibi. Birortasida xato — butun javob xato.)*
