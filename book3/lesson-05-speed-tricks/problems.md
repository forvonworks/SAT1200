## Answer Key

Each entry: answer, a short worked solution that uses the shortcut, and the trick used.

### Set 1 — Bridge: ask first, then shortcut

1. **D ($66$)** — $15x-9=3(5x-3)=3\cdot22=66$. There is no need to find $x$ (it is 5).  *Trick: What is asked?*

2. **A ($6$)** — Add the equations: $5x+5y=30$, so $x+y=6$. (Subtracting would give $x-y=8$, the other trap.)  *Trick: Add the equations*

3. **7** — Sum of roots $=-\dfrac ba=\dfrac{21}{3}=7$. (The discriminant is positive, so both roots are real.)  *Trick: Vieta*

4. **B ($4$)** — Try the middle choices: $x=4$ gives $2^5+2^4=32+16=48$. (Or: $3\cdot2^x=48$, $2^x=16$. Choice 16 is $2^x$, not $x$.)  *Trick: Backsolve*

5. **C ($117\%$)** — Multipliers multiply: $1.30\times0.90=1.17$, so $117\%$. (Adding $+30\%-10\%$ gives the trap $120\%$.)  *Trick: Percent multiplier*

6. **-4** — Divisible by $x-2$ means $p(2)=0$: $16-20+2k+12=0$, so $2k=-8$ and $k=-4$.  *Trick: Factor / remainder theorem*

7. **B ($-3$)** — The zeros are $2$ and $-8$; the vertex is halfway between them: $\dfrac{2+(-8)}2=-3$. (48 is $h(0)$.)  *Trick: Axis = midpoint of the roots*

8. **C ($9$)** — Move everything left and factor: $(x-7)(x+3-5)=(x-7)(x-2)=0$, so $x=7$ or $2$ and the sum is $9$. Dividing by $x-7$ would lose the root $7$.  *Trick: Do not divide: factor*

9. **53** — $x^2+y^2=(x+y)^2-2xy=81-28=53$.  *Trick: Structure (identities)*

10. **D (Exactly three)** — Graph $y=2^x$ and $y=x^2$: they cross at $x=2$, $x=4$ and once more near $x\approx-0.77$. That is 3 points.  *Trick: Graph it (Desmos)*

11. **77** — $(a+b)^2-(a-b)^2=4ab$, so $4ab=324-16=308$ and $ab=77$. (The numbers are 11 and 7.)  *Trick: Structure (identities)*

12. **B ($(x-y-3)(x+y+3)$)** — $x^2-(y^2+6y+9)=x^2-(y+3)^2=(x-y-3)(x+y+3)$: a perfect square first, then a difference of squares.  *Trick: Structure (identities)*

### Set 2 — Vieta: sums and products without solving

1. **B ($22$)** — $r+s=5$ and $rs=\frac32$, so $r^2+s^2=(r+s)^2-2rs=25-3=22$. (Forgetting to divide by $a$ gives 19.)  *Trick: Vieta (sum of squares)*

2. **20** — Let the roots be $t$ and $5t$. The sum is $6t=12$, so $t=2$. The product is $k=t\cdot5t=5(4)=20$.  *Trick: Vieta*

3. **D ($4$)** — $\dfrac1p+\dfrac1q=\dfrac{p+q}{pq}=\dfrac{8/3}{2/3}=4$.  *Trick: Vieta (reciprocals)*

4. **D ($22$)** — $x^2-7x+5=0$, so $x_1+x_2=7$. Then $y_1+y_2=2(x_1+x_2)+2(4)=22$. (Forgetting the second 4 gives 18.)  *Trick: Vieta (system)*

5. **2** — $x^2+(b+4)x+4=0$ has one solution when $(b+4)^2-16=0$, so $b=0$ or $b=-8$; $b<0$ gives $b=-8$. Then $x=-\frac{b+4}2=2$.  *Trick: Tangent: D = 0*

6. **D ($17$)** — $c=(r+2)(s+2)=rs+2(r+s)+4=3+10+4=17$. (Leaving out the final $+4$ gives 13.)  *Trick: Vieta*

7. **-3** — $(r-2)(s-2)=rs-2(r+s)+4=2-2\left(\frac92\right)+4=-3$.  *Trick: Vieta*

8. **C ($\sqrt{26}$)** — $D=144-40=104$, and the roots differ by $\dfrac{\sqrt D}{|a|}=\dfrac{\sqrt{104}}2=\sqrt{26}$. (Forgetting to divide by $a$ gives $2\sqrt{26}$.)  *Trick: Vieta (difference of roots)*

9. **8** — $r+s=k$ and $rs=12$, so $r^2+s^2=k^2-24=40$. Then $k^2=64$ and $k=8$ (positive). The roots are 2 and 6.  *Trick: Vieta (sum of squares)*

10. **A ($\sqrt{97}$)** — $l+w=13$ and $lw=36$, so $l^2+w^2=169-72=97$. The diagonal is $\sqrt{97}$. (13 is the half-perimeter.)  *Trick: Vieta (sum of squares)*

11. **B ($21$)** — $r+s=10$ and $r-s=4$, so $r=7$, $s=3$ and $c=rs=21$. (25 would mean equal roots.)  *Trick: Vieta*

12. **-72** — $r^3+s^3=(r+s)^3-3rs(r+s)=(-3)^3-3(-5)(-3)=-27-45=-72$.  *Trick: Vieta (sum of cubes)*

13. **A ($16$)** — The roots of $2x^2-bx+c=0$ have sum $\frac b2=5$ and product $\frac c2=3$, so $b=10$, $c=6$ and $b+c=16$.  *Trick: Vieta*

14. **C ($2x^2-7x+3=0$)** — New sum $=\frac{S}{P}=\frac{7/3}{2/3}=\frac72$, new product $=\frac1P=\frac32$. So $x^2-\frac72x+\frac32=0$, i.e. $2x^2-7x+3=0$ (the $a$ and $c$ swap).  *Trick: Vieta (reciprocals)*

### Set 3 — Vertex, axis, symmetry and graph-it

1. **C ($11$)** — Equal outputs mean the axis is halfway: $x=\frac{2+9}2=\frac{11}2$. For $-x^2+bx+c$ the axis is $\frac b2$, so $b=11$.  *Trick: Symmetry (axis)*

2. **6** — The axis is halfway between $t=1$ and $t=5$, so it is $t=3$. Launch ($t=0$) is 3 units left of the axis, so the ball is back at 1.2 m at $t=3+3=6$.  *Trick: Symmetry (axis)*

3. **D ($4$)** — $(x-6)(x+2)$ is lowest halfway between $-2$ and $6$, at $x=2$, where it equals $-16$. So the minimum of $f$ is $-16+20=4$. (2 is where it happens.)  *Trick: Axis = midpoint of the roots*

4. **30** — Axis: $x=-\frac{-8}{2}=4$. On the line, $y=3(4)+2=14$. So $16-32+k=14$ and $k=30$.  *Trick: Axis = $-b/2a$*

5. **3.9** — Graph $y=2^x$ and $y=2x+7$ and tap the right-hand intersection: $x\approx3.885$, which rounds to $3.9$. (The other crossing is near $-3.45$.)  *Trick: Graph it (Desmos)*

6. **A ($3$)** — Graphs of $y=2^x$ and $y=x^2+1$ meet at $x=0$, $x=1$ and a third time near $x\approx4.26$. Testing whole numbers alone finds only 2.  *Trick: Graph it (Desmos)*

7. **-3** — $x^2-6x+(5-k)=0$ needs $D=36-4(5-k)=16+4k>0$, so $k>-4$. The least integer is $-3$. (On Desmos: add a slider for $k$ and watch the crossings.)  *Trick: Graph it (slider) / D > 0*

8. **C ($-20$)** — $h=-\frac{-18}{6}=3$ and $k=3(9)-54+4=-23$, so $h+k=-20$. (A negative $h$ would give the trap $-26$.)  *Trick: Axis = $-b/2a$*

9. **B ($-2$)** — The vertex is at $x=\frac{1+7}2=4$, where $y=a(3)(-3)=-9a$. Then $-9a=18$ and $a=-2$.  *Trick: Axis = midpoint of the roots*

10. **C ($14$)** — $y=a(x-3)^2-4$ and $y(5)=4$ give $4a-4=4$, so $a=2$. Then $y(0)=2(9)-4=14$. (2 is $a$, not the intercept.)  *Trick: Vertex form*

11. **625** — The zeros are $p=5$ and $p=30$, so the maximum is at $p=17.5$: profit $=(12.5)(120-70)=(12.5)(50)=625$.  *Trick: Axis = midpoint of the roots*

12. **D ($18$)** — Axis: $x=\frac{1+9}2=5$, so $-\frac b2=5$ and $b=-10$. Minimum: $f(5)=25-50+c=-7$, so $c=18$.  *Trick: Symmetry (axis)*

13. **C ($3$)** — $f=g$ gives $2x^2-8x+8=2(x-2)^2=0$, so they touch only at $x=2$, where $f(2)=4-12+11=3$. (2 is the $x$-coordinate.)  *Trick: Graph it (Desmos)*

14. **B ($5\sqrt2$)** — Substitute: $2x^2+2kx+k^2-25=0$. One solution means $D=4k^2-8(k^2-25)=200-4k^2=0$, so $k^2=50$ and $k=5\sqrt2$.  *Trick: Tangent: D = 0*

### Set 4 — Do not solve: systems, factors and structure

1. **A ($30$)** — Add: $8(x+y)=48$, so $x+y=6$. Subtract: $2(x-y)=10$, so $x-y=5$. Then $x^2-y^2=(x+y)(x-y)=30$.  *Trick: Add / subtract the equations*

2. **22** — Adding all three: $2(a+b+c)=44$, so $a+b+c=22$.  *Trick: Add the equations*

3. **D ($28$)** — The expression $8x+4y$ is the sum of the two left sides: $18+10=28$.  *Trick: Add the equations*

4. **23** — Square both sides: $x^2+2+\dfrac1{x^2}=25$, so $x^2+\dfrac1{x^2}=23$.  *Trick: Structure (identities)*

5. **A ($-5$)** — The remainder is $p(-2)=16-12-10+1=-5$. (Using $p(2)=15$ is the classic sign trap.)  *Trick: Factor / remainder theorem*

6. **D ($x^3+2x^2-13x+10$)** — $x+5$ is a factor exactly when the value at $x=-5$ is 0. Only the first choice works: $-125+50+65+10=0$ (the others give $-20$, $-100$, $-130$).  *Trick: Factor / remainder theorem*

7. **4** — $(A+B)^2-(A-B)^2=4AB$ with $A=3x$, $B=2y$ gives $4(3x)(2y)=24xy=96$, so $xy=4$.  *Trick: Structure (identities)*

8. **C ($12ax$)** — Factor out $a$: $(x+3)^2-(x-3)^2=\big[(x+3)-(x-3)\big]\big[(x+3)+(x-3)\big]=6\cdot2x=12x$. So the result is $12ax$.  *Trick: Structure (identities)*

9. **A ($\$6{,}000$)** — Net multiplier $=1.20\times0.75=0.90$. Start $=\dfrac{5400}{0.90}=6000$. (Multiplying by 0.9 instead of dividing gives 4,860.)  *Trick: Percent multiplier*

10. **B ($25\%$)** — Original $=1$, reduced $=0.8$. Need $0.8(1+r)=1$, so $1+r=1.25$ and $r=25\%$. (Going up by the same 20\% only gets back to 96\%.)  *Trick: Percent multiplier*

11. **D ($40$)** — Add: $7(s+j)=56$, so $s+j=8$. Then $5(s+j)=40$. (No need for the individual prices, \$5 and \$3.)  *Trick: Add the equations*

12. **32** — $(a-b)^2=(a+b)^2-4ab=100-68=32$.  *Trick: Structure (identities)*

13. **D ($27$)** — $3^{x+2}=9\cdot3^x$, so $9\cdot3^x-3^x=8\cdot3^x=216$ and $3^x=27$. (Here $x=3$; the question asks for $3^x$, not $x$.)  *Trick: Structure (factor the common power)*

14. **A ($24$)** — $(x-8)\big[(x+2)-(3x-4)\big]=(x-8)(6-2x)=0$, so $x=8$ or $x=3$. The product is 24. (Dividing by $x-8$ loses a root.)  *Trick: Do not divide: factor*

### Set 5 — Summit: the hardest mixed problems

1. **9** — $x^2-7x+(4-k)=0$, so $x_1+x_2=7$ and $x_1x_2=4-k$. Then $49-2(4-k)=59$, so $4-k=-5$ and $k=9$.  *Trick: Vieta (sum of squares)*

2. **A ($-12m-6$)** — $16+y^2=20$ and $y<0$ give $y=-2$. Then $-2=4m+\frac b3$, so $b=-6-12m$. (Taking $y=+2$ gives the trap $6-12m$.)  *Trick: Substitute the point*

3. **C ($6$)** — Squaring gives $x^2-x-30=0$, so $x=6$ or $x=-5$. Check: $\sqrt{25}=5\neq-5$, so $-5$ is extraneous. Only $x=6$ works. (Vieta's sum, 1, is the trap.)  *Trick: Extraneous solutions (backsolve)*

4. **D ($6\sqrt{26}$)** — $r^2-s^2=(r+s)(r-s)$. Here $r+s=6$ and $r-s=\dfrac{\sqrt{104}}2=\sqrt{26}$, so the value is $6\sqrt{26}$.  *Trick: Vieta + structure*

5. **15** — $p(1)=0$: $a+b=11$. $p(-1)=6$: $-1+a-b-12=6$, so $a-b=19$. Adding: $2a=30$, so $a=15$.  *Trick: Factor / remainder theorem*

6. **B ($20$)** — $1.40\,(1-\frac p{100})=1.12$, so $1-\frac p{100}=0.80$ and $p=20$. (Subtracting $40-12$ gives the trap 28.)  *Trick: Percent multiplier*

7. **C ($2$)** — Graph both: the V-shape touches the parabola at $x=\frac{5-\sqrt5}2\approx1.38$ and $x=\frac{7+\sqrt5}2\approx4.62$. Two points.  *Trick: Graph it (Desmos)*

8. **72** — $xy=\dfrac{36-20}{2}=8$. Then $x^3+y^3=(x+y)^3-3xy(x+y)=216-3(8)(6)=72$.  *Trick: Structure (identities)*

9. **A ($-16$)** — The vertex lies halfway between the intercepts, 4 units from each. With $a=1$: $y=(4)(-4)=-16$ (the product of the distances, signed).  *Trick: Axis = midpoint of the roots*

10. **B ($x-y+3$)** — Numerator $=(x+3)^2-y^2=(x+3-y)(x+3+y)$. Cancel $x+y+3$ to get $x-y+3$.  *Trick: Structure (identities)*

11. **150** — Net multiplier $=1.2\times0.8=0.96$. Original $=\dfrac{144}{0.96}=150$.  *Trick: Percent multiplier*

12. **D ($10$)** — $a^2-b^2=(a+b)(a-b)$, so $a-b=3$. With $a+b=7$: $a=5$, $b=2$ and $ab=10$.  *Trick: What is asked? / structure*

13. **1/2** — The axis is $x=\frac{1+7}2=4$, so $f(x)=a(x-4)^2-3$. Then $f(0)=16a-3=5$ and $a=\frac12$. (Enter 1/2 or 0.5.)  *Trick: Symmetry (axis)*

14. **A ($3$)** — Graph $y=x^3-3x$ and the line $y=1$. The curve rises to a local maximum of 2 at $x=-1$, falls to $-2$ at $x=1$, then rises: 3 crossings.  *Trick: Graph it (Desmos)*

15. **C ($4$)** — The graph of $|x^2-6x+5|$ flips the dip (vertex $(3,-4)$) up to a peak at $(3,4)$. The line $y=k$ crosses it three times only at the peak: $k=4$.  *Trick: Graph it (slider)*

16. **A (No solution)** — For $x\ne2$ the left side minus $\frac{2}{x-2}$ is $\frac{x-2}{x-2}=1$, and $1=3$ is false. Clearing denominators gives $x=2$, which is excluded.  *Trick: Extraneous solutions*

17. **28** — $\frac1r+\frac1s=\frac{r+s}{rs}=\frac6q=\frac32$, so $q=4$. Then $r^2+s^2=36-2(4)=28$.  *Trick: Vieta (sum of squares)*

18. **-7.5** — $2x^2+10x+(5-c)=0$ has one solution when $D=100-8(5-c)=60+8c=0$, so $c=-7.5$.  *Trick: Tangent: D = 0*

19. **B ($60$)** — $r+s=17$ and $r^2+s^2=169$, so $rs=\dfrac{17^2-169}{2}=60$. (The sides are 5 and 12.)  *Trick: Vieta (sum of squares)*

20. **B ($-7$)** — $x^2+(b-3)x+(8-k)=0$, so $x_1+x_2=3-b$. The midpoint is $\frac{3-b}2=5$, so $b=-7$. (Using the vertex, $-\frac b2=5$, ignores the line.)  *Trick: Vieta (system)*
