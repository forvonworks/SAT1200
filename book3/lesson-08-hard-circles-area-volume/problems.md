## Answer Key

### Set 1 — Bridge: the core moves

1. **B ($12$)** — The right side is $r^2=36$, so $r=6$ and the diameter is $2r=12$. ($6$ is the radius; $36$ is $r^2$; $72$ doubles $r^2$.)  *Trick: Read $r^2$, then double $r$*

2. **A $(3,0)$** — A point is inside when $(x-1)^2+(y+2)^2<25$. For $(3,0)$: $4+4=8<25$. ($(-3,-5)$ gives $16+9=25$ and $(1,3)$ gives $0+25=25$: both are \emph{on} the circle; $(6,1)$ gives $25+9=34>25$, outside.)  *Trick: Compare distance$^2$ with $r^2$*

3. **Ans. 4** — The arc is $\frac{80}{360}=\frac29$ of the circumference $2\pi(9)=18\pi$, so it is $\frac29\cdot18\pi=4\pi$ and $k=4$.  *Trick: Arc = fraction of the circumference*

4. **D ($150$)** — The radius is $12$, so the circle has area $144\pi$ and the sector is $\frac{60\pi}{144\pi}=\frac5{12}$ of it: $\frac5{12}(360^\circ)=150^\circ$. ($37.5$ uses the diameter $24$ as the radius; $60$ copies the coefficient; $75$ uses $180^\circ$ for the full turn.)  *Trick: Sector = $\frac{\theta}{360}\pi r^2$ with $r=\frac d2$*
