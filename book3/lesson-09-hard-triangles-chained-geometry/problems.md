## Answer Key

### Set 1 — Bridge: the core moves

1. **A ($18$)** — $\sin A=\frac{BC}{AB}$, so $BC=30\cdot\frac45=24$, and $AC=\sqrt{30^2-24^2}=18$ (or $AC=30\cos A=30\cdot\frac35$). ($24$ is the side opposite $A$, not $AC$; $22.5$ uses $\tan A$; $37.5$ divides $30$ by $\frac45$.)  *Trick: Sine is opposite over hypotenuse*

2. **Ans. 11** — For acute angles, $\sin P=\cos Q$ exactly when $P$ and $Q$ are complementary: $(4x-6)+(2x+30)=90$, so $6x=66$ and $x=11$ (the angles are $38^\circ$ and $52^\circ$).  *Trick: $\sin P=\cos Q$ means $P+Q=90^\circ$*

3. **Ans. 37** — The third side must be less than $7+12=19$ (and more than $12-7=5$). The largest integer is $18$, so the perimeter is $7+12+18=37$. (A side of $19$ would make a flat, degenerate triangle.)  *Trick: Triangle inequality: third side $<$ sum*

4. **C ($70^\circ$)** — The exterior angle equals the two remote interior angles: $A+B=112$. With $5k+3k=112$, $k=14$, so the angles are $70^\circ$ and $42^\circ$. ($42$ is the smaller angle; $68=180-112$ is the interior angle at $C$; $112.5$ wrongly makes $A+B=180$.)  *Trick: Exterior angle = sum of the two far interior angles*

5. **B ($2{,}340^\circ$)** — $n=\frac{360}{24}=15$ sides, so the interior sum is $(15-2)(180)=2340$. ($360$ is the exterior sum; $2160$ uses $n-1$; $2700$ uses $15\cdot180$.)  *Trick: Exterior angles sum to $360^\circ$; interior sum $=(n-2)180$*
