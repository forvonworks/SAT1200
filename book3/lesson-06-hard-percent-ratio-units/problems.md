## Answer Key

Each entry: answer, a short worked solution, and the trick used.

### Set 1 — Bridge: the core moves

1. **B ($96\%$)** — Multiply the factors: $1.20\times0.80=0.96$, so the final price is $96\%$ of the original. The $20\%$ drop is taken from the larger new price, so the changes do not cancel. ($100\%$ assumes they cancel; $104\%$ flips the sign of the net change; $80\%$ uses only the decrease.)  *Trick: Multipliers, not addition*

2. **D ($\$120.00$)** — The sale price is $85\%$ of the original: $0.85p=102$, so $p=120$. (\$117.30 multiplies by $1.15$; \$117 adds $15$; \$86.70 takes $85\%$ of \$102 instead of dividing.)  *Trick: Reverse percent: divide by the multiplier*

3. **Ans. 25** — Percent change $=\dfrac{\text{change}}{\text{original}}=\dfrac{1}{4}=25\%$. The rate rose by $1$ \emph{percentage point}, but the rate itself grew by $25\%$.  *Trick: Percent change vs percentage points*

4. **C ($432$)** — $1\ \text{ft}^2=12\times12=144\ \text{in}^2$, so $3\times144=432$. (Choice $36$ multiplies by $12$ only; $144$ forgets the $3$; $1{,}728$ cubes the $12$.)  *Trick: Squared units: square the factor*

5. **C ($24$)** — Let the animals be $3k$ cats and $5k$ dogs. $3k+6=5k$ gives $k=3$: $9$ cats, $15$ dogs, $24$ in all. ($8$ is one ``set'' $3+5$; $15$ is the dogs; $30$ counts the new cats too.)  *Trick: Ratio changes: use $3k$ and $5k$*

6. **B ($27$)** — Plums minus apples is $6k-2k=4k=36$, so $k=9$ and pears are $3k=27$. ($9$ is one part; $18$ is the apples; $54$ is the plums.)  *Trick: Part-to-part ratio: find one part*

7. **A ($168$)** — The $35\%$ applies to car owners, not to everyone: $0.60(800)=480$ owners, then $0.35(480)=168$. ($280$ is $35\%$ of all $800$; $480$ is the number of owners; $760$ adds $60+35=95$ percent.)  *Trick: Percent of a percent: multiply the factors*

8. **C ($\$2{,}315$)** — Each year multiplies by $1.05$: $2000(1.05)^3=2315.25\approx2315$. (\$2,300 is simple interest, $3\times\$100$ added; \$6,300 is $2000\times1.05\times3$; \$2,100 is one year only.)  *Trick: Compound = multiply by $1+r$ each period*

9. **Ans. 20** — $72\ \dfrac{\text{km}}{\text{h}}\cdot\dfrac{1000\ \text{m}}{1\ \text{km}}\cdot\dfrac{1\ \text{h}}{3600\ \text{s}}=\dfrac{72{,}000}{3600}=20$ meters per second. (Shortcut: divide km/h by $3.6$.)  *Trick: Unit conversion chain*

10. **B ($16$)** — Total distance $=24$ km. Total time $=\frac{12}{12}+\frac{12}{24}=1.5$ h. Average speed $=24\div1.5=16$ km/h. ($18$ averages the two speeds, but more time is spent at the slower one; $8$ is product over sum, half the answer; $24$ is the return speed alone.)  *Trick: Average speed = total distance $\div$ total time*

11. **C ($192$)** — Lengths scale by $k=4$, so area scales by $k^2=16$: $12\times16=192$. ($48$ scales area by $4$; $768$ uses $k^3$, the rule for volume; $16$ forgets the $12$.)  *Trick: Scale factor $k$: area scales by $k^2$*

12. **Ans. 2.5** — $150\%$ \emph{more} means $B+1.5B=2.5B$. So A's price is $2.5$ times B's. (``$150\%$ of'' would be $1.5$ times; ``$150\%$ more than'' is $1+1.5$.)  *Trick: ``$n\%$ more'' vs ``$n$ times as much''*

### Set 2 — Percent change: chains, reversals and wording traps

1. **B ($\$300.00$)** — The discounts multiply: $0.80\times0.85=0.68$, so $0.68p=204$ and $p=300$. (Adding the discounts to $35\%$ gives $204\div0.65\approx\$313.85$; \$240 undoes only the coupon; \$275.40 multiplies by $1.35$.)  *Trick: Chain of multipliers, then divide*

2. **A** — Percentage points are the difference of the two rates: $46-40=6$. Percent change compares with the starting rate: $\frac{6}{40}=15\%$. ($13\%$ divides by the new rate, $46$; ``$6\%$'' mixes the two ideas; ``$15$ points'' confuses them.)  *Trick: Percentage points vs percent change*

### Set 3 — Interest, mixtures, weighted averages and tables

1. **A ($P(1.01)^{12t}$)** — Each month multiplies the balance by $1+\frac{0.12}{12}=1.01$, and $t$ years contain $12t$ months, so $B=P(1.01)^{12t}$. (The other choices use the yearly rate $1.12$ once a year, the yearly rate every month, or only $t$ monthly periods.)  *Trick: Per-period rate and number of periods*

2. **Ans. 76.25** — A: $10{,}000(1+3\cdot0.05)=11{,}500$. B: $10{,}000(1.05)^3=11{,}576.25$. Difference $=76.25$.  *Trick: Simple = add $rP$; compound = multiply by $1+r$*

3. **B ($19.6\%$)** — One year is $12$ months: $(1.015)^{12}\approx1.196$, a growth of $19.6\%$. ($18.0\%$ is simple, $12\times1.5$; $119.6\%$ is the new amount as a percent of the old, not the growth; $180\%$ multiplies $1.5\times12$ and slips a decimal.)  *Trick: Monthly growth compounds: $(1+r)^{12}-1$*

4. **D ($3$)** — Acid: $0.10x+0.40(6)=0.30(x+6)$, so $2.4+0.1x=1.8+0.3x$ and $x=3$. (Check: $0.3+2.4=2.7$ in $9$ liters is $30\%$. $6$ assumes equal amounts; $9$ is the final volume; $12$ flips the ratio.)  *Trick: Mixture: acid in = acid out*

5. **Ans. 5** — Salt: $0.35(12)=4.2$ liters never changes. The new volume is $4.2\div0.60=7$ liters, so $12-7=5$ liters evaporate. (Only water leaves; the salt amount is the invariant.)  *Trick: Evaporation: the salt amount stays fixed*

6. **C ($82$)** — Total points: $20(80)+30(70)+50(90)=1600+2100+4500=8200$, and $8200\div100=82$. ($80$ is the unweighted mean of the three means; $79$ pairs the means with the sizes in reverse order; $90$ is the largest section's mean.)  *Trick: Weighted average: use totals*

7. **Ans. 63** — Total points: $30(72)=2160$. Girls: $18(78)=1404$. Boys: $2160-1404=756$ points among $12$ boys, so the mean is $756\div12=63$.  *Trick: Weighted average: subtract the known group's total*

8. **C ($52\%$)** — The group asked about is the night shift: $120+110=230$ employees. Full-time among them: $\frac{120}{230}\approx52\%$. ($24\%$ divides by all $500$; $40\%$ divides by the $300$ full-time employees; $67\%$ is $\frac{120}{180}$, night over day.)  *Trick: Percent of a column: pick the right denominator*

9. **Ans. 0.6** — Let $f$ be the fraction that is Y. Then $0.20(1-f)+0.50f=0.38$, so $0.20+0.30f=0.38$ and $f=0.6$. (Shortcut: $38$ sits $18$ of the $30$ points from $20$ to $50$, and $\frac{18}{30}=0.6$.)  *Trick: Weighted average gives the fraction directly*

10. **A (Line D)** — Percent increases: A $\frac{15}{60}=25\%$, B $\frac{50}{200}=25\%$, C $\frac{45}{150}=30\%$, D $\frac{7}{20}=35\%$. D is greatest. (B has the greatest \emph{dollar} increase, $50$, but only $25\%$; A ties it at $25\%$; C is the runner-up at $30\%$.)  *Trick: Percent change needs the starting value*

11. **D ($\$2{,}479$)** — $P(1.10)^2=3000$, so $P=\frac{3000}{1.21}\approx2479$. (\$2,500 divides by $1.2$, simple interest; \$2,727 divides by $1.1$ once; \$3,630 multiplies instead of dividing.)  *Trick: Reverse compound growth: divide by $(1+r)^t$*

12. **B ($5$)** — Value $=24000(0.85)^t<12000$ means $0.85^t<0.5$. $0.85^4\approx0.522$ is not enough and $0.85^5\approx0.444$ is, so $t=5$. ($3$ is the linear guess $50\div15\approx3.3$ rounded down; $4$ rounds $4.27$ down; $6$ overshoots.)  *Trick: Exponential decay is not linear*

13. **C ($\$5{,}250$)** — Simple interest adds $4\%$ of $P$ each year, so $A=P(1+0.04\cdot5)=1.2P$ and $P=\frac{6300}{1.2}=5250$. (\$5,040 takes $80\%$ of the balance; \$5,178 divides by $1.04^5$, compound; \$6,058 divides by $1.04$ once.)  *Trick: Simple interest: $A=P(1+rt)$*

14. **B ($4$)** — Antifreeze left: $0.15(20-x)$; add $x$ pure: $0.15(20-x)+x=0.32(20)=6.4$, so $3+0.85x=6.4$ and $x=4$. ($3.4$ forgets that the drained liquid carried antifreeze; $5$ adds $x$ liters instead of replacing; $6.4$ is the antifreeze amount, not the volume.)  *Trick: Replace-and-refill: remove antifreeze too*

### Set 4 — Ratios, rates, units and similar figures

### Set 5 — Summit: the hardest mixed problems
