**22 questions — 35 minutes.** Calculator allowed on every question. A reference sheet
(circle/rectangle/triangle area, cylinder/cone/sphere/prism volume, Pythagorean theorem,
30-60-90 and 45-45-90 triangle ratios, and the facts that a circle has 360° and 2π radians)
is available throughout.

For multiple-choice questions, select the one best answer. For student-produced response
("grid-in") questions, you may enter a fraction or a decimal; negative answers are not
possible for any grid-in question in this module.

**4.** The line graph shows the percent of cars for sale at a used car lot on a given day by model year.

*(Line graph: horizontal axis "Model year" from 2010 to 2019; vertical axis "Percent of cars for sale" from 0% to 15%. The line starts at 12% in 2010, stays at 12% through 2012, drops to 8% in 2013, drops further to a low point of 4% in 2014, rises to 9% in 2015, 10% in 2016, 10% in 2017, 11% in 2018, and 11% in 2019.)*

For what model year is the percent of cars for sale the smallest?

A. 2012
B. 2013
C. 2014
D. 2015

**5.** On a car trip, Rhett and Jessica each drove for part of the trip, and the total distance they drove was under 220 miles. Rhett drove at an average speed of 35 miles per hour (mph), and Jessica drove at an average speed of 40 mph. Which of the following inequalities represents this situation, where $r$ is the number of hours Rhett drove and $j$ is the number of hours Jessica drove?

A. $35r + 40j > 220$
B. $35r + 40j < 220$
C. $40r + 35j > 220$
D. $40r + 35j < 220$

**6.** Which of the following expressions is equivalent to $3(x+4) - 2x$ ?

A. $x + 4$
B. $x + 12$
C. $5x + 12$
D. $5x + 4$

**7.** A company opens an account with an initial balance of \$36,100.00. The account earns interest, and no additional deposits or withdrawals are made. The account balance is given by an exponential function $A$, where $A(t)$ is the account balance, in dollars, $t$ years after the account is opened. The account balance after 13 years is \$68,071.93. Which equation could define $A$ ?

A. $A(t) = 36{,}100.00(1.05)^t$
B. $A(t) = 31{,}971.93(1.05)^t$
C. $A(t) = 31{,}971.93(0.05)^t$
D. $A(t) = 36{,}100.00(0.05)^t$
