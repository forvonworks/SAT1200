## Module 1

**1. Javob: C ($x=7$).** $4x-7=21$ tenglamasining ikkala tomoniga $+7$ qo'shamiz: $4x=28$.
Ikkala tomonni $4$ ga bo'lamiz: $x=7$.

**2. Javob: D ($12$).** $f(x)=x^2-3x+2$ ga $x=5$ ni qo'yamiz: $f(5)=25-15+2=12$.

**3. Javob: $84$.** To'rtburchakning yuzi $=$ uzunlik $\times$ eni $=12\times7=84$ kv. sm.

**4. Javob: C ($2014$).** Grafikda eng past nuqta — ya'ni foiz eng kichik bo'lgan yil —
$2014$-yilda, taxminan $4\%$ da joylashgan.

**5. Javob: B.** Rhett $r$ soat $35$ mil/soat tezlikda, Jessica $j$ soat $40$ mil/soat
tezlikda haydagan — demak, masofalar mos ravishda $35r$ va $40j$. Umumiy masofa $220$
mildan **kam** bo'lgani uchun, $35r+40j<220$.

**6. Javob: B ($x+12$).** Qavsni ochamiz: $3(x+4)-2x=3x+12-2x$. O'xshash hadlarni
qo'shamiz: $x+12$.

**7. Javob: A.** Boshlang'ich balans $N=36{,}100.00$ — bu $A(t)=Nr^t$ dagi $N$. $13$
yildan keyin $A(13)=68{,}071.93$, bundan $r\approx1.05$ (o'sish koeffitsienti). Demak,
$A(t)=36{,}100.00(1.05)^t$.

**8. Javob: $5$.** $3x+5\le20$ ni yechamiz: $3x\le15$, $x\le5$. Eng katta butun qiymat
— $5$.

**9. Javob: B ($x=5$).** Ikkala tenglamani qo'shamiz: $(x+2y)+(x-2y)=6+4$, ya'ni
$2x=10$, demak $x=5$.

**10. Javob: B ($35$).** $x=3$ ni $y=4(2)^x+3$ ga qo'yamiz: $y=4(2)^3+3=4(8)+3=32+3=35$.

**11. Javob: A.** $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$ ko'rinishida, markaz $(h,k)$ va radius $r$.
Berilgan tenglamada $h=13$, $r^2=64$, demak $r=8$ (radius manfiy bo'lmaydi). Markaz:
$(13,k)$, radius: $8$.

**12. Javob: D ($10$).** $y=-2x+b$ to'g'ri chizig'i $(3,4)$ nuqtadan o'tadi: $4=-2(3)+b$,
$4=-6+b$, $b=10$.

**13. Javob: $0.5$ (yoki $\frac12$).** O'rtacha o'zgarish tezligi
$=\dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}=\dfrac{8-6}{6-2}=\dfrac{2}{4}=\dfrac12$.

**14. Javob: $7$, $8$, yoki $13$ (har qanday biri qabul qilinadi).** Tenglama ikkita
butun yechimga ega bo'lishi uchun, $x^2-ax+12$ ifodasi $(x+c)(x+d)$ ko'rinishida
faktorlanishi kerak, bunda $cd=12$ va $c+d=-a$ (demak $a=-(c+d)$, $a>0$ bo'lgani uchun
$c+d<0$). Mos juftliklar: $(-4,-3)\to a=7$; $(-6,-2)\to a=8$; $(-12,-1)\to a=13$.

**15. Javob: C.** $QR=RS=x$, $SQ=y$ bo'lgani uchun, uchburchakning uch tomoni
yig'indisi $=x+x+y=2x+y=37$. Demak, $37$ — uch tomonning yig'indisi.

**16. Javob: $4$.** Ikkala tenglamani qo'shamiz: $(2x+y)+(x-y)=11+1$, ya'ni $3x=12$,
demak $x=4$.

**17. Javob: A ($-7$).** $x^2=49$ ning ikkita yechimi bor: $x=7$ va $x=-7$. Shart
bo'yicha $x<0$, demak $x=-7$.

**18. Javob: B ($12x$).** Qavslarni ochamiz: $(x+3)^2=x^2+6x+9$,
$(x-3)^2=x^2-6x+9$. Ayirma: $(x^2+6x+9)-(x^2-6x+9)=12x$.

**19. Javob: C ($90°$).** Uchburchak burchaklari yig'indisi $180°$: $x+2x+3x=180$,
$6x=180$, $x=30$. Eng katta burchak $=3x=90°$.

**20. Javob: C ($2x+20$).** $x$ — Experiment A stansiyalari soni, $5-x$ — Experiment B
stansiyalari soni. Umumiy tuz: $6x+4(5-x)=6x+20-4x=2x+20$.

**21. Javob: C.** Ikkala tomonni $2$ ga bo'lamiz: $\dfrac{7x}{7y}=\sqrt{w+19}$, ya'ni
$\dfrac{x}{y}=\sqrt{w+19}$. Hamma o'zgaruvchilar musbat bo'lgani uchun, ikkala tomonni
kvadratga ko'taramiz: $\left(\dfrac{x}{y}\right)^2=w+19$. Demak,
$w=\left(\dfrac{x}{y}\right)^2-19$.

**22. Javob: $\frac{5}{12}$.** Ikkala tilni o'rganuvchilar soni $=50$. Ispan tilini
o'rganuvchilar soni $=120$. Shartli ehtimollik $=\dfrac{\text{ikkalasi}}{\text{Ispan}}
=\dfrac{50}{120}=\dfrac{5}{12}$.

## Module 2

**1. Javob: B ($10\pi$).** Doira uzunligi $=2\pi r=2\pi(5)=10\pi$.

**2. Javob: B ($\$30$).** $25\%$ chegirma — narxning $75\%$ i qoladi: $40\times0.75=30$.

**3. Javob: C.** $C=60h+45$ da, $60h$ — soatlar soniga bog'liq qism (o'zgaruvchi), $45$
esa $h=0$ bo'lsa ham qoladigan qism — ya'ni, soatlar sonidan qat'i nazar to'lanadigan
doimiy to'lov.

**4. Javob: A.** Bu — kvadratlar ayirmasi formulasi: $a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b)$. Bu yerda
$a=2x$, $b=5$: $4x^2-25=(2x-5)(2x+5)$.

**5. Javob: B ($12$).** Pifagor teoremasi: $9^2+b^2=15^2$, $81+b^2=225$, $b^2=144$,
$b=12$. (Bu $9$-$12$-$15$ — $3$-$4$-$5$ uchburchagining $3$ marta kattalashtirilgan
versiyasi.)

**6. Javob: B ($4$).** Birinchi tenglamani $3$ ga bo'lamiz: $x+2y=4$. Ikkinchi
tenglama bilan cheksiz ko'p yechimga ega bo'lishi uchun, ular bir xil chiziq bo'lishi
kerak — demak $k=4$.

**7. Javob: C.** Populyatsiya har $3$ soatda ikki baravar ortadi — demak, $t$ soatdan
keyin u $2^{t/3}$ marta ortgan. Boshlang'ich qiymat $200$ bilan: $P(t)=200(2)^{t/3}$.

**8. Javob: $30$.** Nisbat $5:2$ (un:shakar). Shakar $12$ stakan bo'lsa, masshtab
koeffitsienti $=12\div2=6$. Un $=5\times6=30$ stakan.

**9. Javob: B ($11$).** $80-5m=25$, $5m=55$, $m=11$.

**10. Javob: C ($9$).** $h(x)=-(x-4)^2+9$ — bu uchburchak funksiya bo'lib, maksimal
qiymati $x=4$ da, ya'ni $h(4)=-(0)^2+9=9$.

**11. Javob: $7$.** $x^2-5x-14=0$ ni faktorlaymiz: $(x-7)(x+2)=0$, demak $x=7$ yoki
$x=-2$. Shart bo'yicha $x>0$, demak $x=7$.

**12. Javob: A.** Foyda $=$ daromad $-$ xarajat $=12t-400$. Foyda $0$ dan katta bo'lishi
uchun: $12t-400>0$.

**13. Javob: D.** Birinchi kun $\$270$, qolgan $x-1$ kun har biri $\$135$:
$y=270+135(x-1)=270+135x-135=135x+135$.

**14. Javob: $-4.9$ (yoki $-\frac{49}{10}$).** Avval $f(-4)$ ni topamiz:
$f(-4)=3+|-2(-4)-(-4)^2|=3+|8-16|=3+8=11$. Demak $c=11$. Endi $g(11)$ ni hisoblaymiz:
$g(11)=\left|\dfrac{-11}{11-1}\right|-11+5=|-1.1|-6=1.1-6=-4.9$.

**15. Javob: D.** $\sin p=\cos t$ bo'lgani uchun, $p+t=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ (komplementar
burchaklar munosabati), demak $p=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-t$. $g(0)=p(8)^0+6t=p(1)+6t=p+6t$.
$p=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-t$ ni qo'yamiz: $g(0)=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-t+6t=5t+\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

**16. Javob: $4176$.** Kichik kvadrat tomoni $=s$ bo'lsin, katta kvadrat tomoni $=3s$,
demak yuzalar nisbati $1:9$. Umumiy yuza $=s^2+9s^2=10s^2$. Oqim $=$ kuch $\times$ yuza:
$29(10s^2)=4640$, $s^2=16$. Katta kvadrat yuzasi $=9(16)=144$. Katta kvadratdagi oqim
$=29\times144=4176$.

**17. Javob: $12$.** $x+y=20$ va $3x-y=28$ (bu yerda $x$ — katta son). Ikkala
tenglamani qo'shamiz: $4x=48$, $x=12$ (va $y=8$).

**18. Javob: A.** Chiziqning qiyaligi $=\dfrac{17-5}{6-2}=\dfrac{12}{4}=3$. Perpendikulyar
chiziqning qiyaligi — teskari va qarama-qarshi ishorali: $-\dfrac13$.

**19. Javob: B ($2$).** Ikkinchi tenglamani $2$ ga bo'lamiz: $2x+6y=9$. Birinchi
tenglama $kx+6y=9$ bilan bir xil chiziq bo'lishi uchun, $k=2$ bo'lishi kerak.

**20. Javob: $58$.** $(x+y)^2=x^2+2xy+y^2$. $10^2=x^2+2(21)+y^2$, $100=x^2+y^2+42$,
demak $x^2+y^2=58$.

**21. Javob: A ($3$).** Ko'ndalang ko'paytiramiz: $(x-3)(2x-1)=(x-3)(x+2)$. Ikkala
tomondan $(x-3)(x+2)$ ni ayiramiz: $(x-3)[(2x-1)-(x+2)]=0$, ya'ni $(x-3)(x-3)=0$.
Demak $x=3$ (bu qiymat taqiqlangan $x=-2$ va $x=\frac12$ dan farq qiladi, shuning
uchun yaroqli).

**22. Javob: A.** $f$ ning uchi $(2,-5)$. $g(x)=f(x-3)+4$ — bu $f$ ni $3$ birlik
o'ngga va $4$ birlik yuqoriga surish. Yangi uch: $(2+3,\,-5+4)=(5,-1)$.
