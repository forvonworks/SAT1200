# Mock Test 6 — Module 2

**22 questions — 35 minutes.** Calculator allowed on every question. A reference sheet
(circle/rectangle/triangle area, cylinder/cone/sphere/prism volume, Pythagorean theorem,
30-60-90 and 45-45-90 triangle ratios, and the facts that a circle has 360° and 2π radians)
is available throughout.

For multiple-choice questions, select the one best answer. For student-produced response
("grid-in") questions, you may enter a fraction or a decimal; negative answers are not
possible for any grid-in question in this module.

---

**1.** A circle has a radius of 5 units. What is its circumference, in terms of $\pi$ ?

A. $5\pi$
B. $10\pi$
C. $20\pi$
D. $25\pi$

**2.** A shirt originally priced at \$40 is on sale for 25% off. What is the sale price, in dollars, of the shirt?

A. $28
B. $30
C. $32
D. $35

**3.** A plumber charges a \$45 service fee plus \$60 per hour for labor. The equation $C = 60h + 45$ gives the total cost $C$, in dollars, for $h$ hours of work. What does 45 represent in this context?

A. The total cost of 1 hour of labor
B. The number of hours of labor
C. The flat fee charged regardless of how many hours of labor are performed
D. The hourly labor rate, in dollars

**4.** Which of the following expressions is equivalent to $4x^2 - 25$ ?

A. $(2x-5)(2x+5)$
B. $(4x-5)(x+5)$
C. $(2x-25)(2x+1)$
D. $(x-5)(4x+5)$

**5.** In a right triangle, one leg has a length of 9 and the hypotenuse has a length of 15. What is the length of the other leg?

A. 6
B. 12
C. 18
D. $\sqrt{106}$

**6.**
$$3x + 6y = 12$$
$$x + 2y = k$$

For what value of the constant $k$ does the system of equations shown have infinitely many solutions?

A. 2
B. 4
C. 6
D. 12

**7.** A population of bacteria doubles every 3 hours. If the population starts at 200, which function $P$ gives the population after $t$ hours?

A. $P(t) = 200(2)^{t}$
B. $P(t) = 200(2)^{3t}$
C. $P(t) = 200(2)^{t/3}$
D. $P(t) = 200(3)^{t/2}$

**8.** A recipe calls for flour and sugar in a ratio of 5 to 2. If a baker uses 12 cups of sugar, how many cups of flour are needed?

**9.** A tank starts with 80 liters of water and drains at a constant rate of 5 liters per minute. After how many minutes will the tank contain 25 liters of water?

A. 5
B. 11
C. 15
D. 16

**10.** The function $h$ is defined by $h(x) = -(x-4)^2 + 9$. What is the maximum value of $h$ ?

A. $-4$
B. $4$
C. $9$
D. $13$

**11.** If $x^2 - 5x - 14 = 0$ and $x > 0$, what is the value of $x$ ?

**12.** A theater sells tickets for \$12 each. The theater's expenses for one night are \$400. Which inequality represents the number of tickets $t$ the theater must sell for the theater's profit to be greater than \$0 ?

A. $12t - 400 > 0$
B. $12t + 400 > 0$
C. $400t - 12 > 0$
D. $12t - 400 < 0$

**13.** The cost of renting a backhoe for up to 10 days is \$270 for the first day and \$135 for each additional day. Which of the following equations gives the cost $y$, in dollars, of renting the backhoe for $x$ days, where $x$ is a positive integer and $x \le 10$ ?

A. $y = 270x - 135$
B. $y = 270x + 135$
C. $y = 135x + 270$
D. $y = 135x + 135$

**14.** The functions $f$ and $g$ are defined by the given equations.
$$f(x) = 3 + |{-2x - x^2}|$$
$$g(w) = \left|\dfrac{-w}{w-1}\right| - w + 5$$

If $f(-4) = c$, where $c$ is a constant, what is the value of $g(c)$ ?

**15.** Acute angle $P$ has a measure of $p$ radians, and acute angle $T$ has a measure of $t$ radians. The function $g$ is defined by $g(x) = p(8)^x + 6t$. If $\sin p = \cos t$, which of the following represents the value of $g(0)$ in terms of $t$ ?

A. $6t + \pi$
B. $6t$
C. $5t + \pi$
D. $5t + \dfrac{\pi}{2}$

**16.** For an electric field passing through a flat surface perpendicular to it, the electric flux of the electric field through the surface is the product of the electric field's strength and the area of the surface. A certain flat surface consists of two adjacent squares, where the side length, in meters, of the larger square is 3 times the side length, in meters, of the smaller square. An electric field with strength 29.00 volts per meter passes uniformly through this surface, which is perpendicular to the electric field. If the total electric flux of the electric field through this surface is 4,640 volts $\cdot$ meters, what is the electric flux, in volts $\cdot$ meters, of the electric field through the larger square?

**17.** Twice the sum of two numbers is 40. The difference between three times the larger number and the smaller number is 28. What is the larger of the two numbers?

**18.** A line in the $xy$-plane passes through the points $(2, 5)$ and $(6, 17)$. What is the slope of a line that is perpendicular to this line?

A. $-\dfrac{1}{3}$
B. $3$
C. $\dfrac{1}{3}$
D. $-3$

**19.**
$$kx + 6y = 9$$
$$4x + 12y = 18$$

For what value of the constant $k$ does the system of equations shown have infinitely many solutions?

A. 1
B. 2
C. 4
D. 9

**20.** If $x + y = 10$ and $xy = 21$, what is the value of $x^2 + y^2$ ?

**21.** What is the solution to the equation $\dfrac{x-3}{x+2} = \dfrac{x-3}{2x-1}$, where $x \ne -2$ and $x \ne \dfrac{1}{2}$ ?

A. $3$
B. $-2$
C. $\dfrac{1}{2}$
D. $0$

**22.** The graph of $y = f(x)$ is a parabola with vertex $(2, -5)$. If $g(x) = f(x-3) + 4$, what are the coordinates of the vertex of the graph of $g$ ?

A. $(5, -1)$
B. $(-1, 5)$
C. $(5, -9)$
D. $(-1, -9)$
