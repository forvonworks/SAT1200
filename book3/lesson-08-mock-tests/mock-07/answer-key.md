## Module 1

**1. Javob: B — $(3, 6)$**
$r=3$ berilgan. Birinchi tenglamaga qo'yamiz: $s+7(3)=27$, ya'ni $s+21=27$. Ikkala
tomondan $21$ ni ayirsak: $s=6$. Demak, $(r,s)=(3,6)$.
*(Substitute $r=3$ into $s+7r=27$: $s=6$. Solution is $(3,6)$.)*

**2. Javob: B — $30$**
Ikkala tomonni $25$ ga ko'paytiramiz: $x^2=900$. Kvadrat ildiz olsak, $x=30$ yoki
$x=-30$; musbat yechim so'ralgani uchun $x=30$.
*(Multiply both sides by 25: $x^2=900$, so $x=\pm30$; take the positive root.)*

**3. Javob: C — $4^2+b^2=19^2$**
Pifagor teoremasiga ko'ra, to'g'ri burchakli uchburchakda ikki katetning kvadratlari
yig'indisi gipotenuza kvadratiga teng: $a^2+b^2=c^2$. Bu yerda katetlar $4$ va $b$,
gipotenuza $19$.
*(Pythagorean theorem: leg² + leg² = hypotenuse².)*

**4. Javob: D — $\dfrac{10}{70}$**
Ehtimollik = qulay natijalar soni ÷ jami natijalar soni. Igneous jinslar soni $10$,
jami $70$ ta jins bor: $\dfrac{10}{70}$.
*(Probability = favorable ÷ total = 10/70.)*

**5. Javob: A — $6$**
$5x+9=39$. Ikkala tomondan $9$ ni ayiramiz: $5x=30$. Ikkala tomonni $5$ ga bo'lamiz:
$x=6$.
*(Subtract 9, divide by 5: $x=6$.)*

**6. Javob: A — $18$**
$f(x)=2x^2$ da $x=3$ qo'yamiz: $f(3)=2(3)^2=2(9)=18$.
*(Substitute $x=3$: $f(3)=18$.)*

**7. Javob: A — $C=4+2.5m$**
Boshlang'ich to'lov ($4$) o'zgarmaydi, har bir milya uchun $2.5$ qo'shiladi: jami
narx = boshlang'ich + (narx × milya soni).
*(Base fare plus per-mile rate times miles.)*

**8. Javob: A — $x+12$**
Qavsni ochamiz: $3(x+4)=3x+12$. Ifoda: $3x+12-2x=x+12$.
*(Distribute then combine like terms: $x+12$.)*

**9. Javob: A — $y=5x+6$**
Ikki nuqta $(1,11)$ va $(2,16)$ orqali qiyalik: $m=\dfrac{16-11}{2-1}=5$. $y=5x+b$ ga
$(1,11)$ ni qo'yamiz: $11=5(1)+b$, demak $b=6$. Tekshirish: $x=3$ da $y=5(3)+6=21$ —
jadvalga mos keladi.
*(Slope from two points = 5; solve for $b=6$; verify against the third table value.)*

**10. Javob: A — $9$**
Uzunlik $=2w$ (eni ikki marta), yuza $=162=2w\cdot w=2w^2$. $w^2=81$, $w=9$ (musbat
qiymat, chunki eni manfiy bo'lishi mumkin emas).
*(Area $=2w^2=162 \Rightarrow w^2=81 \Rightarrow w=9$, positive root only.)*

**11. Javob: A — $6$**
$g(5)=5^2-4(5)+1=25-20+1=6$.
*(Substitute $x=5$: $g(5)=6$.)*

**12. Javob: A — $15$**
Pifagor teoremasi: $\sqrt{9^2+12^2}=\sqrt{81+144}=\sqrt{225}=15$. Bu — mashhur
3-4-5 uchburchakning $3\times$ kattalashtirilgani ($9=3\times3$, $12=3\times4$,
$15=3\times5$).
*(This is a scaled 3-4-5 Pythagorean triple: hypotenuse $=15$.)*

**13. Javob: A — $2$**
Qiyalik $=\dfrac{13-5}{6-2}=\dfrac{8}{4}=2$.
*(Slope $=\dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}=2$.)*

**14. Javob: A — $7$**

*(Factors to $(x-7)(x+2)$; $x=7$ is the listed choice.)*

**15. Javob: $9$**

**16. Javob: $5$**

**17. Javob: $7$**
Ikkinchi tenglamadan $x=y+1$. Birinchiga qo'yamiz: $2(y+1)+y=11$, $3y+2=11$,
$y=3$, $x=4$. $x+y=4+3=7$.
*(Solve the system: $x=4$, $y=3$, so $x+y=7$.)*

**18. Javob: A**
Namuna ehtimoliy xulosasi: $62\%\pm4$ foiz xato chegarasi degani — haqiqiy populatsiya
qiymati taxminan $58\%$ dan $66\%$ gacha oraliqda ekanligiga ishonch bilan aytish
mumkin, aniq $62\%$ emas va chegaradan tashqari qat'iy xulosa ham emas.
*(A margin of error gives a plausible RANGE for the population value — $58\%$ to
$66\%$ — not a single exact figure (B) and not a claim beyond that range (C, D).)*

**19. Javob: B — $48\pi$**
$\overline{PR}$ va $\overline{QS}$ diametr bo'lgani uchun $\angle SOP$ va $\angle ROQ$
vertikal burchaklar — demak yoy $PS$ va yoy $QR$ teng. Xuddi shunday, yoy $SR$ =
yoy $PQ=x$ deylik. $PS=2x$ (berilgan), demak $QR=2x$ ham. Yig'indi: $x+x+2x+2x=6x=144\pi$,
$x=24\pi$. $QR=2(24\pi)=48\pi$.
*(Vertical angles make arc $PS$ = arc $QR$ and arc $SR$ = arc $PQ$; solving
$6x=144\pi$ gives $x=24\pi$, so arc $QR=48\pi$.)*

**20. Javob: D — $y=9.4-0.9x$**
Nuqtalar pasayish tendentsiyasini ko'rsatadi ($x$ oshganda $y$ kamayadi) — demak
qiyalik manfiy. $x=0$ yaqinida $y>9$ — demak $y$-kesishma $9$ dan katta. Faqat D
varianti ikkala shartni ham qondiradi: qiyalik $-0.9$ (manfiy), $y$-kesishma $9.4$.
*(Negative slope + $y$-intercept above 9 → only D fits both conditions.)*

**21. Javob: $\dfrac{1}{2}$**
$4y+8x=6$ ni $y=mx+b$ ko'rinishiga keltiramiz: $y=-2x+\dfrac{3}{2}$, qiyalik $-2$.
Perpendikulyar chiziqning qiyaligi — teskari va qarama-qarshi ishorali son:
$-\dfrac{1}{-2}=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
*(Line $p$'s slope is $-2$; perpendicular slope is the negative reciprocal,
$\dfrac{1}{2}$.)*

**22. Javob: $-24$**
$(0,-6)$ nuqtasidan: $c=-6$. $(-1,-8)$ nuqtasidan: $-8=2(1)+b(-1)-6$, ya'ni
$-8=-4-b$, demak $b=4$. $bc=(4)(-6)=-24$.
*(From $(0,-6)$: $c=-6$. From $(-1,-8)$: $b=4$. So $bc=-24$.)*

## Module 2

**1. Javob: A — $\dfrac{2}{5}$**
Qizil sharlar $8$, jami sharlar $8+12=20$. Ehtimollik $=\dfrac{8}{20}=\dfrac{2}{5}$.
*(Probability $=8/20=2/5$.)*

**2. Javob: A — $36\pi$**
Doira yuzasi $=\pi r^2=\pi(6)^2=36\pi$.
*(Circle area $=\pi r^2=36\pi$.)*

**3. Javob: $7$**
Ikkinchi tenglamani birinchidan ayiramiz: $(5x+3y)-(x+3y)=38-10$, ya'ni $4x=28$,
$x=7$.
*(Subtract the equations to eliminate $y$: $4x=28 \Rightarrow x=7$.)*

**4. Javob: D — $n=\dfrac{16}{p-20}$**
$p=20+\dfrac{16}{n}$ dan $n$ ni ifodalaymiz: ikkala tomondan $20$ ni ayiramiz,
$p-20=\dfrac{16}{n}$. Ikkala tomonni $n$ ga ko'paytirib, so'ng $(p-20)$ ga
bo'lamiz: $n=\dfrac{16}{p-20}$.
*(Subtract 20, then solve for $n$: $n=16/(p-20)$.)*

**5. Javob: A — $11$**
Qavsni ochamiz: $4x-12=2x+10$. $2x$ ni ikkala tomondan ayirib, $12$ ni qo'shamiz:
$2x=22$, $x=11$.
*(Distribute, collect terms: $x=11$.)*

**6. Javob: A — $3$**
$f(-3)=(-3)^2+2(-3)=9-6=3$.
*(Substitute $x=-3$: $f(-3)=3$.)*

**7. Javob: A — $10$**
30-60-90 uchburchakda, $30°$ ga qarama-qarshi tomon — eng kichik tomon ($x$),
gipotenuza esa $2x$. $x=5$ bo'lgani uchun gipotenuza $=10$.
*(In a 30-60-90 triangle, the side opposite 30° is half the hypotenuse:
hypotenuse $=2(5)=10$.)*

**8. Javob: A — $10$**
$5$ ta sonning yig'indisi $=5\times18=90$. $4$ ta sonning yig'indisi $=4\times20=80$.
Olib tashlangan son $=90-80=10$.
*(Sum of 5 numbers $=90$; sum of remaining 4 $=80$; removed number $=10$.)*

**9. Javob: A — $7$**
$y=-3x+b$ tenglamasiga $(2,1)$ ni qo'yamiz: $1=-3(2)+b=-6+b$, demak $b=7$.
*(Substitute the point into $y=mx+b$: $b=7$.)*

**10. Javob: $1.5$**
$125=5^3$, demak $5^{2x}=5^3$ dan $2x=3$, $x=1.5$.
*(Since $125=5^3$: $2x=3 \Rightarrow x=1.5$.)*

**11. Javob: $25$**
Ketma-ket sonlar: $n,(n+1),(n+2)$. Yig'indi $=3n+3=72$, $n=23$. Eng kattasi
$=n+2=25$.
*(Three consecutive integers sum to 72: smallest is 23, largest is $23+2=25$.)*

**12. Javob: A — $x^2-16$**
$(x+4)(x-4)$ — kvadratlar ayirmasi formulasi: $x^2-4^2=x^2-16$.
*(Difference of squares: $(x+4)(x-4)=x^2-16$.)*

**13. Javob: B — $p=5x$**
Har bir porsiya kunlik normaning $5\%$ ini beradi, demak $x$ porsiya $5x$ foizni
beradi — bu chiziqli (ko'rsatkichli emas) munosabat.
*(Each serving gives 5%, so $x$ servings give $5x$ percent — a linear, not
exponential, relationship.)*

**14. Javob: C — $(10, 2)$**
Markazdan tegish nuqtasigacha bo'lgan radius qiyaligi: $\dfrac{-4-1}{5-(-1)}=-\dfrac{5}{6}$.
Tegish chizig'i bu radiusga perpendikulyar, demak uning qiyaligi $\dfrac{6}{5}$.
$(10,2)$ va $(5,-4)$ orasidagi qiyalik: $\dfrac{-4-2}{5-10}=\dfrac{-6}{-5}=\dfrac{6}{5}$
— mos keladi.
*(Radius slope $=-5/6$; tangent slope (perpendicular) $=6/5$; checking $(10,2)$
confirms the same slope.)*

**15. Javob: $20$**
Ikkala tenglamani qo'shamiz: $5y-5y=10x+5x+11-21$, ya'ni $0=15x-10$, $x=\dfrac{2}{3}$.
$30x=30\times\dfrac{2}{3}=20$.
*(Adding the equations eliminates $y$: $x=2/3$, so $30x=20$.)*

**16. Javob: D — Neither I nor II**
$x=0$ qo'ysak: I tenglamada $p(0)=a+b$ — bu $a$ yoki $b$ ning o'zi emas, ularning
yig'indisi, demak koeffitsient/konstanta sifatida ko'rsatilmagan. II tenglamada
$r(0)=a(4.1)^b$ — bu ham $a$ yoki $b$ ga teng emas (chunki $b<0$ bo'lgani uchun
$(4.1)^b\ne1$). Demak, ikkala tenglama ham $y$-kesishmani konstanta/koeffitsient
sifatida ko'rsatmaydi.
*(Setting $x=0$ in each: neither $a+b$ nor $a(4.1)^b$ appears as a bare constant
or coefficient in its equation.)*
