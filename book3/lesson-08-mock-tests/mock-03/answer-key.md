## Module 1

**1. Javob: B (18).** 43 kitobdan 7 tasi ortib qolsa, demak 43−7=36 ta kitob
tarqatilgan. Har bir bolaga 2 tadan berilgani uchun, bolalar soni 36÷2=18.
*(Subtract the leftover from the total: 43−7=36 books distributed; divide by 2 per
child: 18 children.)*

**2. Javob: D ($r^3+6r^2+8r+19$).** O'xshash hadlarni guruhlaymiz: $r^3+(5r^2+r^2)+8r+(7+12)$,
bundan $r^3+6r^2+8r+19$.
*(Group like terms: $r^3+(5r^2+r^2)+8r+(7+12)=r^3+6r^2+8r+19$.)*

**3. Javob: B (39).** Uchburchak burchaklari yig'indisi $180°$; to'g'ri burchak $90°$ ni
oladi, qolgan ikki o'tkir burchak yig'indisi $90°$. Demak ikkinchi burchak $90-51=39°$.

**4. Javob: A (\$4.15 va \$4.31 orasida).** Ishonchli oraliq $4.23\pm0.08$, ya'ni
$4.23-0.08=4.15$ dan $4.23+0.08=4.31$ gacha.
*(The plausible range is the estimate ± margin of error: 4.23−0.08 to 4.23+0.08.)*

**5. Javob: C (8).** Perimetr $=2(l+w)=36$, $l=10$ bo'lgani uchun $2(10+w)=36$,
$10+w=18$, $w=8$.

**6. Javob: A ($2x^2-5x-12$).** $(2x+3)(x-4)=2x^2-8x+3x-12=2x^2-5x-12$.

**7. Javob: B (5).** $5x-3=2x+12$, bundan $3x=15$, $x=5$.

**8. Javob: 12.** $f(5)=5^2-3(5)+2=25-15+2=12$.

**9. Javob: A.** Ikki chiziq tenglamasi $y=6x+3$ va $y=6x+9$ bir xil burchak
koeffitsientiga ($m=6$) ega, lekin $y$-kesish nuqtalari har xil ($3\ne9$) — demak
parallel va kesishmaydi, yechim yo'q.
*(Same slope (6), different y-intercepts (3 vs. 9) → parallel lines, no solution.)*

**10. Javob: D ($(x+4)^{1/2}$).** $\sqrt[4]{x^2+8x+16}=(x^2+8x+16)^{1/4}$. Trinom
$(x+4)^2$ ko'rinishida faktorlanadi, demak ifoda $((x+4)^2)^{1/4}=(x+4)^{1/2}$.

**11. Javob: C (15).** $9$-$12$-$15$ — Pifagor uchligi: $9^2+12^2=81+144=225=15^2$.

**12. Javob: A (0.60).** Yaqinlashuv chizig'i $(0,3.2)$ va $(2,4.4)$ nuqtalaridan
o'tadi, nishabligi $\dfrac{4.4-3.2}{2-0}=\dfrac{1.2}{2}=0.6$.

**13. Javob: B (4).** Tenglamalarni qo'shamiz: $(2x+y)+(x-y)=11+1$, $3x=12$, $x=4$.

**14. Javob: C ($x=8$ va $x=-3$).** $x^2-5x-24=(x-8)(x+3)$, bundan $x=8$ yoki $x=-3$.

**15. Javob: 90.** Silindr hajmi $V=\pi r^2h=\pi(3^2)(10)=90\pi$, demak $k=90$.

**16. Javob: 2.** Nishablik $=\dfrac{13-5}{6-2}=\dfrac{8}{4}=2$.

**17. Javob: A ($(x-3)^2+4$).** 3 birlik o'ngga siljish $x$ o'rniga $(x-3)$ qo'yishni,
4 birlik yuqoriga siljish esa oxiriga $+4$ qo'shishni bildiradi.

**18. Javob: B ($x\ge-5$).** $-3x+7\le22$, bundan $-3x\le15$. Manfiy songa bo'lganda
tengsizlik belgisi teskari bo'ladi: $x\ge-5$.
*(Dividing by a negative flips the inequality direction.)*

**19. Javob: 1.** $(x+6)^2=49$, bundan $x+6=\pm7$. $x>0$ shartiga ko'ra $x+6=7$,
$x=1$ (chunki $x+6=-7$ dan $x=-13<0$ chiqadi, shart buziladi).

**20. Javob: B (1,304).** Kruz uchun $6424\times\frac{483}{803}=3864$ ovoz kutiladi;
qolgan $6424-3864=2560$ ovoz Smitga. Farq: $3864-2560=1304$.

**21. Javob: A.** To'rtinchi toq son $x+6$, birinchi va uchinchisi $x$ va $x+4$. Shart:
$12(x+6)\le x+(x+4)-26$.

**22. Javob: 5.** $a=\dfrac{v_f-12}{5}$ ni $5$ ga ko'paytiramiz: $5a=v_f-12$, bundan
$v_f=5a+12$. Demak $x=5$ (va $y=12$).

## Module 2

**1. Javob: 46.** $x+10=56$, bundan $x=46$.

**2. Javob: C (42).** $\dfrac{42}{x}=7x$ ni $x$ ga ko'paytiramiz: $42=7x^2$. Demak
$7x^2=42$ to'g'ridan-to'g'ri javob (qo'shimcha hisoblash shart emas).

**3. Javob: C (30).** $24\times1.25=30$.

**4. Javob: D (115).** Yondosh burchaklar yig'indisi $180°$: $180-65=115$.

**5. Javob: C (15).** $f(-3)=-2(-3)+9=6+9=15$.

**6. Javob: C (36).** $h(2)=3^2=9$, $h(3)=3^3=27$, yig'indi $9+27=36$.

**7. Javob: B (22).** Jami yig'indi $20\times8=160$; $38$ qo'shilgach $160+38=198$;
yangi o'rtacha $198\div9=22$.

**8. Javob: B.** Markaz $(h,k)=(-8,6)$, radius $5$: $(x-(-8))^2+(y-6)^2=5^2$, ya'ni
$(x+8)^2+(y-6)^2=25$.

**9. Javob: 4.** Tenglamalarni qo'shamiz: $6x=24$, $x=4$.

**10. Javob: A ($-10x+50$).** $(x-5)^2=x^2-10x+25$; $(x-5)(x+5)=x^2-25$. Ayirma:
$(x^2-10x+25)-(x^2-25)=-10x+50$.

**11. Javob: C (2).** $y=4x+b$, nuqta $(1,6)$: $6=4(1)+b$, $b=2$.

**12. Javob: 4.** $2^{x+1}=32=2^5$, bundan $x+1=5$, $x=4$.

**13. Javob: C (22).** Birinchi $10$ soatda $\$80$ topadi. $x$ — qolgan soatlar. Jami
daromad $10x+80$; $90\%$ini tejaydi: $0.9(10x+80)\ge270$. $10x+80=10(x+8)$, demak
$9(x+8)\ge270$, $x+8\ge30$, $x\ge22$.

**14. Javob: -7.** $f(x)=(x-h)^2+k$ ko'rinishida $x=h$ da minimumga erishiladi; bu yerda
$h=-7$ (chunki $(x+7)=(x-(-7))$).

**15. Javob: B.** Daromad $x+0.11s$; shart $3x\le x+0.11s\le4x$. $x$ ni ayiramiz:
$2x\le0.11s\le3x$; $0.11$ ga bo'lamiz: $\dfrac{2}{0.11}x\le s\le\dfrac{3}{0.11}x$.

**16. Javob: A.** $1$ yilda $4$ chorak yil bor, demak $q=4t$, ya'ni $t=q/4$. $M=1800(1.02)^t$
ga qo'yib, $M=1800(1.02)^{q/4}$.

**17. Javob: A (83.75).** Jami ball: $25\times80+15\times90=2000+1350=3350$. Jami
o'quvchi: $40$. O'rtacha: $3350\div40=83.75$. *(Oddiy $(80+90)/2=85$ xato — guruhlar
teng kattalikda emas, shuning uchun og'irlikli o'rtacha kerak.)*
*(Averaging the two means directly (85) is wrong — the groups are different sizes, so a
weighted average is required.)*

**18. Javob: 123.** $\triangle SQX$ da: $\angle QSX=180-48-86=46°$. $\angle SWU=85°$
bo'lgani uchun $\angle SWR=180-85=95°$ (yondosh burchaklar). $\triangle RSW$ da
$\angle RSW=\angle QSX=46°$ (vertikal burchak), demak $\angle WRS=180-46-95=39°$.
$\angle VTU=162°$ bo'lgani uchun $\angle STU=180-162=18°$. $\triangle RTU$ da
$\angle URT=\angle WRS=39°$ (vertikal burchak), demak $\angle TUR=180-39-18=123°$.

**19. Javob: C (4).** $6x+9y=12$ ni $3$ ga bo'lsak $2x+3y=4$ chiqadi — bu ikkinchi
tenglama bilan bir xil bo'lishi uchun $k=4$ bo'lishi kerak.

**20. Javob: 10.** $5+3(h-1)\le32$, bundan $3(h-1)\le27$, $h-1\le9$, $h\le10$. Eng
ko'pi — $10$ soat.

**21. Javob: 25.** Bitta yechim uchun diskriminant nolga teng: $(-10)^2-4(1)(k)=0$,
$100-4k=0$, $k=25$.

**22. Javob: C (2).** $2(x-3)^2-8=0$, bundan $(x-3)^2=4$, $x-3=\pm2$, $x=5$ yoki $x=1$
— ikkita yechim.
