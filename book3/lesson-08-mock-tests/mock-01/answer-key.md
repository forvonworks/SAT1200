# Book 3 — Mock Test 1 — Answer Key & To'liq Yechimlar

Har bir javob to'liq yechim bilan beriladi — shunchaki "to'g'ri/noto'g'ri" emas, **nega**
to'g'ri ekanligini tushunib oling. Barcha sonli javoblar Python skripti orqali
tekshirilgan.

*(Every answer comes with a full solution — not just "right/wrong." All numeric answers
were verified via a Python script.)*

## Module 1

**1. Javob: D** ($y=4.8x+162$)

Kitning tug'ilgandagi uzunligi ($162$) — bu $x=0$ dagi boshlang'ich qiymat, ya'ni
$y$-kesishma. Oyiga $4.8$ sm o'sish — bu burchak koeffitsienti (slope). Demak,
$y = 4.8x + 162$.

*(Birth length is the starting value at x=0 — the y-intercept. The monthly growth rate
is the slope. So y = 4.8x + 162.)*

**2. Javob: B** ($y=8x+25$)

Qat'iy to'lov ($\$25$) — $y$-kesishma; soatiga to'lov ($\$8$) — burchak koeffitsienti.

**3. Javob: $10$**

$5x-12=3x+8$. $2x=20$. $x=10$.

**4. Javob: A** ($2$)

$2x^2+38x+10 = 2(x^2+19x+5)$. Demak, ifodaning ko'paytuvchilari $2$ va $x^2+19x+5$. Faqat
$2$ javoblar orasida bor.

*(Factor out the GCF: 2(x²+19x+5). Only 2 appears among the answer choices.)*

**5. Javob: A** ($10x-15$)

$3(2x-5)+4x = 6x-15+4x = 10x-15$.

**6. Javob: A** ($5$)

$f(3)=3^2-4=9-4=5$.

**7. Javob: A** ($31°$)

Uchburchak burchaklari yig'indisi $180°$. $\angle J=\angle K=x$ bo'lsin: $2x+118=180$,
$2x=62$, $x=31$.

**8. Javob: $12$**

Nisbat: $\dfrac{3}{2}=\dfrac{18}{x}$. Bundan $x=12$.

**9. Javob: B** ($a=2$, $b=7$)

$a(3-x)-b=-1-2x$ ni ochamiz: $3a-ax-b=-1-2x$. $x$ koeffitsientlari teng bo'lishi kerak:
$-a=-2$, demak $a=2$. Erkin hadlar: $3a-b=-1$, ya'ni $6-b=-1$, $b=7$.

**10. Javob: $-3$**

$a(2x+5)-b=4x+15$ ni ochamiz: $2ax+5a-b=4x+15$. $x$ koeffitsienti: $2a=4$, $a=2$. Erkin
had: $5a-b=15$, ya'ni $10-b=15$, $b=-5$. $a+b=2+(-5)=-3$.

**11. Javob: C** ($4$)

$x-y=2$ dan $x=y+2$. Birinchi tenglamaga qo'yamiz: $3(y+2)+2y=16$, $5y+6=16$, $y=2$,
$x=4$.

**12. Javob: B** ($x\ge-5$)

$-3x+7\le22$. $-3x\le15$. Ikkala tomonni **manfiy** songa bo'lganimiz uchun tengsizlik
belgisi teskariga o'zgaradi: $x\ge-5$.

*(Dividing by a negative number flips the inequality sign.)*

**13. Javob: B** ($a=\dfrac{x}{8(b+9)}$)

$x=8a(b+9)$. $b$ musbat son bo'lgani uchun $b+9\ne0$. Ikkala tomonni $8(b+9)$ ga
bo'lamiz: $a=\dfrac{x}{8(b+9)}$.

**14. Javob: $6$**

$x^2-6x-16=0$ ni faktorlab yechamiz: $(x-8)(x+2)=0$, $x=8$ yoki $x=-2$. Yig'indi:
$8+(-2)=6$. (Tezroq yo'l: Vieta formulasi bo'yicha yig'indi $=-b/a=6$ — Book 3 Lesson 4.)

**15. Javob: C** ($7$)

$g(x)=-2(x-3)^2+7$ — bu cho'qqi (vertex) ko'rinishi. Koeffitsient $-2<0$ bo'lgani uchun
parabola pastga qaragan, demak cho'qqi — maksimum nuqta: $g_{max}=7$.

**16. Javob: A** ($16x$)

$(x+4)^2-(x-4)^2 = (x^2+8x+16)-(x^2-8x+16) = 16x$.

**17. Javob: $80$**

Narx $25\%$ ga oshib $\$100$ bo'lgan: $1.25p=100$, $p=80$.

**18. Javob: D** ($24$)

Kvadrat X perimetri: $4\times12=48$. Kvadrat Y perimetri: $2\times48=96$. Y tomoni:
$96\div4=24$.

**19. Javob: C** ($144\pi$)

$V=\pi r^2h = \pi(4)^2(9)=144\pi$.

**20. Javob: $\dfrac{189}{5}$** (yoki $37.8$)

$y$-kesishma — $x=0$ bo'lgan nuqta. $\dfrac{3(0)}{7}=-\dfrac{5y}{9}+21$, demak
$0=-\dfrac{5y}{9}+21$. $\dfrac{5y}{9}=21$. $y=\dfrac{189}{5}$.

**21. Javob: D** (Day 12)

Bakteriyalar soni har kuni ikki baravar ko'payadi. $3$-kun oxirida $10^6$. $d$-kun
oxirida: $10^6\times2^{d-3}$. Shart: $10^6\times2^{d-3}=5.12\times10^8=512\times10^6$,
demak $2^{d-3}=512=2^9$. $d-3=9$, $d=12$.

**22. Javob: D** ($383$)

$a=\dfrac{230}{100}b$ va $a=\dfrac{60}{100}c$. Demak $\dfrac{230}{100}b=\dfrac{60}{100}c$,
bundan $c=\dfrac{23}{6}b$. $c=\dfrac{p}{100}b$ bo'lgani uchun $\dfrac{23}{6}=\dfrac{p}{100}$,
$p=\dfrac{2300}{6}\approx383.33$. Eng yaqin javob: $383$.

## Module 2

**1. Javob: $31$**

$1{,}116\div36=31$ yard.

**2. Javob: A** ($10$)

Pifagor teoremasi: $\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=\sqrt{36+64}=\sqrt{100}=10$. (Bu — tanish $3$-$4$-$5$
uchburchagining $2$ baravari: $6$-$8$-$10$.)

**3. Javob: $0.25$** (yoki $\dfrac{1}{4}$)

$24.5x$ — tuproqning umumiy narxi, demak $1$ kub yard tuproq $\$24.50$. $24.75y$ —
maydatosh narxi, demak $1$ tonna maydatosh $\$24.75$. Farq: $24.75-24.50=0.25$.

**4. Javob: B** ($2$)

Burchak koeffitsienti: $\dfrac{13-5}{6-2}=\dfrac{8}{4}=2$.

**5. Javob: C** ($x-1$)

Agar $f(x)$ grafigi $(a,0)$ nuqtadan o'tsa, $x-a$ — $f(x)$ ning ko'paytuvchisi. Grafik
$(1,0)$ dan o'tgani uchun $x-1$ ko'paytuvchi bo'lishi shart.

**6. Javob: B** ($5$)

$x+y=11$ dan $x=11-y$. $2(11-y)-y=7$: $22-3y=7$, $y=5$.

**7. Javob: $3$**

$x^2+5x-24=0$. $(x+8)(x-3)=0$. $x=-8$ yoki $x=3$. Musbat yechim: $3$.

**8. Javob: B** ($2$)

Kvadrat funksiya $ax^2+bx+c$ ning minimum/maksimum nuqtasi $x=-\dfrac{b}{2a}$ da.
$x=-\dfrac{-12}{2(3)}=\dfrac{12}{6}=2$.

**9. Javob: A** ($2x^2+7x-15$)

$(2x-3)(x+5)=2x^2+10x-3x-15=2x^2+7x-15$.

**10. Javob: $20$**

$12(50)+15y=900$: $600+15y=900$, $15y=300$, $y=20$.

**11. Javob: C** ($20$)

$5$ sonning yig'indisi: $5\times18=90$. $10$ ni olib tashlasak: $90-10=80$. $4$ ta son
uchun o'rtacha: $80\div4=20$.

**12. Javob: C** ($100°$)

$\angle A+\angle B+\angle C=180°$. $\angle B=2\angle C$ bo'lsin $\angle C=c$. $30+2c+c=180$,
$3c=150$, $c=50$. $\angle B=2(50)=100°$.

**13. Javob: D** ($\dfrac{5}{7}$)

$\dfrac{1}{5}x+\dfrac{1}{7}y-70=0$ ni $y=mx+b$ ko'rinishiga keltiramiz:
$\dfrac{1}{7}y=-\dfrac{1}{5}x+70$, $y=-\dfrac{7}{5}x+490$. Burchak koeffitsienti
$h=-\dfrac{7}{5}$. Perpendikulyar chiziqning burchak koeffitsienti — qarama-qarshi
teskari son: $-\dfrac{1}{-7/5}=\dfrac{5}{7}$.

**14. Javob: B** ($3$)

Cheksiz ko'p yechim bo'lishi uchun ikkala tenglama **bir xil chiziqni** ifodalashi
kerak — ya'ni barcha koeffitsientlar nisbati teng: $\dfrac{8}{4}=\dfrac{6}{k}=\dfrac{24}{12}$.
$\dfrac{8}{4}=2$, demak $\dfrac{6}{k}=2$, $k=3$. (Tekshirish: $\dfrac{24}{12}=2$ ✓.)

**15. Javob: $21$**

$x$-kesishma — $y=0$ bo'lgan nuqta. $\dfrac{2x}{3}=0+14=14$. $x=\dfrac{3\times14}{2}=21$.

**16. Javob: B** ($\dfrac{2}{3}$)

$\dfrac{1}{4}x+\dfrac{1}{6}y-20=0$: $\dfrac{1}{6}y=-\dfrac{1}{4}x+20$,
$y=-\dfrac{6}{4}x+120=-\dfrac{3}{2}x+120$. Burchak koeffitsienti $p=-\dfrac{3}{2}$.
Perpendikulyar: $-\dfrac{1}{-3/2}=\dfrac{2}{3}$.

**17. Javob: $14$**

Ikkinchi son $y$, birinchi son $2y+5$. $y(2y+5)=462$: $2y^2+5y-462=0$. Faktorlab:
$(2y+33)(y-14)=0$. $y=14$ (musbat butun son). Kichikroq son: $14$. (Kattaroq:
$2(14)+5=33$; tekshirish: $14\times33=462$ ✓.)

**18. Javob: C** (Day 24)

$200\times2^{d/4}=12800$. $2^{d/4}=64=2^6$. $\dfrac{d}{4}=6$, $d=24$.

**19. Javob: $20$**

Kichikroq son $y$, kattaroq $y+4$. $y(y+4)=320$: $y^2+4y-320=0$. Faktorlab:
$(y+20)(y-16)=0$. $y=16$ (musbat). Kattaroq son: $16+4=20$.

**20. Javob: A** ($3$)

$3x^2+51x+18=3(x^2+17x+6)$. Ko'paytuvchilar: $3$ va $x^2+17x+6$. Faqat $3$ javoblar
orasida bor. (Bu — $4$-savoldagi bir xil naqsh, boshqa sonlar bilan.)

**21. Javob: A** ($\dfrac{14}{95}$)

Jami marmar: $8+5+7=20$. Ikkita qizil tortib olish (qaytarilmasdan):
$\dfrac{8}{20}\times\dfrac{7}{19}=\dfrac{56}{380}=\dfrac{14}{95}$.

**22. Javob: D** ($x^2+(y+1)^2=49$)

Doira A: markaz $(0,1)$, radius $7$. $2$ birlik pastga surilsa, markaz $(0,1-2)=(0,-1)$
bo'ladi, radius o'zgarmaydi. Yangi tenglama: $x^2+(y-(-1))^2=49$, ya'ni
$x^2+(y+1)^2=49$.
