# Book 3 — Mock Test 1 — Module 2

**22 savol — 35 daqiqa.** Bu modul — Module 1'da yaxshi natija ko'rsatgan talaba oladigan
**qiyinroq versiya** (`pacing-strategy.md`'ga qarang). Kalkulyator ruxsat etilgan.
Geometriya ma'lumotnoma varag'i mavjud.

*(This module is the **harder version** a student who did well on Module 1 would
receive (see `pacing-strategy.md`). Calculator allowed. Geometry reference sheet
available.)*

---

**1.** How many yards are equivalent to $1{,}116$ inches? ($1$ yard $= 36$ inches)

**2.** A right triangle has legs of length $6$ and $8$. What is the length of the
hypotenuse?

A) $10$
B) $12$
C) $14$
D) $\sqrt{48}$

**3.** Isabel ordered topsoil and crushed stone, which cost a total of $\$641$, for her
garden. The equation $24.5x + 24.75y = 641$ represents the relationship between the
number of cubic yards of topsoil, $x$, and the number of tons of crushed stone, $y$,
Isabel ordered. How much more, in dollars, did a ton of crushed stone cost Isabel than a
cubic yard of topsoil?

**4.** A line passes through the points $(2,5)$ and $(6,13)$. What is the slope of the
line?

A) $\dfrac{1}{2}$
B) $2$
C) $4$
D) $8$

**5.** For the polynomial function $f$, the graph of $y = f(x)$ in the $xy$-plane passes
through the points $(-5,0)$, $(1,0)$, and $(4,0)$. Which of the following must be a
factor of $f(x)$?

A) $x+1$
B) $x+4$
C) $x-1$
D) $x-5$

**6.** If $2x - y = 7$ and $x + y = 11$, what is the value of $y$?

A) $3$
B) $5$
C) $6$
D) $7$

**7.** What is the positive solution to the equation $x^2 + 5x - 24 = 0$?

**8.** The function $h$ is defined by $h(x) = 3x^2 - 12x + 7$. For what value of $x$ does
$h$ reach its minimum?

A) $1$
B) $2$
C) $3$
D) $4$

**9.** Which expression is equivalent to $(2x-3)(x+5)$?

A) $2x^2 + 7x - 15$
B) $2x^2 - 7x - 15$
C) $2x^2 + 13x - 15$
D) $2x^2 + 2x - 15$

**10.** A company rents out chairs for $\$12$ each and tables for $\$15$ each. The
equation $12x + 15y = 900$ relates the number of chairs, $x$, and tables, $y$, rented for
a total revenue of $\$900$. If the company rented out $50$ chairs, how many tables did
it rent?

**11.** The mean of $5$ numbers is $18$. If one of the numbers, $10$, is removed, what is
the mean of the remaining $4$ numbers?

A) $16$
B) $18$
C) $20$
D) $22$

**12.** In $\triangle ABC$, the measure of $\angle A$ is $30°$ and the measure of
$\angle B$ is twice the measure of $\angle C$. What is the measure of $\angle B$?

A) $50°$
B) $80°$
C) $100°$
D) $130°$

**13.** Line $h$ is defined by $\dfrac{1}{5}x + \dfrac{1}{7}y - 70 = 0$. Line $j$ is
perpendicular to line $h$ in the $xy$-plane. What is the slope of line $j$?

A) $-\dfrac{7}{5}$
B) $-\dfrac{5}{7}$
C) $\dfrac{7}{5}$
D) $\dfrac{5}{7}$

**14.** For what value of $k$ does the system of equations below have infinitely many
solutions?
$$4x + ky = 12$$
$$8x + 6y = 24$$

A) $2$
B) $3$
C) $6$
D) $12$

**15.** What is the $x$-coordinate of the $x$-intercept of the graph of
$\dfrac{2x}{3} = -\dfrac{7y}{4} + 14$ in the $xy$-plane?

**16.** Line $p$ is defined by $\dfrac{1}{4}x + \dfrac{1}{6}y - 20 = 0$. Line $q$ is
perpendicular to line $p$ in the $xy$-plane. What is the slope of line $q$?

A) $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
B) $\dfrac{2}{3}$
C) $-\dfrac{2}{3}$
D) $\dfrac{3}{2}$

**17.** The product of two positive integers is $462$. If the first integer is $5$
greater than twice the second integer, what is the smaller of the two integers?

**18.** A population of insects doubles every $4$ days. If there are $200$ insects on
day $0$, on which day will the population first reach $12{,}800$?

A) $16$
B) $20$
C) $24$
D) $28$

**19.** The product of two positive integers is $320$. If the first integer is $4$ more
than the second integer, what is the larger of the two integers?

**20.** Which expression is a factor of $3x^2 + 51x + 18$?

A) $3$
B) $17x$
C) $51x$
D) $3x^2$

**21.** A bag contains $8$ red, $5$ blue, and $7$ green marbles. If two marbles are drawn
at random without replacement, what is the probability that both marbles drawn are red?

A) $\dfrac{14}{95}$
B) $\dfrac{7}{38}$
C) $\dfrac{2}{5}$
D) $\dfrac{8}{39}$

**22.** The equation $x^2 + (y-1)^2 = 49$ represents circle A. Circle B is obtained by
shifting circle A down $2$ units in the $xy$-plane. Which of the following equations
represents circle B?

A) $(x-2)^2 + (y-1)^2 = 49$
B) $x^2 + (y-3)^2 = 49$
C) $(x+2)^2 + (y-1)^2 = 49$
D) $x^2 + (y+1)^2 = 49$
