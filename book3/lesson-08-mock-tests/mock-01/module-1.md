# Book 3 — Mock Test 1 — Module 1

**22 savol — 35 daqiqa.** Kalkulyator ruxsat etilgan (har qanday turda). Geometriya
ma'lumotnoma varag'i (doira/to'rtburchak/uchburchak yuzi, prizma/silindr/konus/shar
hajmi, Pifagor teoremasi, 30-60-90 va 45-45-90 nisbatlari, 360°/2π faktlari) butun
modul davomida mavjud. Har bir savol uchun yoki 4 ta javobdan birini tanlang (A-D),
yoki talab qilingan son qiymatini kiriting (grid-in — birlik yozilmaydi, manfiy son
bo'lmaydi).

*(22 questions — 35 minutes. Calculator allowed throughout — any type. A geometry
reference sheet (circle/rectangle/triangle area, prism/cylinder/cone/sphere volume,
Pythagorean theorem, 30-60-90 and 45-45-90 ratios, 360°/2π facts) is available for the
whole module. For each question, either choose one of 4 answer choices (A-D) or enter
the required numeric value — grid-in items have no units, no negative numbers.)*

---

**1.** The length, $y$, of a white whale was $162$ centimeters (cm) when it was born and
increased an average of $4.8$ cm per month for the first $12$ months after it was born.
Which equation best represents this situation, where $x$ is the number of months after
the whale was born and $y$ is the length, in cm, of the whale?

A) $y = 162x$
B) $y = 162x + 162$
C) $y = 4.8x + 4.8$
D) $y = 4.8x + 162$

**2.** A moving company charges a flat fee of $\$25$ plus $\$8$ per hour of labor. Which
equation gives the total charge $y$, in dollars, for $x$ hours of labor?

A) $y = 25x$
B) $y = 8x + 25$
C) $y = 25x + 8$
D) $y = 33x$

**3.** Solve for $x$: $5x - 12 = 3x + 8$

**4.** Which expression is a factor of $2x^2 + 38x + 10$?

A) $2$
B) $5x$
C) $38x$
D) $2x^2$

**5.** Which expression is equivalent to $3(2x - 5) + 4x$?

A) $10x - 15$
B) $10x - 5$
C) $6x - 15 + 4x$
D) $2x - 15$

**6.** If $f(x) = x^2 - 4$, what is $f(3)$?

A) $5$
B) $9$
C) $-4$
D) $13$

**7.** In $\triangle JKL$, the measures of both $\angle J$ and $\angle K$ are equal and the
measure of $\angle L$ is $118°$. What is the measure of $\angle J$?

A) $31°$
B) $59°$
C) $62°$
D) $90°$

**8.** A recipe requires $3$ cups of flour for every $2$ cups of sugar. If a baker uses
$18$ cups of flour, how many cups of sugar are needed?

**9.** In the equation $a(3-x) - b = -1 - 2x$, $a$ and $b$ are constants. If the equation
has infinitely many solutions, what are the values of $a$ and $b$?

A) $a = 2$ and $b = 1$
B) $a = 2$ and $b = 7$
C) $a = -2$ and $b = 5$
D) $a = -2$ and $b = -5$

**10.** In the equation $a(2x+5) - b = 4x + 15$, $a$ and $b$ are constants. If the
equation has infinitely many solutions, what is the value of $a + b$?

**11.** If $3x + 2y = 16$ and $x - y = 2$, what is the value of $x$?

A) $2$
B) $3$
C) $4$
D) $5$

**12.** Which of the following is equivalent to $-3x + 7 \le 22$?

A) $x \le -5$
B) $x \ge -5$
C) $x \le 5$
D) $x \ge 5$

**13.** The given equation relates the positive numbers $a$, $b$, and $x$:
$$x = 8a(b+9)$$
Which equation correctly expresses $a$ in terms of $b$ and $x$?

A) $a = \dfrac{x}{8} - (b+9)$
B) $a = \dfrac{x}{8(b+9)}$
C) $a = \dfrac{8(b+9)}{x}$
D) $a = 8x(b+9)$

**14.** What is the sum of the solutions to $x^2 - 6x - 16 = 0$?

**15.** The function $g$ is defined by $g(x) = -2(x-3)^2 + 7$. What is the maximum value
of $g$?

A) $3$
B) $-2$
C) $7$
D) $-3$

**16.** Which expression is equivalent to $(x+4)^2 - (x-4)^2$?

A) $16x$
B) $32$
C) $8x$
D) $0$

**17.** A jacket's price was increased by $25\%$ to $\$100$. What was the original price,
in dollars?

**18.** Square $X$ has a side length of $12$ centimeters. The perimeter of square $Y$ is
$2$ times the perimeter of square $X$. What is the length, in centimeters, of one side
of square $Y$?

A) $6$
B) $10$
C) $14$
D) $24$

**19.** A cylinder has a radius of $4$ and a height of $9$. What is its volume, in terms
of $\pi$?

A) $36\pi$
B) $72\pi$
C) $144\pi$
D) $576\pi$

**20.** What is the $y$-coordinate of the $y$-intercept of the graph of
$\dfrac{3x}{7} = -\dfrac{5y}{9} + 21$ in the $xy$-plane?

**21.** A culture of bacteria is growing at an exponential rate, as shown in the table.

| Day | Number of bacteria per milliliter at end of day |
|---|---|
| 1 | $2.5 \times 10^5$ |
| 2 | $5.0 \times 10^5$ |
| 3 | $1.0 \times 10^6$ |

At this rate, on which day would the number of bacteria per milliliter reach
$5.12 \times 10^8$?

A) Day 5
B) Day 9
C) Day 11
D) Day 12

**22.** The positive number $a$ is $230\%$ of the number $b$, and $a$ is $60\%$ of the
number $c$. If $c$ is $p\%$ of $b$, which of the following is closest to the value of
$p$?

A) $138$
B) $217$
C) $283$
D) $383$
