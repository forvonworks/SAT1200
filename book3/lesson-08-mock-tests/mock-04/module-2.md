# Mock Test 4 — Module 2

**Vaqt: 35 daqiqa | 22 savol | Kalkulyator ruxsat etilgan**

*(Time: 35 minutes | 22 questions | Calculator allowed)*

**Bu modul qiyinroq versiya sifatida qurilgan** — Module 1'da yaxshi natija ko'rsatgan
talaba haqiqiy testda olishi mumkin bo'lgan versiya (qarang: `pacing-strategy.md`).

*(**This module is built as the harder version** — the one a student who did well on
Module 1 would receive on the real test. See `pacing-strategy.md`.)*

Geometriya formulalar varag'i butun modul davomida mavjud. Kalkulyator har bir savolda
ruxsat etilgan.

*(The geometry reference sheet is available throughout. A calculator is allowed on every
question.)*

---

**1.** A recipe calls for 3 cups of flour for every 2 cups of sugar. If a baker uses 12 cups of flour, how many cups of sugar are needed?

A. 6
B. 7
C. 8
D. 9

**2.** A rectangle has a length of 15 cm and a width of 8 cm. What is the area, in square centimeters, of the rectangle?

A. 92
B. 104
C. 120
D. 128

**3.** In the $xy$-plane, which of the following does NOT contain any points that are part of the solution set to $5x - 7y > 35$?

A. The $x$-axis
B. The region where $x > 0$ and $y > 0$
C. The region where $x < 0$ and $y < 0$
D. The region where $x < 0$ and $y > 0$

**4.** Solve the system: $2x + y = 11$, $x - y = 1$. What is the value of $x$?

A. 3
B. 4
C. 5
D. 6

**5.** A line has a slope of $-2$ and passes through the point $(3, 4)$. What is the $y$-intercept of the line?

A. $-2$
B. 4
C. 10
D. 16

**6.** What is the smallest integer value of $x$ that satisfies the inequality $3x - 5 > 16$? *(grid-in)*

**7.** The expression $90y^5 - 54y^4$ is equivalent to $ry^4(15y - 9)$, where $r$ is a constant. What is the value of $r$? *(grid-in)*

**8.** $$g(x) = -2(x-3)^2 + 7$$

What is the maximum value of the given function?

A. $-2$
B. 3
C. 7
D. 9

**9.** A radioactive substance decays according to the model $A(t) = 500(0.5)^{t/10}$, where $t$ is in years. What is the value of $A(t)$ when $t = 20$?

A. 100
B. 125
C. 150
D. 250

**10.** The expression $6x^2 + 13x - 5$ can be written as $(2x+5)(ax-1)$, where $a$ is a constant. What is the value of $a$? *(grid-in)*

**11.** The mean of 5 numbers is 20. If a sixth number, 38, is added to the set, what is the new mean? *(grid-in)*

**12.** A triangle has a base length of 56 centimeters and a height of 112 centimeters. What is the area, in square centimeters, of the triangle?

A. 168
B. 1,568
C. 3,136
D. 6,272

**13.** The table below shows some values of $x$ and their corresponding values $f(x)$ for the linear function $f$.

| $x$ | $-11$ | $-10$ | $-9$ | $-8$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $f(x)$ | 21 | 18 | 15 | 12 |

What is the $x$-intercept of the graph of $y = f(x)$ in the $xy$-plane?

A. $(-3, 0)$
B. $(-4, 0)$
C. $(-9, 0)$
D. $(-12, 0)$

**14.** $$7x - 5y = 4$$ $$4x - 8y = 9$$

If $(x, y)$ is the solution to the system of equations above, what is the value of $3x + 3y$?

A. $-13$
B. $-5$
C. $5$
D. $13$

**15.** The system of equations $kx + 6y = 18$ and $4x + 8y = 24$ has infinitely many solutions. What is the value of $k$?

A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 6

**16.** Line $p$ is defined by $y = -4x + 9$. Line $q$ is perpendicular to line $p$ and passes through the point $(8, 5)$. What is the $y$-intercept of line $q$?

A. 1
B. 2
C. 3
D. 5

**17.** $$\sqrt[5]{70n}\left(\sqrt[6]{70n}\right)^2$$

For what value of $x$ is the given expression equivalent to $(70n)^{30x}$, where $n > 1$? *(grid-in)*

**18.** $$\frac{1}{4xy} + xyz = \frac{1}{3yz}$$

In the given equation, $x$, $y$, and $z$ are positive numbers. Which expression is equivalent to $y$?

A. $\dfrac{4x-3z}{12x^2z^2}$
B. $\sqrt{\dfrac{4x-3z}{12x^2z^2}}$
C. $\dfrac{1}{3xz^2-4x^2z}$
D. $\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{3xz^2-4x^2z}}$

**19.** For how many values of $x$ does the system $y = x^2 - 4x + 7$ and $y = 2x - 1$ intersect?

A. 0
B. 1
C. 2
D. Infinitely many

**20.** What is the sum of the solutions to the equation $\dfrac{x-3}{x+2} = \dfrac{4}{x}$, where $x \neq 0, -2$?

A. 5
B. 6
C. 7
D. 8

**21.** 210 is $p\%$ greater than 30. What is the value of $p$? *(grid-in)*

**22.** For triangles $ABC$ and $FGH$, angles $B$ and $G$ each measure $51°$, the length of $\overline{AB}$ is 6, and the length of $\overline{FG}$ is 18. Which additional piece(s) of information is(are) sufficient to prove that triangle $ABC$ is similar to triangle $FGH$?

I. The measure of angle $A$ is equal to the measure of angle $F$.
II. The measure of angle $C$ is equal to the measure of angle $H$.
III. The length of $\overline{FH}$ is 3 times the length of $\overline{AC}$.

A. I is sufficient, II is sufficient, and III is sufficient.
B. I is sufficient and II is sufficient, but III is not.
C. II is sufficient and III is sufficient, but I is not.
D. III is sufficient, but I is not and II is not.
