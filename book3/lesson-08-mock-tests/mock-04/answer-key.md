## Module 1

**1. Javob: C (6 oy)**

$25m + 45 = 195$ tenglamasini tuzamiz, bu yerda $m$ — oylar soni. Ikkala tomondan $45$ ni
ayiramiz: $25m = 150$. Ikkala tomonni $25$ ga bo'lamiz: $m = 6$.

**2. Javob: C (32)**

$3x - 7 = 20$ dan $3x = 27$. Savol $3x+5$ ni so'ramoqda, $3x$ ni qayta hisoblash shart emas
— to'g'ridan-to'g'ri $27+5=32$.

**3. Javob: D (4)**

$p$ — kartoshka funti, $c$ — selderey funti. $0.69p + 0.99c = 5.34$ va $c = 2p$. Ikkinchisini
birinchisiga qo'yamiz: $0.69p + 0.99(2p) = 5.34$, ya'ni $2.67p = 5.34$, bundan $p = 2$.
Demak $c = 2(2) = 4$.

**4. Javob: 99**

Shartga ko'ra $x + 8 = 11$, bundan $x = 3$. Buni $y = x^2+14x+48$ ga qo'yamiz:
$y = 9 + 42 + 48 = 99$.

**5. Javob: B (12)**

$f(5) = 5^2 - 3(5) + 2 = 25 - 15 + 2 = 12$.

**6. Javob: A ($x^7$)**

Bir xil asosni ko'paytirishda darajalar qo'shiladi: $x^3 \cdot x^4 = x^{3+4} = x^7$.

**7. Javob: D ($\frac{107}{135}$)**

Jadvalga ko'ra, tashkilotda jami $135$ a'zo bor, ulardan $107$ tasi kamida $40$ yoshda.
Tasodifiy tanlangan a'zoning kamida $40$ yoshda bo'lish ehtimoli — mos keluvchilar soni
butun songa bo'linadi: $\dfrac{107}{135}$.

**8. Javob: C (60°)**

Uchburchak burchaklari yig'indisi $180°$. $180 - 50 - 70 = 60$.

**9. Javob: 7**

Chiziq tenglamasi $y = 5x - 35$ (slope $5$, y-kesishma $-35$). x-kesishma — $y=0$
bo'lgan nuqta: $0 = 5x - 35$, bundan $x = 7$.

**10. Javob: C (66)**

Kichik son $s$, katta son $3s+12$. $s + 3s+12 = 84$, bundan $4s=72$, $s=18$. Katta son:
$3(18)+12=66$.

**11. Javob: 19**

$\dfrac{2x-3}{5}=7$ — ikkala tomonni $5$ ga ko'paytiramiz: $2x-3=35$, bundan $2x=38$,
$x=19$.

**12. Javob: C (11)**

$n+p=20$ va $4n+2p=62$. Birinchisidan $p=20-n$. Ikkinchisiga qo'yamiz: $4n+2(20-n)=62$,
ya'ni $2n+40=62$, $2n=22$, $n=11$.

**13. Javob: B (55)**

$g(x)=x^2+55$ — bu $a(x-h)^2+k$ ko'rinishida, bu yerda $h=0$, $k=55$. $a=1>0$ bo'lgani
uchun minimal qiymat — $k$, ya'ni $55$.

**14. Javob: B (3)**

$x^2-9x+18=0$ ni ko'paytuvchilarga ajratamiz: $(x-3)(x-6)=0$, demak $x=3$ yoki $x=6$.
Musbat farq: $6-3=3$.

**15. Javob: 4**

$2x^2=32$, bundan $x^2=16$, $x=\pm4$. Shartga ko'ra $x>0$, demak $x=4$.

**16. Javob: C (1,600)**

Populyatsiya har $3$ soatda ikki baravar ortadi. $9$ soat $=3\times3$ davr, demak
$200\times2^3=200\times8=1600$.

**17. Javob: C (60)**

$150\times0.6=90$ talaba online darslarni afzal ko'rdi. Afzal ko'rmaganlar:
$150-90=60$.

**18. Javob: C ($\frac23\pi$)**

$T$, $O$, $P$ nuqtalari $x$-o'qida yotadi, demak ular to'g'ri chiziq (yig'indisi $180°$).
$\angle POQ=\angle ROT=30°$ berilgan, demak $\angle QOR=180-30-30=120°$. Darajani
radianga aylantiramiz: $120°\times\dfrac{\pi}{180°}=\dfrac{2}{3}\pi$.

**19. Javob: 36**

Aylana uzunligi $C=2\pi r$. $72\pi=2\pi r$, bundan $r=36$.

**20. Javob: A ($-432$)**

$h$ chizig'ining burchak koeffitsienti: $(0,-9)$ va $(-6,0)$ nuqtalari orqali,
$k=\dfrac{-9-0}{0-(-6)}=\dfrac{-9}{6}=-\dfrac32$. Tizim yechimga ega bo'lmasligi uchun
$k$ chizig'i $h$ bilan parallel bo'lishi, ammo boshqa y-kesishmaga ega bo'lishi kerak.
$sx+48y=t$ ni $y=-\dfrac{s}{48}x+\dfrac{t}{48}$ ko'rinishiga keltiramiz — y-kesishma
$\dfrac{t}{48}$. Bu $-9$ ga teng **bo'lmasligi** kerak: $\dfrac{t}{48}\ne-9$, bundan
$t\ne-432$. Demak $t=-432$ — mumkin **emas** qiymat, bu aynan savol so'ragan narsa.

**21. Javob: B ($-9$)**

$g(x)=f(x-1)=(x-1+6)(x-1+5)(x-1+1)=(x+5)(x+4)(x)$. x-kesishmalar: $x=0$, $x=-5$,
$x=-4$. Yig'indi: $0+(-5)+(-4)=-9$.

**22. Javob: 42**

$p$ million MTN obunachilari, jami $97$ million obunachi, ehtimollik $0.43$:
$\dfrac{p(1{,}000{,}000)}{97{,}000{,}000}=0.43$, soddalashtirib $\dfrac{p}{97}=0.43$,
bundan $p=0.43\times97=41.71$, yaqin butun songa yaxlitlab $42$.

## Module 2

**1. Javob: C (8)**

Nisbat $3:2$ (un:shakar). $12$ stakan un $= 3\times4$, demak masshtab koeffitsienti
$4$. Shakar: $2\times4=8$.

**2. Javob: C (120)**

To'rtburchak yuzi $=$ uzunlik $\times$ kenglik $=15\times8=120$.

**3. Javob: D ($x<0$ va $y>0$)**

$5x-7y>35$ ni $5x+(-7y)>35$ deb yozamiz. Agar $x<0$ va $y>0$ bo'lsa, $5x$ manfiy va
$-7y$ ham manfiy — ularning yig'indisi manfiy, demak $35$ dan katta bo'la olmaydi. Shuning
uchun bu hudud yechim nuqtalarini o'z ichiga **olmaydi**.

**4. Javob: B (4)**

$2x+y=11$ va $x-y=1$ tenglamalarini qo'shamiz: $3x=12$, bundan $x=4$.

**5. Javob: C (10)**

$y=mx+b$, $m=-2$, nuqta $(3,4)$: $4=-2(3)+b=-6+b$, bundan $b=10$.

**6. Javob: 8**

$3x-5>16$, bundan $3x>21$, $x>7$. Eng kichik butun son — $8$.

**7. Javob: 6**

$ry^4(15y-9)=15ry^5-9ry^4$ deb ochamiz. $90y^5-54y^4$ ga tenglashtiramiz: $90=15r$,
bundan $r=6$.

**8. Javob: C (7)**

$g(x)=-2(x-3)^2+7$ — $a=-2<0$ bo'lgani uchun bu yuqoriga qarab ochiladigan emas, balki
pastga qarab ochiladigan parabola, demak maksimal qiymat — vertex $k=7$.

**9. Javob: B (125)**

$A(20)=500(0.5)^{20/10}=500(0.5)^2=500\times0.25=125$.

**10. Javob: 3**

$(2x+5)(ax-1)$ ni ochamiz: $2ax^2+(5a-2)x-5$. $x^2$ koeffitsientini solishtiramiz:
$2a=6$, bundan $a=3$. Tekshirish: $5(3)-2=13$ ✓ (o'rtacha hadning koeffitsienti mos
keladi).

**11. Javob: 23**

$5$ sonning o'rtachasi $20$, demak yig'indi $=100$. $38$ qo'shilganda yangi yig'indi
$=138$, yangi o'rtacha $=138/6=23$.

**12. Javob: C (3,136)**

Uchburchak yuzi $=\dfrac12 \times$ asos $\times$ balandlik $=\dfrac12(56)(112)=3{,}136$.

**13. Javob: B ($(-4,0)$)**

Jadvaldan ikki nuqta: $(-11,21)$ va $(-10,18)$. Burchak koeffitsienti:
$m=\dfrac{18-21}{-10-(-11)}=-3$. $y=-3x+b$, $(-11,21)$ ni qo'yamiz: $21=-3(-11)+b=33+b$,
bundan $b=-12$. x-kesishma: $0=-3x-12$, $x=-4$.

**14. Javob: B ($-5$)**

$(7x-5y)-(4x-8y)=4-9$ — birinchi tenglamadan ikkinchisini ayiramiz:
$7x-5y-4x+8y=-5$, ya'ni $3x+3y=-5$.

**15. Javob: B (3)**

Cheksiz ko'p yechim uchun koeffitsientlar nisbati teng bo'lishi kerak:
$\dfrac{k}{4}=\dfrac{6}{8}=\dfrac34$. Bundan $k=4\times\dfrac34=3$.

**16. Javob: C (3)**

$p$ chizig'ining burchak koeffitsienti $-4$. Perpendikulyar chiziq uchun teskari-qarama-qarshi
qiymat: $\dfrac14$. $(8,5)$ nuqtasidan: $5=\dfrac14(8)+b=2+b$, bundan $b=3$.

**17. Javob: 4/225 (yoki .0177, .0178)**

$\sqrt[5]{70n}=(70n)^{1/5}$, $\left(\sqrt[6]{70n}\right)^2=(70n)^{2/6}=(70n)^{1/3}$.
Ko'paytmada darajalar qo'shiladi: $(70n)^{1/5+1/3}=(70n)^{8/15}$. Shartga ko'ra bu
$(70n)^{30x}$ ga teng, demak $\dfrac{8}{15}=30x$, bundan $x=\dfrac{8}{450}=\dfrac{4}{225}$.

**18. Javob: B**

$\dfrac{1}{4xy}+xyz=\dfrac{1}{3yz}$ ni $12xyz$ ga ko'paytiramiz: $3z+12x^2y^2z^2=4x$.
Bundan $12x^2y^2z^2=4x-3z$, $y^2=\dfrac{4x-3z}{12x^2z^2}$. $y$ musbat bo'lgani uchun
kvadrat ildiz olamiz: $y=\sqrt{\dfrac{4x-3z}{12x^2z^2}}$ — variant **B**.

**19. Javob: C (2)**

$x^2-4x+7=2x-1$, bundan $x^2-6x+8=0$, ya'ni $(x-2)(x-4)=0$. Ikkita yechim: $x=2$ va
$x=4$ — demak ikkita kesishish nuqtasi.

**20. Javob: C (7)**

Ko'ndalang ko'paytiramiz: $x(x-3)=4(x+2)$, ya'ni $x^2-3x=4x+8$, $x^2-7x-8=0$,
$(x-8)(x+1)=0$. $x=8$ yoki $x=-1$ — ikkalasi ham $x\ne0,-2$ shartini qanoatlantiradi.
Yig'indi: $8+(-1)=7$.

**21. Javob: 600**

$210$ soni $30$ dan $p\%$ ko'p: $210=\left(1+\dfrac{p}{100}\right)(30)$. Ikkala tomonni
$30$ ga bo'lamiz: $7=1+\dfrac{p}{100}$, bundan $\dfrac{p}{100}=6$, $p=600$.

**22. Javob: B (I yetarli va II yetarli, ammo III yetarli emas)**

Agar $\angle A=\angle F$ bo'lsa (I), $\angle B=\angle G$ bilan birga ikkita mos burchak
teng — AA mezoni bo'yicha uchburchaklar o'xshash, demak I **yetarli**. Xuddi shunday,
agar $\angle C=\angle H$ bo'lsa (II), yana ikkita mos burchak teng — II ham **yetarli**.
III uchun: $FH=3\times AC$ bo'lsa, $FG/AB=18/6=3$ bilan bir xil nisbat — ammo $\angle B$
($AB$ va $BC$ orasida emas, $AB$ va... aslida $\angle B$ — $AB$ bilan $BC$ orasida, $AC$
bilan emas) berilgan burchak $AB$ va $AC$ orasida **emas** (u $AB$ va $BC$ orasida), demak
SAS mezonini qo'llash uchun burchak noto'g'ri joyda — III **yetarli emas**.
