# Mock Test 9 — Module 1

**22 savol — 35 daqiqa.** (22 questions — 35 minutes.)

Kalkulyatordan har qanday savolda foydalanish mumkin. Doira/to'rtburchak/uchburchak yuzasi,
prizma/silindr/konus/sfera hajmi, Pifagor teoremasi va 30-60-90/45-45-90 uchburchak
nisbatlari uchun ma'lumotnoma varag'i butun modul davomida ekranda mavjud.

*(A calculator is allowed on every question. A reference sheet — circle/rectangle/triangle
area, prism/cylinder/cone/sphere volume, the Pythagorean theorem, and 30-60-90/45-45-90
triangle ratios — is available throughout this module.)*

Ko'p tanlovli savollarda faqat bitta to'g'ri javob bor. Raqamli javob (grid-in) talab
qiluvchi savollarda javobni kasr yoki o'nli kasr shaklida kiritish mumkin; manfiy javob
va o'lchov birliklari qabul qilinmaydi.

*(Multiple-choice questions have exactly one correct answer. Grid-in questions accept a
fraction or decimal; negative answers and units are not accepted.)*

---

**1.** If $3x + 7 = 22$, what is the value of $x$?

A. $3$
B. $5$
C. $7$
D. $15$

**2.** In a box of pens, the ratio of black pens to red pens is $8$ to $1$. There are $40$ black pens in the box. How many red pens are in the box?

A. $5$
B. $8$
C. $40$
D. $320$

**3.** If $x^2 - 9 = 0$ and $x > 0$, what is the value of $x$?

**4.** The area of a rectangle is $630$ square inches. The length of the rectangle is $70$ inches. What is the width, in inches, of this rectangle?

A. $9$
B. $70$
C. $315$
D. $560$

**5.** Maria plans to rent a boat. The boat rental costs $\$60$ per hour, and she will also have to pay for a water safety course that costs $\$10$. Maria wants to spend no more than $\$280$ for the rental and the course. If the boat rental is available only for a whole number of hours, what is the maximum number of hours for which Maria can rent the boat?

**6.** Which expression is equivalent to $3(x+4) - 2x$?

A. $x + 4$
B. $x + 12$
C. $5x + 12$
D. $x - 12$

**7.** If $5x - 3 \le 17$, what is the greatest integer value of $x$?

**8.** Which of the following is a solution to the equation $2x^2 - 4 = x^2$?

A. $1$
B. $2$
C. $3$
D. $4$

**9.**
$$x + 3 = -2y + 5$$
$$x - 3 = 2y + 7$$

The solution to the given system of equations is $(x, y)$. What is the value of $2x$?

A. $-2$
B. $6$
C. $12$
D. $24$

**10.** An object has a mass of $168$ grams and a volume of $24$ cubic centimeters. What is the density, in grams per cubic centimeter, of the object?

A. $7$
B. $144$
C. $192$
D. $4{,}032$

**11.** If $f(x) = 2x^2 - 5$, what is the value of $f(3)$?

A. $1$
B. $7$
C. $13$
D. $18$

**12.** In $\triangle ABC$, $\angle B$ is a right angle and the length of $\overline{BC}$ is $136$ millimeters. If $\cos A = \dfrac{3}{5}$, what is the length, in millimeters, of $\overline{AB}$?

A. $34$
B. $102$
C. $136$
D. $170$

**13.** If $2x + y = 10$ and $x - y = 2$, what is the value of $x$?

A. $2$
B. $3$
C. $4$
D. $6$

**14.** Which expression is equivalent to $(x+5)(x-5)$?

A. $x^2 - 25$
B. $x^2 + 25$
C. $x^2 - 10x - 25$
D. $x^2 + 10x - 25$

**15.** Two angles are supplementary. One angle measures $112°$. What is the measure, in degrees, of the other angle?

**16.** A line has a slope of $-3$ and passes through the point $(2, 1)$. What is the $y$-intercept of the line?

A. $-5$
B. $-1$
C. $1$
D. $7$

**17.** Which of the following expressions is equivalent to $8x^{10} - 8x^9 + 88x$?

A. $x(7x^{10} - 7x^9 + 87x)$
B. $x(8^{10} - 8^9 + 88)$
C. $8x(x^{10} - x^9 + 11x)$
D. $8x(x^9 - x^8 + 11)$

**18.** If $4x - 3y = 12$ and $x = 6$, what is the value of $y$?

A. $2$
B. $4$
C. $6$
D. $8$

**19.** What is the positive solution to $x^2 + 2x - 15 = 0$?

**20.** Hector used a tool called an auger to remove corn from a storage bin at a constant rate. The bin contained $24{,}000$ bushels of corn when Hector began to use the auger. After $5$ hours of using the auger, $19{,}350$ bushels of corn remained in the bin. If the auger continues to remove corn at this rate, what is the total number of hours Hector will have been using the auger when $12{,}840$ bushels of corn remain in the bin?

A. $3$
B. $7$
C. $8$
D. $12$

**21.** For the exponential function $f$, the table below shows several values of $x$ and their corresponding values of $f(x)$, where $a$ is a constant greater than $1$.

| $x$ | $f(x)$ |
|---|---|
| $1$ | $a$ |
| $2$ | $a^5$ |
| $3$ | $a^9$ |

If $k$ is a constant and $f(k) = a^{29}$, what is the value of $k$?

**22.** The results of two random samples of votes for a proposition are shown below. The samples were selected from the same population, and the margins of error were calculated using the same method.

| Sample | Percent in favor | Margin of error |
|---|---|---|
| A | $52\%$ | $4.2\%$ |
| B | $48\%$ | $1.6\%$ |

Which of the following is the most appropriate reason that the margin of error for sample A is greater than the margin of error for sample B?

A. Sample A had a smaller number of votes that could not be recorded.
B. Sample A had a higher percent of favorable responses.
C. Sample A had a larger sample size.
D. Sample A had a smaller sample size.
