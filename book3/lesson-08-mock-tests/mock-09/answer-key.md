## Module 1

**1. Javob: B ($x=5$).** $3x+7=22$. Ikkala tomondan $7$ ni ayiramiz: $3x=15$. Ikkala
tomonni $3$ ga bo'lamiz: $x=5$.

**2. Javob: A ($5$).** Qora va qizil ruchkalar nisbati $8:1$, demak qizil ruchkalar soni
qora ruchkalar sonining $\frac18$ qismiga teng. $40$ ta qora ruchka bor, demak qizil
ruchkalar soni $40\times\frac18=5$.

**3. Javob: $3$.** $x^2-9=0$, demak $x^2=9$, $x=\pm3$. $x>0$ shart qilingani uchun $x=3$.

**4. Javob: A ($9$).** To'rtburchak yuzi $A=\ell w$. $630=70w$, demak $w=630\div70=9$.

**5. Javob: $4$.** Qayiq ijarasi: $60h+10\le280$. Ikkala tomondan $10$ ni ayiramiz:
$60h\le270$, demak $h\le4.5$. Soat faqat butun son bo'lishi kerak, shuning uchun eng ko'pi
$4$ soat.

**6. Javob: B ($x+12$).** $3(x+4)-2x=3x+12-2x=x+12$.

**7. Javob: $4$.** $5x-3\le17$. Ikkala tomonga $3$ qo'shamiz: $5x\le20$, demak $x\le4$. Eng
katta butun qiymat — $4$.

**8. Javob: B ($2$).** $2x^2-4=x^2$. Ikkala tomondan $x^2$ ni ayiramiz: $x^2-4=0$, demak
$x^2=4$, $x=\pm2$. Berilgan variantlar orasida $x=2$ bor.

**9. Javob: C ($12$).** $x+3=-2y+5$ va $x-3=2y+7$ tenglamalarini qo'shamiz:
$(x+3)+(x-3)=(-2y+5)+(2y+7)$, demak $2x=12$.

**10. Javob: A ($7$).** Zichlik $=\dfrac{\text{massa}}{\text{hajm}}=\dfrac{168}{24}=7$.

**11. Javob: C ($13$).** $f(x)=2x^2-5$. $f(3)=2(3)^2-5=2(9)-5=18-5=13$.

**12. Javob: B ($102$).** $\angle B$ to'g'ri burchak, $\cos A=\frac35$, demak $AB:AC=3:5$.
Pifagor uchligi $3$-$4$-$5$ ga ko'ra, $BC:AC=4:5$, shuning uchun $AB:BC=3:4$. $BC=136$,
demak $\frac{AB}{136}=\frac34$, $AB=136\times\frac34=102$.

**13. Javob: C ($4$).** $2x+y=10$ va $x-y=2$ ni qo'shamiz: $3x=12$, demak $x=4$.

**14. Javob: A ($x^2-25$).** $(x+5)(x-5)=x^2-5x+5x-25=x^2-25$ — bu ayirmalar kvadrati
formulasi: $(a+b)(a-b)=a^2-b^2$.

**15. Javob: $68$.** Yondosh (supplementary) burchaklar yig'indisi $180°$. $180-112=68$.

**16. Javob: D ($7$).** $y=-3x+b$. Nuqta $(2,1)$: $1=-3(2)+b=-6+b$, demak $b=7$.

**17. Javob: D ($8x(x^9-x^8+11)$).** Har bir hadning umumiy ko'paytuvchisi — $8x$:
$8x^{10}-8x^9+88x=8x(x^9-x^8+11)$.

**18. Javob: B ($4$).** $4x-3y=12$, $x=6$: $4(6)-3y=12$, $24-3y=12$, $-3y=-12$, $y=4$.

**19. Javob: $3$.** $x^2+2x-15=0$ ko'paytuvchilarga ajratiladi: $(x+5)(x-3)=0$, demak
$x=-5$ yoki $x=3$. Musbat yechim — $3$.

**20. Javob: D ($12$).** Hector soatiga $\frac{24000-19350}{5}=\frac{4650}{5}=930$ bushel
olib tashlaydi. $24000-930h=12840$ tenglamasidan $930h=11160$, $h=12$.

**21. Javob: $8$.** Jadvaldagi qiymatlar $x$ har $1$ ga oshganda $a^4$ ga ko'payadi, demak
$f(x)=a^{4x+b}$. $x=2$ da $f(x)=a^5$: $4(2)+b=5$, $b=-3$. Shuning uchun
$f(x)=a^{4x-3}$. $f(k)=a^{29}$: $4k-3=29$, $4k=32$, $k=8$.

**22. Javob: D (kichikroq namuna hajmi — smaller sample size).** Xatolik chegarasi
(margin of error) namuna hajmiga bog'liq: namuna qanchalik kichik bo'lsa, xatolik chegarasi
shunchalik katta bo'ladi, chunki kichik namuna populyatsiyani kamroq aniq aks ettiradi.
A namunasining xatolik chegarasi kattaroq ($4.2\%>1.6\%$), demak A namunasi kichikroq
bo'lgan.

## Module 2

**1. Javob: $36$.** $15\%$ of $240 = 0.15\times240=36$.

**2. Javob: C ($90\pi$).** Silindr hajmi $V=\pi r^2h=\pi(3)^2(10)=90\pi$.

**3. Javob: C ($g(x)=2x+12$).** $g(x)=2x+b$. Nuqta $(1,14)$: $14=2(1)+b$, demak $b=12$.

**4. Javob: D ($44$).** Sham $17-6=11$ untsiya mumni yo'qotgan. $1$ untsiya har $4$ soatda
yo'qoladi, demak $11\times4=44$ soat.

**5. Javob: B ($2$).** $x-y=2$ dan $x=y+2$. $3(y+2)+2y=16$: $5y+6=16$, $5y=10$, $y=2$.

**6. Javob: C ($2$).** Qiyalik $=\dfrac{5-(-3)}{4-0}=\dfrac{8}{4}=2$.

**7. Javob: B ($7x^3+10$).** $(8x^3+8)-(x^3-2)=8x^3+8-x^3+2=7x^3+10$.

**8. Javob: $-4$.** $g(x)=x^2+4x$. $g(-2)=(-2)^2+4(-2)=4-8=-4$.

**9. Javob: C ($4x^2-12x+9$).** $(2x-3)^2=(2x)^2-2(2x)(3)+3^2=4x^2-12x+9$.

**10. Javob: $5$.** $3x^2=75$, $x^2=25$, $x=\pm5$. Musbat qiymat — $5$.

**11. Javob: B ($\frac{1}{10}$).** $5$ kunda $1200\times5=6000$ hisob ro'yxatdan o'tadi.
$\dfrac{6000}{60000}=\dfrac{1}{10}$.

**12. Javob: B ($\frac{308}{317}$).** $\triangle FGH\sim\triangle JKL$ da $\angle F$ mos
keladi $\angle J$ ga, demak ular teng burchaklar — teng burchaklarning sinusi ham teng:
$\sin(J)=\sin(F)=\frac{308}{317}$.

**13. Javob: $25$.** $x+y=32$ va $x=3y+4$. O'rniga qo'yamiz: $3y+4+y=32$, $4y=28$, $y=7$,
demak $x=3(7)+4=25$.

**14. Javob: $210$.** $0.10(90)+0.20y=0.17(90+y)$: $9+0.20y=15.3+0.17y$. $0.03y=6.3$,
$y=210$.

**15. Javob: C ($19$).** Perimetr: $2(L+W)=54$, demak $L+W=27$. $L=2W+3$: $2W+3+W=27$,
$3W=24$, $W=8$, demak $L=2(8)+3=19$.

**16. Javob: D ($600$).** $25+0.08m=73$: $0.08m=48$, $m=48\div0.08=600$.

**17. Javob: C ($f(x)=x^2+91x$).** $x$ dan $91$ ko'p son — $x+91$. Ko'paytma:
$f(x)=x(x+91)=x^2+91x$.

**18. Javob: B ($-2$).** $h(x)=(x-4)(x+6)=0$: $x=4$ yoki $x=-6$. Yig'indi:
$4+(-6)=-2$. *(Bu — Vieta formulasi: $ax^2+bx+c=0$ uchun ildizlar yig'indisi $=-b/a$;
kengaytirilsa $h(x)=x^2+2x-24$, demak yig'indi $=-\frac{2}{1}=-2$, mos keladi.)*

**19. Javob: $25$.** Bitta haqiqiy yechim uchun diskriminant nolga teng bo'lishi kerak:
$(-10)^2-4(1)(k)=0$, $100-4k=0$, $k=25$.

**20. Javob: B ($(2x-3)(2x+3)(4x^2+9)$).** $16x^4-81=(4x^2)^2-9^2$ — ayirmalar kvadrati:
$(4x^2-9)(4x^2+9)$. $4x^2-9$ o'zi ham ayirmalar kvadrati: $(2x-3)(2x+3)$. To'liq
ko'paytuvchilarga ajratilgan shakl: $(2x-3)(2x+3)(4x^2+9)$.

**21. Javob: $0.88$ (yoki $\frac{22}{25}$).** $a=1.60b$ (60% ko'p). $c=0.55a$ (45% kam).
$c=0.55(1.60b)=0.88b$.

**22. Javob: B ($12$).** $\sqrt{80}$ eng katta uzunlik, demak u gipotenuza. Qolgan ikki
tomon — katetlar: $2\sqrt2$ va $6\sqrt2$. Yuza $=\frac12(2\sqrt2)(6\sqrt2)=\frac12(12\times2)
=\frac12(24)=12$.
