**1.** What is $15\%$ of $240$?

**2.** A cylinder has a radius of $3$ and a height of $10$. What is the volume of the cylinder, in terms of $\pi$?

A. $30\pi$
B. $60\pi$
C. $90\pi$
D. $900\pi$

**3.** For the linear function $g$, the graph of $y = g(x)$ in the $xy$-plane has a slope of $2$ and passes through the point $(1, 14)$. Which equation defines $g$?

A. $g(x) = 2x$
B. $g(x) = 2x + 2$
C. $g(x) = 2x + 12$
D. $g(x) = 2x + 14$

**4.** A candle is made of $17$ ounces of wax. When the candle is burning, the amount of wax in the candle decreases by $1$ ounce every $4$ hours. If $6$ ounces of wax remain in this candle, for how many hours has it been burning?

A. $3$
B. $6$
C. $24$
D. $44$

**5.** If $3x + 2y = 16$ and $x - y = 2$, what is the value of $y$?

A. $0$
B. $2$
C. $4$
D. $6$

**6.** A line passes through the points $(0, -3)$ and $(4, 5)$. What is the slope of the line?

A. $-2$
B. $\dfrac{1}{2}$
C. $2$
D. $8$

**7.** Which expression is equivalent to $(8x^3 + 8) - (x^3 - 2)$?

A. $8x^3 + 6$
B. $7x^3 + 10$
C. $8x^3 + 10$
D. $7x^3 + 6$

**8.** If $g(x) = x^2 + 4x$, what is the value of $g(-2)$?

**9.** Which expression is equivalent to $(2x - 3)^2$?

A. $4x^2 - 9$
B. $4x^2 + 9$
C. $4x^2 - 12x + 9$
D. $4x^2 - 6x + 9$

**10.** If $3x^2 = 75$, what is the positive value of $x$?

**11.** If $1{,}200$ customers register for new accounts at a social media website every day, what fraction of the first $60{,}000$ new accounts are registered in the first $5$ days?

A. $\dfrac{1}{5}$
B. $\dfrac{1}{10}$
C. $\dfrac{1}{12}$
D. $\dfrac{1}{50}$

**12.** Triangle $FGH$ is similar to triangle $JKL$, where angle $F$ corresponds to angle $J$ and angles $G$ and $K$ are right angles. If $\sin(F) = \dfrac{308}{317}$, what is the value of $\sin(J)$?

A. $\dfrac{75}{317}$
B. $\dfrac{308}{317}$
C. $\dfrac{317}{308}$
D. $\dfrac{317}{75}$

**13.** A piece of wire with a length of $32$ inches is cut into two parts. One part has a length of $x$ inches, and the other part has a length of $y$ inches. The value of $x$ is $4$ more than $3$ times the value of $y$. What is the value of $x$?

**14.** The equation $0.10x + 0.20y = 0.17(x+y)$ gives a volume $x$, in gallons, of a $10\%$ solution that could be mixed with a volume $y$, in gallons, of a $20\%$ solution to produce a $17\%$ solution. According to this equation, what volume, in gallons, of the $20\%$ solution could be mixed with $90.0$ gallons of the $10\%$ solution to produce a $17\%$ solution? (Assume that the volume of the mixture is the sum of the volumes of the two solutions before they were mixed.)

**15.** A rectangle's perimeter is $54$ centimeters. The length is $3$ centimeters more than twice the width. What is the length, in centimeters, of the rectangle?

A. $8$
B. $16$
C. $19$
D. $24$

**16.** A company charges a flat fee of $\$25$ plus $\$0.08$ per mile for a delivery service. If a customer's total charge was $\$73$, how many miles did the delivery travel?

A. $48$
B. $300$
C. $480$
D. $600$

**17.** The function $f$ gives the product of a number, $x$, and a number that is $91$ more than $x$. Which equation defines $f$?

A. $f(x) = x^2 + x + 91$
B. $f(x) = x^2 + 91$
C. $f(x) = x^2 + 91x$
D. $f(x) = x^2 + 91x + 91$

**18.** The function $h$ is defined by $h(x) = (x-4)(x+6)$. What is the sum of the two values of $x$ for which $h(x) = 0$?

A. $-24$
B. $-2$
C. $2$
D. $10$

**19.** If $x^2 - 10x + k = 0$ has exactly one real solution, what is the value of $k$?

**20.** What is $16x^4 - 81$ written as a product of polynomials with integer coefficients, factored completely?

A. $(4x^2 - 9)(4x^2 + 9)$
B. $(2x-3)(2x+3)(4x^2+9)$
C. $(4x^2-9)^2$
D. $(2x-9)(2x+9)$

**21.** The number $a$ is $60\%$ greater than the positive number $b$. The number $c$ is $45\%$ less than $a$. The number $c$ is how many times $b$?

**22.** A right triangle has sides of length $2\sqrt{2}$, $6\sqrt{2}$, and $\sqrt{80}$ units. What is the area of the triangle, in square units?

A. $8\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{80}$
B. $12$
C. $24\sqrt{80}$
D. $24$
