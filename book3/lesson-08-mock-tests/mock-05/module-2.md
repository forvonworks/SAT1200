# Mock Test 5 — Module 2

**Vaqt: 35 daqiqa. 22 savol.** Bu modul birinchi moduldan **qiyinroq** — agar siz birinchi
modulda yaxshi natija ko'rsatgan bo'lsangiz, haqiqiy testda aynan shu qiyinlikdagi modulni
olasiz. Kalkulyator har bir savolda ruxsat etilgan. Geometriya ma'lumotnoma varag'i
testning oxirida berilgan.

*(Time: 35 minutes. 22 questions. This module is harder than Module 1 — matching the
version a student who did well on Module 1 would receive on the real test. Calculator
allowed throughout.)*

---

**1.** A survey of 40 students found that 24 prefer tea over coffee. What percent of the
students prefer tea?

A. 40%
B. 50%
C. 60%
D. 75%

**2.** A right triangle has legs of length 6 and 8. What is the length of the hypotenuse?

A. 9
B. 10
C. 12
D. 14

**3.** If $3(2x - 1) = 5x + 4$, what is the value of $x$?

**4.** A line has a slope of $-3$ and passes through the point $(1, 4)$. What is the
$y$-intercept of the line?

A. 1
B. 4
C. 7
D. 10

**5.** If $4x + 3y = 24$ and $y = 4$, what is the value of $x$?

A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 5

**6.**
$$7m = 5(n + p)$$
The given equation relates the positive numbers $m$, $n$, and $p$. Which equation
correctly gives $n$ in terms of $m$ and $p$?

A. $n = \dfrac{5p}{7m}$
B. $n = \dfrac{7m}{5} - p$
C. $n = 5(7m) + p$
D. $n = 7m - 5 - p$

**7.** If $3^{x+1} = 81$, what is the value of $x$?

**8.** One of the two equations in a linear system is $2x + 6y = 10$. The system has no
solution. Which of the following could be the other equation in the system?

A. $x + 3y = 5$
B. $x + 3y = -20$
C. $6x - 2y = 0$
D. $6x + 2y = 10$

**9.** The average (arithmetic mean) of 5 numbers is 18. If one of the numbers is removed,
the average of the remaining 4 numbers is 20. What is the value of the number that was
removed?

A. 8
B. 10
C. 12
D. 15

**10.** What is the product of the solutions to $(x-5)(x+9) = 0$?

A. $-45$
B. $-14$
C. 14
D. 45

**11.** A rectangle has a length of 12 and a width of 5. What is the length of the
rectangle's diagonal?

A. 11
B. 12
C. 13
D. 15

**12.** If $f(x) = \sqrt{x+5}$, what is the value of $f(11)$?

A. 3
B. 4
C. 5
D. 6

**13.**
$$2x^2 - 4x = t$$
In the equation above, $t$ is a constant. If the equation has no real solutions, which of
the following could be the value of $t$?

A. $-3$
B. $-1$
C. 1
D. 3

**14.** An event planner is planning a party. It costs the event planner a one-time fee of
$35 to rent the venue and $10.25 per attendee. The event planner has a budget of $300.
What is the greatest number of attendees possible without exceeding the budget?

**15.**
$$3x - y = 7$$
$$6x - 2y = k$$
For what value of $k$ does the given system of equations have infinitely many solutions?

A. 7
B. 10
C. 14
D. 21

**16.** $f(x) = (x-10)(x+13)$. The function $f$ is defined by the given equation. For what
value of $x$ does $f(x)$ reach its minimum?

A. $-130$
B. $-13$
C. $-\dfrac{23}{2}$
D. $-\dfrac{3}{2}$

**17.** A boat travels 60 miles upstream (against a current) in 5 hours, and the same 60
miles downstream (with the current) in 3 hours. Let $b$ be the boat's speed in still
water and $c$ be the speed of the current, both in miles per hour. What is the value of
$c$?

A. 2
B. 4
C. 6
D. 8

**18.** A quadratic equation $x^2 - 10x + k = 0$ has exactly one real solution. What is
the value of $k$?

A. 10
B. 20
C. 25
D. 50

**19.** A sample of a certain alloy has a total mass of 50.0 grams and is 50.0% silicon by
mass. The sample was created by combining two pieces of different alloys. The first piece
was 30.0% silicon by mass and the second piece was 80.0% silicon by mass. What was the
mass, in grams, of the silicon in the second piece?

A. 9.0
B. 16.0
C. 20.0
D. 30.0

**20.** The function $p$ is defined by $p(x) = 2x^3 - 5$. What is the value of $x$ for
which $p(x) = 11$?

A. 1
B. 2
C. 3
D. 4

**21.** A data set has a mean of 50 and a standard deviation of 4. If every value in the
data set is increased by 10 and then the result is multiplied by 2, what is the standard
deviation of the new data set?

**22.** The graph of $x^2 + x + y^2 + y = \dfrac{199}{2}$ in the $xy$-plane is a circle.
What is the length of the circle's radius?
