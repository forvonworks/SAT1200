# Mock Test 5 — Module 1

**Vaqt: 35 daqiqa. 22 savol.** Kalkulyator har bir savolda ruxsat etilgan. Geometriya
ma'lumotnoma varag'i (doira/to'rtburchak/uchburchak yuzi, prizma/silindr/konus/shar hajmi,
Pifagor teoremasi, 30-60-90 va 45-45-90 uchburchak nisbatlari) testning oxirida berilgan.

*(Time: 35 minutes. 22 questions. A calculator is allowed on every question. The geometry
reference sheet is at the end of this test.)*

Har bir ko'p tanlovli savolda faqat bitta javob to'g'ri. "Grid-in" savollarda javobni
raqam sifatida yozing (butun son, o'nli kasr yoki oddiy kasr; manfiy javob, birlik yoki
aralash son kiritilmaydi).

**1.** If $4x + 9 = 33$, what is the value of $x$?

**2.** What is the solution to $2(x+3) = 16$?

A. 2
B. 5
C. 8
D. 11

**3.** If $y = 3x - 2$ and $x = 4$, what is the value of $y$?

A. 8
B. 10
C. 12
D. 14

**4.** If $f(x) = x^2 - 3$, what is the value of $f(4)$?

**5.** Which of the following is equivalent to $3(x+2) - x$?

A. $2x + 2$
B. $2x + 6$
C. $3x + 6$
D. $4x + 6$

**6.** If $2^x = 32$, what is the value of $x$?

A. 4
B. 5
C. 6
D. 16

**7.** The bar graph shows the distribution of the number of students in each of four
extracurricular activities at a high school: chess — 30, drama — 40, robotics — 58,
yearbook — 43. How many more students are in drama than in chess?

A. 10
B. 30
C. 40
D. 70

**8.** In the triangle shown, the three interior angles measure $31°$, $2b°$, and $a°$,
where $a = 45$. What is the value of $b$?

A. 52
B. 59
C. 76
D. 104

**9.** If $4x - \dfrac{1}{2} = -5$, what is the value of $8x - 1$?

A. 2
B. $-\dfrac{9}{8}$
C. $-\dfrac{5}{2}$
D. $-10$

**10.** A rectangle's length is 3 more than twice its width $w$. If the perimeter of the
rectangle is 54, what is the width?

A. 6
B. 7
C. 8
D. 9

**11.** Line $k$ passes through the points $(2, 5)$ and $(6, 13)$. What is the slope of
line $k$?

A. 1
B. 2
C. 3
D. 4

**12.**
$$\frac{7}{2}x + 6y = 25$$
$$\frac{5}{2}x + 6y = 23$$
The solution to the given system of equations is $(x, y)$. What is the value of
$\frac{17}{2}x + 18y$?

A. 2
B. 3
C. 48
D. 71

**13.** If $g(x) = 2x^2 - 5x + 1$, what is the value of $g(-2)$?

A. 9
B. 11
C. 15
D. 19

**14.** A model predicts that the population of a town was $15{,}000$ in 2005, and that
each year for the next 5 years, the population $p$ increased by $4\%$ of the previous
year's population. Which equation best represents this model, where $x$ is the number of
years after 2005, for $x \le 5$?

A. $p = 0.96(15{,}000)^x$
B. $p = 1.04(15{,}000)^x$
C. $p = 15{,}000(0.96)^x$
D. $p = 15{,}000(1.04)^x$

**15.** The function $h$ is defined by $h(x) = (x-3)(x+7)$. What is the sum of the
solutions to $h(x) = 0$?

A. $-10$
B. $-4$
C. 4
D. 10

**16.** The table summarizes the distribution of color and shape for 100 tiles of equal
area:

| | Red | Blue | Yellow | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Square | 10 | 20 | 25 | 55 |
| Pentagon | 20 | 10 | 15 | 45 |
| Total | 30 | 30 | 40 | 100 |

If one of these tiles is selected at random, what is the probability of selecting a red
tile? (Express your answer as a decimal or fraction, not as a percent.)

**17.** A sphere has a radius of $\dfrac{17}{5}$ feet. What is the volume, in cubic feet,
of the sphere?

A. $\dfrac{5\pi}{17}$
B. $\dfrac{68\pi}{15}$
C. $\dfrac{32\pi}{5}$
D. $\dfrac{19{,}652\pi}{375}$

**18.** A circle has a circumference of $18\pi$. What is the radius of the circle?

**19.** An object hangs from a spring. The formula $\ell = 30 + 2w$ relates the length
$\ell$, in centimeters, of the spring to the weight $w$, in newtons, of the object. Which
of the following describes the meaning of the $2$ in this context?

A. The length, in centimeters, of the spring with no weight attached
B. The weight, in newtons, of an object that will stretch the spring 30 centimeters
C. The increase in the weight, in newtons, of the object for each one-centimeter increase
   in the length of the spring
D. The increase in the length, in centimeters, of the spring for each one-newton increase
   in the weight of the object

**20.** On average, a certain plant grows 87 millimeters every $m$ months. At this rate,
which expression represents the number of millimeters, on average, the plant grows every
$k$ years?

A. $\dfrac{29m}{4k}$
B. $\dfrac{29k}{4m}$
C. $\dfrac{1{,}044m}{k}$
D. $\dfrac{1{,}044k}{m}$

$$y = -1.5$$
$$y = x^2 + 8x + a$$
In the given system of equations, $a$ is a positive constant. The system has exactly one
distinct real solution. What is the value of $a$?
