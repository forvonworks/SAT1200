## Module 1

**1. Javob: $x=6$.** $4x+9=33$ tenglamasining ikkala tomonidan $9$ ni ayiramiz:
$4x=24$, so'ng $4$ ga bo'lamiz: $x=6$. *(Subtract 9 from both sides, then divide by 4.)*

**2. Javob: B ($5$).** $2(x+3)=16$ ikkala tomonni $2$ ga bo'lamiz: $x+3=8$, demak $x=5$.

**3. Javob: B ($10$).** $x=4$ ni $y=3x-2$ ga qo'yamiz: $y=3(4)-2=10$.

**4. Javob: $13$.** $f(4)=4^2-3=16-3=13$.

**5. Javob: B ($2x+6$).** Qavsni ochamiz: $3x+6-x=2x+6$.

**6. Javob: B ($5$).** $2^x=32=2^5$, demak $x=5$.

**7. Javob: A ($10$).** Drama ustuni $40$, chess ustuni $30$: $40-30=10$.

**8. Javob: A ($52$).** Uchburchak burchaklari yig'indisi $180°$: $31+2b+45=180$, demak
$2b=104$, $b=52$.

**9. Javob: D ($-10$).** Tenglamani $2$ ga ko'paytiramiz: $2(4x-\frac12)=2(-5)$, ya'ni
$8x-1=-10$. *(This is the fast path: multiply by 2 to directly produce $8x-1$ without
solving for $x$ first.)*

**10. Javob: C ($8$).** Perimetr: $2((2w+3)+w)=54$, ya'ni $6w+6=54$, $6w=48$, $w=8$.

**11. Javob: B ($2$).** Nishablik $=\dfrac{13-5}{6-2}=\dfrac{8}{4}=2$.

**12. Javob: D ($71$).** Ikkinchi tenglamani $2$ ga ko'paytiramiz: $5x+12y=46$. Birinchi
tenglamaga qo'shamiz: $\left(\frac72x+6y\right)+\left(5x+12y\right)=25+46$, ya'ni
$\frac{17}{2}x+18y=71$. *(Choices A, B, C are the values of $x$, $y$, and $6x+12y$
respectively — not what's actually asked.)*

**13. Javob: D ($19$).** $g(-2)=2(-2)^2-5(-2)+1=2(4)+10+1=19$.

**14. Javob: D.** Aholi soni har yili $1.04$ ga ko'payadi (dastlabki qiymatning
$100\%+4\%=104\%$): $p=15{,}000(1.04)^x$.

**15. Javob: B ($-4$).** Vieta formulasiga ko'ra, $(x-3)(x+7)=0$ ning ildizlari $3$ va
$-7$; ularning yig'indisi $3+(-7)=-4$ — bu $x$ uchun to'liq tenglamani yechmasdan, Book 3
Lesson 4'dagi tezkor usul.

**16. Javob: $0.3$ yoki $\frac{3}{10}$.** Qizil kataklar $=30$, jami $=100$:
$\frac{30}{100}=0.3$.

**17. Javob: D.** $V=\frac43\pi r^3=\frac43\pi\left(\frac{17}{5}\right)^3
=\frac43\pi\cdot\frac{4913}{125}=\frac{19{,}652\pi}{375}$.

**18. Javob: $9$.** $2\pi r=18\pi$, demak $r=9$.

**19. Javob: D.** Formula $\ell=30+2w$ da $2$ koeffitsienti — og'irlik $1$ nyutonga
ortganda, uzunlikning necha santimetrga ortishini ko'rsatadi.

**20. Javob: D ($\frac{1{,}044k}{m}$).** Tezlik $=\frac{87}{m}$ mm/oy. $k$ yilda $12k$ oy
bor, demak o'sish $=\frac{87}{m}\times12k=\frac{1{,}044k}{m}$.

**21. Javob: $\frac{29}{2}$ yoki $14.5$.** $y=-1.5$ ni ikkinchi tenglamaga qo'yamiz:
$-1.5=x^2+8x+a$, ya'ni $0=x^2+8x+(a+1.5)$. Aynan bitta yechim uchun diskriminant $=0$:
$8^2-4(a+1.5)=0$, $64=4a+6$, $4a=58$, $a=\frac{29}{2}$.

**22. Javob: A ($432$).** $x=3$ ni ikkinchi tenglamaga qo'yamiz: $y=(15-3)^2=144$.
$xy=3\times144=432$.

## Module 2

**1. Javob: C ($60\%$).** $\dfrac{24}{40}=0.6=60\%$.

**2. Javob: B ($10$).** Pifagor teoremasi: $\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=\sqrt{100}=10$.

**3. Javob: $x=7$.** $3(2x-1)=5x+4$: $6x-3=5x+4$, demak $x=7$.

**4. Javob: C ($7$).** $y=-3x+b$ chizig'i $(1,4)$ dan o'tadi: $4=-3(1)+b$, demak $b=7$.

**5. Javob: B ($3$).** $y=4$ ni qo'yamiz: $4x+12=24$, $4x=12$, $x=3$.

**6. Javob: B.** $7m=5(n+p)$ ikkala tomonni $5$ ga bo'lamiz: $\frac{7m}{5}=n+p$, demak
$n=\frac{7m}{5}-p$.

**7. Javob: $x=3$.** $3^{x+1}=81=3^4$, demak $x+1=4$, $x=3$.

**8. Javob: B.** $2x+6y=10$ ning koeffitsientlar nisbati $2:6=1:3$. Yechimi yo'q bo'lishi
uchun, boshqa tenglama bir xil nisbatga ega, ammo ekvivalent bo'lmasligi kerak:
$x+3y=-20$ aynan shu nisbatga ega ($1:3$), lekin $x+3y=5$ ga ekvivalent emas.

**9. Javob: B ($10$).** $5$ ta sonning yig'indisi $=5\times18=90$. $4$ ta sonning
yig'indisi $=4\times20=80$. Olib tashlangan son $=90-80=10$.

**10. Javob: A ($-45$).** Vieta: $(x-5)(x+9)=0$ ildizlari $5$ va $-9$; ko'paytmasi
$5\times(-9)=-45$.

**11. Javob: C ($13$).** Diagonal $=\sqrt{12^2+5^2}=\sqrt{169}=13$ (bu — $5$-$12$-$13$
Pifagor uchligi, Book 3 Lesson 4'da keltirilgan).

**12. Javob: B ($4$).** $f(11)=\sqrt{11+5}=\sqrt{16}=4$.

**13. Javob: A ($-3$).** $2x^2-4x-t=0$ ning diskriminanti $(-4)^2-4(2)(-t)=16+8t$. Haqiqiy
yechim yo'q bo'lishi uchun $16+8t<0$, ya'ni $t<-2$. Variantlardan faqat $-3$ shu shartni
qanoatlantiradi.

**14. Javob: $25$.** $35+10.25x\le300$, demak $10.25x\le265$, $x\le25.854$. Eng ko'p butun
son $=25$.

**15. Javob: C ($14$).** Ikkinchi tenglamani $2$ ga bo'lamiz: $3x-y=\frac{k}{2}$. Cheksiz
yechim uchun bu birinchi tenglama bilan bir xil bo'lishi kerak: $\frac{k}{2}=7$, $k=14$.

**16. Javob: D ($-\frac{3}{2}$).** $f(x)=(x-10)(x+13)=x^2+3x-130$. Vertex'ning
$x$-koordinatasi $=-\frac{b}{2a}=-\frac{3}{2}$. *(Alternative: the midpoint of the two
roots $10$ and $-13$ is $\frac{10+(-13)}{2}=-\frac{3}{2}$ — same answer, no expansion
needed.)*

**17. Javob: B ($4$).** $b-c=\frac{60}{5}=12$ va $b+c=\frac{60}{3}=20$. Qo'shamiz:
$2b=32$, $b=16$; demak $c=20-16=4$.

**18. Javob: C ($25$).** $x^2-10x+k=0$ ning diskriminanti $(-10)^2-4k=100-4k$. Aynan bitta
yechim uchun $100-4k=0$, $k=25$.

**19. Javob: B ($16.0$).** $x+y=50$ (jami massa), $0.3x+0.8y=25$ (kremniy massasi,
$0.5\times50$). $x=50-y$ ni qo'yamiz: $0.3(50-y)+0.8y=25$, $15+0.5y=25$, $y=20$.
Ikkinchi bo'lakdagi kremniy $=0.8\times20=16.0$.

**20. Javob: B ($2$).** $2x^3-5=11$, $2x^3=16$, $x^3=8$, $x=2$.

**21. Javob: $8$.** Barcha qiymatlarga doimiy son qo'shish standart og'ishni
o'zgartirmaydi; $2$ ga ko'paytirish esa standart og'ishni $2$ barobar oshiradi:
$4\times2=8$.

**22. Javob: $10$.** $x^2+x$ va $y^2+y$ ni to'liq kvadratga keltiramiz: har biriga
$\frac14$ qo'shamiz: $\left(x+\frac12\right)^2+\left(y+\frac12\right)^2=\frac{199}{2}+
\frac14+\frac14=100$. Demak $r=\sqrt{100}=10$.
