## Module 1

**1. Javob: B ($x=5$).** $3x+7=22$. Ikkala tomondan $7$ ni ayiramiz: $3x=15$. Ikkala
tomonni $3$ ga bo'lamiz: $x=5$.

**2. Javob: A ($7x^2-6$).** O'xshash hadlarni qo'shamiz: $5x^2+2x^2=7x^2$, $3+(-9)=-6$.
Demak, $(5x^2+3)+(2x^2-9)=7x^2-6$.

**3. Javob: D ($18$).** Diagrammada har bir ustunning balandligi shu sovg'ani tanlagan
ko'ngillilar sonini ko'rsatadi. C ustunining balandligi $18$, demak $18$ kishi C sovg'asini
tanlagan.

**4. Javob: D ($154$).** $m \parallel n$ bo'lgani uchun, kesuvchining bir tomonida joylashgan
ichki burchaklar (bir tomonlama ichki burchaklar) yig'indisi $180°$ ga teng. Shartga ko'ra,
$x+26=180$. Bundan $x=154$.

**5. Javob: B ($3.7$).** Harorat kamida $55.0°$F bo'lishi kerak. $x$ — kerakli ko'tarilish
bo'lsin: $51.3+x \ge 55.0$. Ikkala tomondan $51.3$ ni ayiramiz: $x \ge 3.7$.

**6. Javob: D ($x^2+6$).** $(9x^2+6)-(8x^2)=9x^2+6-8x^2=x^2+6$.

**7. Javob: B ($7$).** $84 \div 12 = 7$.

**8. Javob: B ($12$).** $f(6)=6^2-4(6)=36-24=12$.

**9. Javob: D ($y=-\frac{x}{3}+13$).** Nishab-kesma shaklida $y=mx+b$, bunda $m=-\frac13$.
$(9,10)$ nuqtasini qo'yamiz: $10=-\frac93+b=-3+b$, bundan $b=13$. Demak, $y=-\frac{x}{3}+13$.

**10. Javob: D ($f(x)=x^2+5x+14$).** $x=0$ bo'lganda $f(0)=14$ — bu har qanday
$f(x)=ax^2+bx+14$ shaklida bo'ladi (D variantida mos keladi). Tekshiramiz: $f(-1)=1-5+14=10$
✓, $f(1)=1+5+14=20$ ✓. Boshqa variantlar kamida bitta qiymatga mos kelmaydi (masalan, A:
$f(-1)=3-3+14=14\ne10$).

**11. Javob: $13$.** Doira tenglamasi $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$ shaklida, bunda $r$ — radius.
Berilgan tenglamada $r^2=169$, demak $r=\sqrt{169}=13$.

**12. Javob: D ($2$).** To'g'ri chiziq taxminan $(1,3.3)$ va $(7,14.5)$ nuqtalaridan o'tadi.
Nishab $=\frac{14.5-3.3}{7-1}=\frac{11.2}{6}\approx1.87$ — berilgan variantlardan eng yaqini
$2$.

**13. Javob: D ($2$).** $10x-5y=-12$. $-5y=-10x-12$. Ikkala tomonni $-5$ ga bo'lamiz:
$y=2x+\frac{12}{5}$. Demak, nishab $m=2$.

**14. Javob: $11875$.** $g(3)=19a^3=2375$. Ikkala tomonni $19$ ga bo'lamiz: $a^3=125$,
demak $a=5$. $g(4)=19\cdot5^4=19\cdot625=11{,}875$.

**15. Javob: B ($14\pi$).** Doira yuzi $\pi r^2=49\pi$, demak $r^2=49$, $r=7$. Aylana
uzunligi $=2\pi r=2\pi(7)=14\pi$.

**16. Javob: $1$.** $3x+6=4y$ va $3x+4=2y$. Ikkinchisini birinchisidan ayiramiz:
$(3x-3x)+(6-4)=4y-2y$, ya'ni $2=2y$. Demak, $y=1$.

**17. Javob: B.** $c=a(1+\frac{r}{100})^m$ shaklida, $a=1300$, $r=7$. Demak,
$c=1300(1.07)^m$.

**18. Javob: A ($-5$).** $y=4x+b$ shaklida, $(2,3)$ nuqtasini qo'yamiz: $3=4(2)+b=8+b$.
Bundan $b=-5$.

**19. Javob: D ($5$).** $(x-2)^2 \ge 0$ har doim, demak $h(x)=3(x-2)^2+5 \ge 5$. Minimal
qiymat $x=2$ bo'lganda erishiladi: $h(2)=0+5=5$.

**20. Javob: D ($5n+10=100$).** $9$-dyuymli: $n$ dona. $10$-dyuymli: $4n$ dona. $7$-dyuymli:
$10$ dona. Jami: $n+4n+10=5n+10$. Shartga ko'ra, bu $100$ ga teng: $5n+10=100$.

**21. Javob: $120$.** Kvadrat tenglama formulasi: $x=\frac{8\pm\sqrt{(-8)^2-4(2)(-7)}}{2(2)}
=\frac{8\pm\sqrt{64+56}}{4}=\frac{8\pm\sqrt{120}}{4}$. Berilgan shaklga solishtirsak,
$k=120$.

**22. Javob: $4.5$ (yoki $\frac{9}{2}$).** $13$-yanvar $11$-yanvardan $2$ kun keyin —
grafikda $x=2$ da $y=6$. $15$-yanvar $4$ kun keyin — $x=4$ da $y=15$. O'rtacha o'zgarish
tezligi $=\frac{15-6}{4-2}=\frac{9}{2}=4.5$ soat/kun.

## Module 2 (Harder)

**1. Javob: B ($7$).** Tartiblangan qatorda ($4,7,7,9,13$) o'rtadagi qiymat — $5$ ta sondan
$3$-chisi — $7$.

**2. Javob: B ($55$).** To'ldiruvchi burchaklar yig'indisi $90°$. $90-35=55$.

**3. Javob: A ($45+15h\le150$).** Boshlang'ich to'lov $\$45$ ga, har soat uchun qo'shimcha
$\$15h$. Jami xarajat $150$ dan oshmasligi kerak: $45+15h\le150$.

**4. Javob: A ($(x-4)(x-5)$).** $-4$ va $-5$ ning ko'paytmasi $20$, yig'indisi $-9$ — bu
$x^2-9x+20$ ning koeffitsientlariga mos keladi. Demak, $(x-4)(x-5)$.

**5. Javob: $6$.** $3x-2y=16$ va $x+2y=8$. Ikkala tenglamani qo'shamiz: $4x=24$, demak
$x=6$.

**6. Javob: D ($24$).** $p(5)=2^5=32$, $p(3)=2^3=8$. $32-8=24$.

**7. Javob: C ($15$).** Pifagor teoremasi: $\sqrt{9^2+12^2}=\sqrt{81+144}=\sqrt{225}=15$
(bu $3$-$4$-$5$ uchburchakning $3$ barobar kattalashtirilgan versiyasi).

**8. Javob: C ($86.8$).** Jami ball $=20(82)+30(90)=1640+2700=4340$. O'rtacha
$=\frac{4340}{50}=86.8$.

**9. Javob: C ($x=8$ yoki $x=-2$).** $|2x-6|=10$ ikki holatni beradi: $2x-6=10$, bundan
$x=8$; yoki $2x-6=-10$, bundan $x=-2$.

**10. Javob: $13$.** Chalkesma ko'paytirish: $5(x+2)=3(2x-1)$, ya'ni $5x+10=6x-3$. Bundan
$13=x$.

**11. Javob: C ($90$).** $a+c=150$ va $12a+8c=1560$, bunda $a$ — kattalar chiptasi, $c$ —
bolalar chiptasi. $c=150-a$ ni ikkinchi tenglamaga qo'yamiz: $12a+8(150-a)=1560$, ya'ni
$4a+1200=1560$, bundan $a=90$.

**12. Javob: C ($10$).** $y=-2(x-3)^2+7$ cho'qqi shaklida ($a<0$, demak maksimum), cho'qqi
nuqtasi $(h,k)=(3,7)$. $h+k=3+7=10$.

**13. Javob: B ($5$).** $5x-3y=17$ va $2x+y=9$. Ikkinchi tenglamadan $y=9-2x$. Birinchisiga
qo'yamiz: $5x-3(9-2x)=17$, ya'ni $5x-27+6x=17$, $11x=44$, $x=4$. Demak, $y=9-2(4)=1$.
$x+y=4+1=5$.

**14. Javob: C ($10$).** Ildizlarning ko'paytmasi $=\frac{c}{a}=24$. Bitta ildiz $4$ bo'lsa,
ikkinchisi $24\div4=6$. Ildizlarning yig'indisi $=\frac{-b}{a}$, ya'ni $k=4+6=10$ (Vieta
formulasi — Book 3 Lesson 4 bilan bog'liq).

**15. Javob: $6$.** Cheksiz ko'p yechim uchun koeffitsientlar proportsional bo'lishi kerak:
$\frac{6}{4}=\frac{9}{k}$. Chalkesma ko'paytirish: $6k=36$, demak $k=6$. (Ozod hadlarni
tekshiramiz: $\frac{15}{10}=1.5=\frac{6}{4}$ ✓ — rost cheksiz ko'p yechim.)

**16. Javob: C ($12$).** $(x-5)^2=49$, ikkala tomondan ildiz olamiz: $x-5=\pm7$. Musbat
yechim: $x=5+7=12$.

**17. Javob: $-34$.** Ikkala tenglamani standart shaklga keltiramiz: $48x-102y=24$ va
$16x+ry=\frac16$. Parallel bo'lishi uchun koeffitsientlar proportsional: $\frac{16}{48}=
\frac13$, demak $r=\frac13(-102)=-34$.

**18. Javob: $336$.** $g(x)=0$ bo'lishi uchun $x=-14$ yoki $x=t$. $(24,0)$ nuqtasidan
o'tgani uchun $t=24$. $g(x)=(x+14)(24-x)$. $g(0)=(0+14)(24-0)=14\times24=336$.

**19. Javob: C ($100$).** $a+s=200$ va $25a+10s=3500$. $a=200-s$ ni qo'yamiz:
$25(200-s)+10s=3500$, ya'ni $5000-15s=3500$, bundan $15s=1500$, $s=100$.

**20. Javob: $12$.** Aynan bitta yechim uchun diskriminant $=0$: $b^2-4(1)(36)=0$, ya'ni
$b^2=144$. $b$ musbat bo'lgani uchun $b=12$ (Book 3 Lesson 3'dagi diskriminant mavzusi
bilan bog'liq).

**21. Javob: $36$.** $7$ ta sonning yig'indisi $=7\times20=140$. $8$ ta sonning yig'indisi
$=8\times22=176$. $8$-son $=176-140=36$.

**22. Javob: D ($\frac{38}{15}$).** $\angle B=90°$ va $BD$ — balandlik, demak
$\triangle ABC \sim \triangle BDC$ (burchak-burchak o'xshashligi). $AC=AB+23=15+23=38$.
O'xshashlikdan $\frac{AC}{AB}=\frac{BC}{BD}$, ya'ni $\frac{BC}{BD}=\frac{38}{15}$.
