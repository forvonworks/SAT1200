*(A calculator is allowed on every question. A geometry reference sheet — circle/rectangle/triangle area, prism/cylinder/cone/sphere volume, the Pythagorean theorem, 30-60-90 and 45-45-90 triangle ratios, 360°/2π facts — is available throughout this module.)*

**1.** Solve for $x$: $3x + 7 = 22$.

A) 3  B) 5  C) 7  D) 9

**2.** If $f(x) = x^2 - 4$, what is $f(3)$?

A) $-1$  B) 5  C) 13  D) 23

**3.** A rectangle has length 12 and width 5. What is its area?

A) 17  B) 34  C) 60  D) 120

**4.** A jar contains 8 red marbles and 12 blue marbles. What is the probability of selecting a red marble at random? Express your answer as a fraction.

A) $\frac{2}{5}$  B) $\frac{3}{5}$  C) $\frac{2}{3}$  D) $\frac{8}{12}$ (not in lowest terms)

**5.** If $f(x) = 2x - 5$, what is $f(4)$?

A) $-3$  B) 3  C) 8  D) 13

**6.** Which of the following is equivalent to $3(x+2)^2$?

A) $3x^2 + 12$  B) $3x^2 + 6x + 12$  C) $3x^2 + 12x + 12$  D) $3x^2 + 12x + 4$

**7.** Which value of $x$ satisfies $2(x-3) = 10$?

A) 5  B) 6.5  C) 7  D) 8

**8.** If $2^x = 32$, what is the value of $x$?

A) 4  B) 5  C) 6  D) 16

**9.** A company that creates and sells tape dispensers calculates its monthly profit, in dollars, by subtracting its fixed monthly costs, in dollars, from its monthly sales revenue, in dollars. The equation $15{,}000 = 2.00x - 4{,}500$ represents this situation for a month where $x$ tape dispensers are created and sold. Which statement is the best interpretation of $2.00x$ in this context?

A) The monthly sales revenue, in dollars, from selling $x$ tape dispensers
B) The monthly sales revenue, in dollars, from each tape dispenser sold
C) The monthly cost, in dollars, of creating each tape dispenser
D) The monthly cost, in dollars, of creating $x$ tape dispensers

**10.** The function $g$ is defined by $g(x) = (x-3)(x+7)$. What is the sum of the solutions to $g(x) = 0$? (Grid-in.)

**11.** An angle has a measure of $\dfrac{9\pi}{20}$ radians. What is the measure of the angle in degrees? (Grid-in.)

**12.** The function $h$ is defined by $h(x) = -3x + 12$. If $h(k) = -6$, what is the value of $k$?

A) $-2$  B) 2  C) 6  D) 18

**13.** $w^2 + 12w - 40 = 0$. Which of the following is a solution to the given equation?

A) $6 - 2\sqrt{19}$  B) $2\sqrt{19}$  C) $\sqrt{19}$  D) $-6 + 2\sqrt{19}$

**14.** A line in the $xy$-plane passes through the points $(2, 5)$ and $(6, 17)$. What is the slope of the line?

A) $\frac{1}{3}$  B) 3  C) 4  D) 12

**15.** $(x-3)^2 + (y+16)^2 = 289$. The graph of the given equation is a circle in the $xy$-plane. The point $(q, s)$ lies on the circle. Which of the following is **NOT** a possible value of $q$?

A) $-17$  B) $-3$  C) 3  D) 17

**16.** If $x^2 + 6x + c = (x+3)^2$, what is the value of $c$? (Grid-in.)

**17.** If $4x - 9 = 2x + 15$, what is the value of $3x$?

A) 30  B) 33  C) 36  D) 39

**18.** Which expression is equivalent to $(2x-3)(x+4)$?

A) $2x^2 + 5x - 12$  B) $2x^2 - 5x - 12$  C) $2x^2 + x - 12$  D) $2x^2 + 5x + 12$

**19.** The average (arithmetic mean) of 5 numbers is 18. If one number is removed, the average of the remaining 4 numbers is 20. What was the removed number? (Grid-in.)

**20.** $4x - 6y = 10y + 2$ and $ty = \frac{1}{2} + 2x$. In the given system of equations, $t$ is a constant. If the system has no solution, what is the value of $t$? (Grid-in.)

**21.** $y = x^2 + 2x + 1$ and $x + y + 1 = 0$. If $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$ are the two solutions to the system of equations above, what is the value of $y_1 + y_2$?

A) $-3$  B) $-2$  C) $-1$  D) 1

**22.** For 100 buttons, the table below summarizes the distribution by group and diameter.

| Group | Diameter $<20$ mm | Diameter $20$–$30$ mm | Diameter $>30$ mm |
|---|---|---|---|
| Group 1 | 12 | 8 | 3 |
| Group 2 | 0 | 17 | 18 |
| Group 3 | 10 | 32 | 0 |

One of these buttons will be selected at random. What is the probability of selecting a button with a diameter that is less than or equal to 30 millimeters, given that it is **not** in Group 2? Express your answer as a decimal or fraction, not as a percent. (Grid-in.)
