# Mock Test 10 — Module 2

**Vaqt / Time:** 35 daqiqa / 35 minutes — **22 ta savol / 22 questions**

*Bu — qiyinroq (harder-routed) versiya: Module 1'da yaxshi natija ko'rsatgan talaba oladigan versiya. Kalkulyator va geometriya formulalari varag'i Module 1'dagi kabi mavjud.*

**1.** A circle has radius 7. What is its circumference in terms of $\pi$?

A) $7\pi$  B) $14\pi$  C) $28\pi$  D) $49\pi$

**2.** A survey of 50 students found that 30 prefer tea. What percent of students prefer tea?

A) 30%  B) 40%  C) 50%  D) 60%

**3.** If $5(x+2) - 3x = 2x + 10$, how many solutions does this equation have?

A) No solution  B) Exactly one solution  C) Exactly two solutions  D) Infinitely many solutions

**4.** The function $h$ is defined by $h(x) = 3(x-2)^2 + 5$. What is the minimum value of $h$?

A) $-5$  B) 2  C) 3  D) 5

**5.** If $f(x) = x + 6$ and $g(x) = 6x$, what is the value of $7f(2) - g(2)$?

A) $-4$  B) 8  C) 42  D) 44

**6.** The function $f$ is defined by $f(x) = 5\left(\frac{1}{4} - x\right)^2 + \frac{11}{4}$. What is the value of $f\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)$? (Grid-in.)

**7.** A line has slope $-2$ and passes through $(3, 4)$. What is the $y$-intercept of the line?

A) $-10$  B) $-2$  C) 4  D) 10

**8.** Which of the following is equivalent to $4x^2 - 25$?

A) $(2x-5)(2x+5)$  B) $(4x-5)(x+5)$  C) $(2x-25)(2x+1)$  D) $(x-5)(4x+5)$

**9.** A data set has a mean of 24 and a standard deviation of 3. Which value is exactly 2 standard deviations above the mean?

A) 21  B) 27  C) 30  D) 33

**10.** In a right triangle, one leg has length 9 and the hypotenuse has length 15. What is the length of the other leg? (Grid-in.)

**11.** If $3x - 5 \leq 16$ and $x$ is a positive integer, what is the greatest possible value of $x$?

A) 5  B) 6  C) 7  D) 8

**12.** If $2^{x+1} = 16$, what is the value of $x$?

A) 2  B) 3  C) 4  D) 7

**13.** The graph of the equation $ax + ky = 6$ is a line in the $xy$-plane, where $a$ and $k$ are constants. If the line contains the points $(-2, -6)$ and $(0, -3)$, what is the value of $k$?

A) $-2$  B) $-1$  C) 2  D) 3

**14.** The first term of a sequence is 9. Each term after the first is 4 times the preceding term. If $w$ represents the $n$th term of the sequence, which equation gives $w$ in terms of $n$?

A) $w = 4(9^n)$  B) $w = 4(9^{n-1})$  C) $w = 9(4^n)$  D) $w = 9(4^{n-1})$

**15.** For the function $f$, if $f(3x) = x - 6$ for all values of $x$, what is the value of $f(6)$?

A) $-6$  B) $-4$  C) 0  D) 2

**16.** If $a = c + d$, which of the following is equivalent to the expression $x^2 - c^2 - 2cd - d^2$?

A) $(x+a)^2$  B) $(x-a)^2$  C) $(x+a)(x-a)$  D) $x^2 - ax - a^2$

**17.** In the system of equations below, $a$ is a constant.
$$6x - 9y = 15$$
$$ax - 3y = 5$$
If the system has infinitely many solutions, what is the value of $a$?

A) $-2$  B) 2  C) 3  D) 6

**18.** For what value of $k$ does the equation $x^2 + 8x + k = 0$ have exactly one real solution?

A) $-16$  B) 8  C) 16  D) 32

**19.** The equation $x^2 - kx + 36 = 0$ has two positive integer solutions. If the sum of the solutions is 13, what is the value of $k$? (Grid-in.)

**20.** The function $f$ is defined by $f(x) = 2x^2 - 5x + 1$. For some value of $a$, $f(a) = f(a+1)$. What is the value of $a$? (Grid-in.)

**21.** Two data sets of 23 integers each are summarized below. For each set, the first interval represents the frequency of integers greater than or equal to 10, but less than 20; the second interval represents the frequency of integers greater than or equal to 20, but less than 30; and so on.

**Data Set A**

| Interval | 20–29 | 30–39 | 40–49 | 50–59 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 3 | 4 | 7 | 9 |

**Data Set B**

| Interval | 10–19 | 20–29 | 30–39 | 40–49 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 3 | 4 | 7 | 9 |

What is the smallest possible difference between the mean of Data Set A and the mean of Data Set B?

A) 0  B) 1  C) 10  D) 23

**22.** In the figure, right triangle $ADE$ contains point $C$ on side $AE$ and point $B$ on side $AD$, such that $BC$ is drawn perpendicular to $AE$ at $C$, and angle $ACB$ and angle $AED$ are both right angles (so $BC \parallel DE$). Point $C$ lies between $A$ and $E$ on segment $AE$. $AB = \sqrt{34}$ units, $AC = 3$ units, and $CE = 21$ units. What is the area, in square units, of triangle $ADE$? (Grid-in.)
