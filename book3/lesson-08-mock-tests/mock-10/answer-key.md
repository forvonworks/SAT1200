## Module 1

**1. Javob: B) 5**

$3x+7=22$. Ikkala tomondan $7$ ni ayiramiz: $3x=15$. Ikkala tomonni $3$ ga bo'lamiz: $x=5$.

**2. Javob: B) 5**

$f(x)=x^2-4$. $f(3)=3^2-4=9-4=5$.

**3. Javob: C) 60**

To'g'ri to'rtburchak yuzasi $=$ uzunlik $\times$ kenglik $=12\times5=60$.

**4. Javob: A) $\frac{2}{5}$**

Jami shar $=8+12=20$. Qizil shar ehtimoli $=\frac{8}{20}=\frac{2}{5}$.

**5. Javob: C) 8**

$f(x)=2x-5$. $f(4)=2(4)-5=8-5=3$... *(tekshirib ko'ring: to'g'ri javob $3$, B band)*

**Javobni tuzatish: B) 3.** $f(4)=2(4)-5=3$.

**6. Javob: C) $3x^2+12x+12$**

$3(x+2)^2=3(x^2+4x+4)=3x^2+12x+12$.

**7. Javob: D) 8**

$2(x-3)=10$. Ikkala tomonni $2$ ga bo'lamiz: $x-3=5$. Demak, $x=8$.

**8. Javob: B) 5**

$2^x=32=2^5$. Darajalar teng bo'lgani uchun $x=5$.

**9. Javob: A) The monthly sales revenue, in dollars, from selling $x$ tape dispensers**

Shartga ko'ra, oylik foyda $=$ savdo daromadi $-$ xarajatlar. Tenglamada $2.00x$ — savdo daromadi qismi, $4{,}500$ — xarajatlar qismi. Demak, $2.00x$ — $x$ ta dispenser sotishdan tushgan oylik daromad.

**10. Javob: $-4$**

$g(x)=(x-3)(x+7)=0$ bo'lsa, $x=3$ yoki $x=-7$. Yig'indisi: $3+(-7)=-4$.

**11. Javob: $81$**

Radiandan darajaga: $\dfrac{9\pi}{20}\times\dfrac{180°}{\pi}=\dfrac{9\times180}{20}=81°$.

**12. Javob: C) 6**

$h(x)=-3x+12$. $h(k)=-6$: $-3k+12=-6$. Bundan $-3k=-18$, demak $k=6$.

**13. Javob: D) $-6+2\sqrt{19}$**

$w^2+12w-40=0$. To'liq kvadratga keltiramiz: $w^2+12w=40$, $w^2+12w+36=76$, $(w+6)^2=76$. Kvadrat ildiz: $w+6=\pm2\sqrt{19}$. Demak $w=-6\pm2\sqrt{19}$. Javoblar orasida faqat $-6+2\sqrt{19}$ bor.

**14. Javob: B) 3**

Nishablik $=\dfrac{17-5}{6-2}=\dfrac{12}{4}=3$.

**15. Javob: A) $-17$**

Tenglama $(x-3)^2+(y+16)^2=289=17^2$ — markaz $(3,-16)$, radius $17$. $q$ markazning $x$-koordinatasidan ko'pi bilan $17$ birlik uzoqda bo'lishi kerak: $3-17=-14 \le q \le 3+17=20$. $-17$ bu oraliqdan tashqarida.

**16. Javob: $9$**

$(x+3)^2=x^2+6x+9$. Demak $c=9$.

**17. Javob: C) 36**

$4x-9=2x+15$. Bundan $2x=24$, $x=12$. $3x=3\times12=36$.

**18. Javob: A) $2x^2+5x-12$**

$(2x-3)(x+4)=2x^2+8x-3x-12=2x^2+5x-12$.

**19. Javob: $10$**

5 sonning yig'indisi $=5\times18=90$. 4 sonning yig'indisi $=4\times20=80$. Olib tashlangan son $=90-80=10$.

**20. Javob: $8$**

Ikkinchi tenglamani $-2$ ga ko'paytiramiz: $-2ty=-1-4x$. Birinchi tenglama bilan qo'shamiz va $y$ koeffitsientlarini tenglashtiramiz (tizim yechimsiz bo'lishi uchun): $-16=-2t$, demak $t=8$.

**21. Javob: D) 1**

Ikkinchi tenglamadan $y=-x-1$. Birinchisiga qo'yamiz: $-x-1=x^2+2x+1$, bundan $x^2+3x+2=0$, $(x+2)(x+1)=0$. $x=-2$ yoki $x=-1$. Mos $y$ qiymatlari: $y=1$ va $y=0$. $y_1+y_2=1+0=1$.

**22. Javob: $\dfrac{62}{65}$ (yoki $0.9538$)**

Guruh 2'da bo'lmagan tugmalar — Guruh 1 va Guruh 3. Guruh 1: $12+8=20$ ta ($\le30$mm). Guruh 3: $10+32=42$ ta. Jami $\le30$mm va Guruh 2'da emas: $20+42=62$. Guruh 2'da bo'lmagan jami: $(12+8+3)+(10+32+0)=23+42=65$. Ehtimollik $=\dfrac{62}{65}$.

## Module 2

**1. Javob: B) $14\pi$**

Aylana uzunligi $=2\pi r=2\pi(7)=14\pi$.

**2. Javob: D) 60%**

$\dfrac{30}{50}\times100=60\%$.

**3. Javob: D) Infinitely many solutions**

$5(x+2)-3x=5x+10-3x=2x+10$. O'ng tomon ham $2x+10$. Ikkala tomon ayniy teng — har qanday $x$ uchun to'g'ri, demak cheksiz ko'p yechim.

**4. Javob: D) 5**

$h(x)=3(x-2)^2+5$ — vertex shaklida, minimum $x=2$ da, qiymati $5$ (chunki $3(x-2)^2\ge0$).

**5. Javob: D) 44**

$f(2)=2+6=8$. $g(2)=6(2)=12$. $7f(2)-g(2)=7(8)-12=56-12=44$.

**6. Javob: $\dfrac{11}{4}$ (yoki $2.75$)**

$f\left(\frac14\right)=5\left(\frac14-\frac14\right)^2+\frac{11}{4}=5(0)^2+\frac{11}{4}=\frac{11}{4}$.

**7. Javob: D) 10**

$y=mx+b$: $4=-2(3)+b$, $4=-6+b$, demak $b=10$.

**8. Javob: A) $(2x-5)(2x+5)$**

$4x^2-25$ — kvadratlar ayirmasi: $(2x)^2-5^2=(2x-5)(2x+5)$.

**9. Javob: C) 30**

O'rtacha qiymatdan 2 ta standart og'ish yuqorida: $24+2(3)=24+6=30$.

**10. Javob: $12$**

Pifagor teoremasi: boshqa katet $=\sqrt{15^2-9^2}=\sqrt{225-81}=\sqrt{144}=12$.

**11. Javob: C) 7**

$3x-5\le16$. Bundan $3x\le21$, $x\le7$. Eng katta butun son $=7$.

**12. Javob: B) 3**

$2^{x+1}=16=2^4$. Demak $x+1=4$, $x=3$.

**13. Javob: A) $-2$**

$(0,-3)$ nuqtasi $y$-kesishma: $-3=\dfrac{6}{k}$ (chunki $ax+ky=6$ dan $y=-\frac{a}{k}x+\frac{6}{k}$, $x=0$ da $y=\frac6k$). Bundan $k=-2$.

**14. Javob: D) $w=9(4^{n-1})$**

$n=1$ da $w=9$. Har keyingi had oldingisining $4$ barobari. Demak $w=9\times4^{n-1}$.

**15. Javob: B) $-4$**

$f(3x)=x-6$. $3x=6$ bo'lsa $x=2$. $f(6)=f(3\times2)=2-6=-4$.

**16. Javob: C) $(x+a)(x-a)$**

$a=c+d$ bo'lsa, $x^2-c^2-2cd-d^2=x^2-(c^2+2cd+d^2)=x^2-(c+d)^2=x^2-a^2=(x+a)(x-a)$.

**17. Javob: B) 2**

Birinchi tenglamani $3$ ga bo'lamiz: $2x-3y=5$. Ikkinchisi: $ax-3y=5$. Cheksiz ko'p yechim uchun ikkala tenglama bir xil bo'lishi kerak: $a=2$.

**18. Javob: C) 16**

Aniqlovchi $=0$ bo'lganda bitta yechim: $8^2-4(1)(k)=0$, $64-4k=0$, $k=16$.

**19. Javob: $13$**

Yig'indisi $13$, ko'paytmasi $36$ bo'lgan ikkita musbat butun son: $4$ va $9$ ($4+9=13$, $4\times9=36$). Vieta formulasiga ko'ra, yig'indi $=k$. Demak $k=13$.

**20. Javob: $\dfrac{3}{4}$ (yoki $0.75$)**

$f(a)=2a^2-5a+1$. $f(a+1)=2(a+1)^2-5(a+1)+1=2a^2-a-2$. Tenglashtiramiz: $2a^2-5a+1=2a^2-a-2$. Bundan $-5a+1=-a-2$, $-4a=-3$, $a=\frac34$.

**21. Javob: B) 1**

Ikkala to'plam bir xil shaklga ega, lekin A to'plami B'dan $10$ birlik yuqoriroq diapazonda. Eng kichik o'rtacha farq $=\left|\dfrac{3(20)+4(30)+7(40)+9(50)}{23}-\dfrac{3(19)+4(29)+7(39)+9(49)}{23}\right|=\dfrac{3(1)+4(1)+7(1)+9(1)}{23}=\dfrac{23}{23}=1$.

**22. Javob: $480$**

$\angle ACB=\angle AED=90°$, $\angle A$ ikkala uchburchakda umumiy — demak $\triangle ABC\sim\triangle ADE$ (AA). $AE=AC+CE=3+21=24$. Mos tomonlar nisbati: $\dfrac{AD}{AB}=\dfrac{AE}{AC}=\dfrac{24}{3}=8$. Demak $AD=8\sqrt{34}$. Pifagor teoremasi ($\triangle ADE$ to'g'ri burchakli): $DE^2+AE^2=AD^2$, $DE^2+576=64(34)=2176$, $DE^2=1600$, $DE=40$. Yuza $=\frac12(AE)(DE)=\frac12(24)(40)=480$.
