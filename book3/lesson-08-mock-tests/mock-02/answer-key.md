**1. Javob: C) 9.** $5x-8=37$. Ikkala tomonga $+8$: $5x=45$. Ikkala tomonni $5$ ga bo'lamiz: $x=9$.

Parallel chiziqlar kesuvchi bilan kesilganda, mos burchaklar doim teng. Demak, $w=170$.

**7. Javob: C) 17.** $f(4)=3(4)+5=12+5=17$.

**8. Javob: C) 21.** $g(5)=5^2-4=25-4=21$.

**9. Javob: D) $h(x)=-\frac{7}{2}x+113$.** Nuqtalar $(28,15)$ va $(26,22)$ orqali qiyalik: $m=\dfrac{22-15}{26-28}=\dfrac{7}{-2}=-\dfrac{7}{2}$. $y=mx+b$ ga $(28,15)$ ni qo'yamiz: $15=-\frac{7}{2}(28)+b=-98+b$, demak $b=113$.

**16. Javob: C) 5.** Qiyalik $-\frac{2}{3}$, nuqta $(6,1)$. $y=mx+b$: $1=-\frac{2}{3}(6)+b=-4+b$, demak $b=5$.

## Module 2

**1. Javob: A) $p=\dfrac{2}{3}s$.** $s=\frac{3}{2}p$. Ikkala tomonni $\frac{3}{2}$ ning teskarisiga ($\frac{2}{3}$) ko'paytiramiz: $\frac{2}{3}s=p$.

**2. Javob: 42.** $(x+6)$ ikkala hadda umumiy ko'paytuvchi: $(x+6)\left(\frac13-\frac12\right)=-8$, ya'ni $(x+6)\left(-\frac16\right)=-8$. Ikkala tomonni $-6$ ga ko'paytiramiz: $x+6=48$, demak $x=42$.

**3. Javob: C) 15.** $f(-2)=2(-2)^2-3(-2)+1=2(4)+6+1=8+6+1=15$.

**4. Javob: B) 5.** Ikkala tenglamani qo'shamiz: $(x+y)+(2x-y)=10+5$, $3x=15$, $x=5$. Birinchi tenglamaga qo'yamiz: $5+y=10$, $y=5$.

**5. Javob: C) $\dfrac{1}{2}$.** Ehtimollik $=\dfrac{\text{qoyali sayyoralar soni}}{\text{jami sayyoralar soni}}=\dfrac{4}{8}=\dfrac{1}{2}$.

**6. Javob: A) $x^2-16$.** Bu — kvadratlar ayirmasi formulasi: $(x-4)(x+4)=x^2-16$.

**7. Javob: B) 2.** Qiyalik $=\dfrac{13-5}{6-2}=\dfrac{8}{4}=2$.

**8. Javob: D) 162.** $XYZ$ uchburchagi $TUV$ ga o'xshash, masshtab koeffitsienti $k=\dfrac{333}{37}=9$. $\overline{TU}\leftrightarrow\overline{XY}$, demak $XY=18\times9=162$.

**9. Javob: C) 7.** $x^2=49$, demak $x=\pm7$. Musbat yechim $-7$.

**10. Javob: A) $x<2$.** $-4x+9>1$, demak $-4x>-8$. Ikkala tomonni $-4$ ga bo'lganda (manfiy son!) tengsizlik belgisi aylanadi: $x<2$.

**11. Javob: $\frac{1}{14}$ (yoki $0.0714$).** $14$ ta uchdan faqat bittasi $D$ harfi bilan belgilangan, demak ehtimollik $\dfrac{1}{14}$.

**12. Javob: 162.** $162°$ va $x°$ — ikkala parallel chiziqda bir xil nisbiy holatda, demak **mos burchaklar**, va ular teng: $x=162$.

**13. Javob: $-12$.** $h(x)=f(x)+g(x)=\left(\frac14x-9\right)+\left(\frac34x+21\right)=x+12$. $x$-kesishma — $y=0$ bo'lgan nuqta: $0=x+12$, demak $x=-12$.

**14. Javob: B) 2.** $p(x)=x(x^2-6x+9)=x(x-3)^2$. Ildizlar: $x=0$ va $x=3$ (ikkinchisi — qo'sh ildiz). Jami **ikki xil** qiymat.

**15. Javob: B) 4.** Ikkinchi tenglama birinchisining $2$ karrasi bo'lishi kerak: $6x=2(3x)$ ✓, $8y=2(ky)$ demak $k=4$, va $24=2(12)$ ✓. Cheksiz ko'p yechim uchun $k=4$.

**16. Javob: $-324$.** $y$-kesishma $(0,-323)$ demak $f(0)=-323$. $f(0)=a^0+b=1+b=-323$, demak $b=-324$.

**17. Javob: A) $-10$.** Yechim yo'q bo'lishi uchun, ikkinchi tenglamaning chap tomoni birinchisining karrasi bo'lishi, lekin o'ng tomoni mos kelmasligi kerak: $4x=2(2x)$ ✓, demak $my=2(-5y)$, ya'ni $m=-10$.

**18. Javob: B) 1,300.** $140=10+\frac{p}{100}(10)$, demak $130=0.1p$, $p=1300$.

**19. Javob: A) 0.** Diskriminant $D=4^2-4(1)(9)=16-36=-20<0$. Diskriminant manfiy bo'lsa, haqiqiy yechim yo'q.

**20. Javob: C) 6.** Kichik son $=s$, katta son $=l$. $s+l=20$ va $3s=l+4$, demak $l=3s-4$. $s+(3s-4)=20$, $4s=24$, $s=6$.

**21. Javob: C) 12.** $(x+a)^2=x^2+2ax+a^2$. $a^2=36$, $a$ musbat bo'lgani uchun $a=6$. $b=2a=12$.
