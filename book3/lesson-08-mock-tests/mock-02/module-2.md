**1.** An oceanographer uses the equation $s = \dfrac{3}{2}p$ to model the speed $s$, in knots, of an ocean wave, where $p$ represents the period of the wave, in seconds. Which of the following represents the period of the wave in terms of the speed of the wave?

A) $p = \dfrac{2}{3}s$  B) $p = \dfrac{3}{2}s$  C) $p = \dfrac{2}{3} + s$  D) $p = \dfrac{3}{2} + s$

**2.** What value of $x$ is the solution to the given equation?
$$\frac{1}{3}(x+6) - \frac{1}{2}(x+6) = -8$$

**3.** If $f(x) = 2x^2 - 3x + 1$, what is the value of $f(-2)$?

A) 3  B) 11  C) 15  D) 23

**4.** If $x + y = 10$ and $2x - y = 5$, what is the value of $y$?

A) 3  B) 5  C) 7  D) 10

**5.** Of the 8 planets in our solar system, 4 are considered rocky. If a student randomly selects 1 of those 8 planets as a topic for a report, what is the probability that the selected planet will be rocky?

A) $\dfrac{1}{8}$  B) $\dfrac{1}{4}$  C) $\dfrac{1}{2}$  D) 2

**6.** Which of the following is equivalent to $(x-4)(x+4)$?

A) $x^2 - 16$  B) $x^2 + 16$  C) $x^2 - 8x + 16$  D) $x^2 - 8$

**7.** A line passes through the points $(2, 5)$ and $(6, 13)$. What is the slope of the line?

A) $\dfrac{1}{2}$  B) 2  C) 4  D) 8

**8.** The table gives the perimeters of similar triangles $TUV$ and $XYZ$, where $\overline{TU}$ corresponds to $\overline{XY}$. The length of $\overline{TU}$ is 18.

| | Perimeter |
|---|---|
| Triangle $TUV$ | 37 |
| Triangle $XYZ$ | 333 |

What is the length of $\overline{XY}$?

A) 2  B) 18  C) 55  D) 162

**9.** What is the positive solution to $x^2 - 49 = 0$?

A) $-7$  B) 0  C) 7  D) 49

**10.** If $-4x + 9 > 1$, which of the following describes all possible values of $x$?

A) $x < 2$  B) $x > 2$  C) $x < -2$  D) $x > -2$

**11.** Each vertex of a 14-sided polygon is labeled with one of the 14 letters $A$ through $N$, with a different letter at each vertex. If one vertex is selected at random, what is the probability that the letter $D$ will be at the selected vertex? (Express your answer as a decimal or fraction, not as a percent.)

**12.** Line $n$ intersects two parallel lines, $r$ (upper) and $s$ (lower), running from lower-left to upper-right (so $n$ crosses $r$ above where it crosses $s$). At the point where $n$ crosses $r$, the angle marked $162°$ sits between $n$ and $r$, opening toward the lower-left. At the point where $n$ crosses $s$, the angle marked $x°$ sits in the same relative position (between $n$ and $s$, opening toward the lower-left). What is the value of $x$?

**13.** The functions $f$ and $g$ are defined as $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{4}x - 9$ and $g(x) = \dfrac{3}{4}x + 21$. If the function $h$ is defined as $h(x) = f(x) + g(x)$, what is the $x$-coordinate of the $x$-intercept of the graph of $y = h(x)$ in the $xy$-plane?

**14.** The function $p$ is defined by $p(x) = x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x$. For how many distinct values of $x$ does $p(x) = 0$?

A) 1  B) 2  C) 3  D) 4

**15.** For what value of $k$ does the system of equations below have infinitely many solutions?
$$3x + ky = 12$$
$$6x + 8y = 24$$

A) 2  B) 4  C) 6  D) 8

**16.** The function $f$ is defined by $f(x) = a^x + b$, where $a$ and $b$ are constants. In the $xy$-plane, the graph of $y = f(x)$ has an $x$-intercept at $(2, 0)$ and a $y$-intercept at $(0, -323)$. What is the value of $b$?

**17.** A system of equations is given below:
$$2x - 5y = 7$$
$$4x + my = -3$$
For what value of $m$ does this system have no solution?

A) $-10$  B) $-5$  C) 5  D) 10

**18.** $140$ is $p\%$ greater than $10$. What is the value of $p$?

A) 1,400  B) 1,300  C) 140  D) 130

**19.** For how many real values of $x$ does the equation $x^2 + 4x + 9 = 0$ have solutions?

A) 0  B) 1  C) 2  D) 4

**20.** The sum of two numbers is 20. Three times the smaller number is 4 more than the larger number. What is the smaller number?

A) 4  B) 5  C) 6  D) 8

**21.** If $x^2 + bx + 36$ is equivalent to $(x+a)^2$ for positive constants $a$ and $b$, what is the value of $b$?

A) 6  B) 9  C) 12  D) 36

**22.** In right triangle $ABC$, angle $C$ is the right angle, $AC = 9$, and $BC = 12$. Point $D$ lies on $\overline{AB}$ such that $\overline{CD}$ is the altitude from $C$ to the hypotenuse. What is the length of $\overline{CD}$?

A) 6  B) 7.2  C) 7.5  D) 10.8
