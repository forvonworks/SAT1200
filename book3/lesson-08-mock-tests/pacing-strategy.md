# Book 3 — Lesson 8: Full-Length Mock Tests — Module 1 Pacing Strategy

**Read this before your first mock.** It explains *why* these mocks are built the way they
are, and how to actually use that structure to improve your score — not just take 10 tests
and hope.

---

## Nega Module 1 — "darvozabon" (Module 1 is the gatekeeper)

Haqiqiy raqamli SAT'da Matematika bo'limi ikkita modulga bo'lingan: har biri 22 ta savol,
35 daqiqa. Bu — moslashuvchan (adaptive) tizim: **Module 1'dagi natijangiz Module 2'ning
qaysi versiyasini olishingizni hal qiladi** — qiyinroq versiya (yuqori balllarga yo'l ochadi)
yoki osonroq versiya (maksimal ball cheklanadi, taxminan 600 atrofida, garchi College Board
aniq chegarani e'lon qilmasa ham).

*(In the real Digital SAT, Math is split into two modules: 22 questions each, 35 minutes
each. This is an adaptive system: **your Module 1 performance decides which version of
Module 2 you get** — a harder version (which unlocks the top of the score scale) or an
easier version (which caps your maximum possible score, estimated around 600, though
College Board doesn't publish the exact cutoff).)*

**Amaliy xulosa:** 700+ ballga erishish uchun, siz Module 1'da tez va aniq ishlashingiz
kerak — hatto bu sizga "oson" tuyulsa ham. Module 1'da sekinlashish yoki xato qilish sizni
avtomatik ravishda Module 2'ning osonroq (demak, past balllik) versiyasiga yo'naltiradi,
Module 2'da qanchalik yaxshi ishlamang ham.

*(Practical implication: to reach 700+, you need to be fast and accurate on Module 1 — even
when it feels "easy." Slowing down or making mistakes on Module 1 automatically routes you
into Module 2's easier, lower-ceiling version, no matter how well you then do on Module 2.)*

## Bu mock testlar qanday qurilgan

Kitob (bosma shaklda) haqiqiy moslashuvchan tizimni takrorlay olmaydi — biz Module
2'ni real vaqtda sizning natijangizga qarab o'zgartira olmaymiz. Shuning uchun har bir mock
testda:

- **Module 1** — aralash qiyinlikda (asosan oson/o'rta, bir nechta qiyin savol) — bu
  haqiqiy Module 1'ning o'rtacha qiyinligini aks ettiradi.
- **Module 2** — **doimiy ravishda qiyinroq versiya** sifatida qurilgan — ya'ni, Module
  1'da yaxshi natija ko'rsatgan talaba oladigan versiya. Bunday qilishning sababi: agar
  siz 800 balga intilsangiz, aynan shu — qiyinroq — Module 2'ga mashq qilishingiz kerak.

*(The book can't replicate the real adaptive system — we can't change Module 2 in real
time based on your performance. So every mock test here fixes **Module 2 as the harder
version** — the one a student who did well on Module 1 would receive — because if you're
aiming for 800, that's the version you need to practice against.)*

## Modul ichida vaqtni taqsimlash (35 daqiqa, 22 savol)

| Bosqich | Vaqt | Strategiya |
|---|---|---|
| 1-savoldan 15-savolgacha | ~18 daqiqa (savol boshiga ~1.2 daqiqa) | Tez va ishonchli bajaring — bu savollar odatda to'g'ridan-to'g'ri. Shubha qilsangiz, belgilab o'ting (flag) va keyinroq qayting. |
| 16-savoldan 22-savolgacha | ~17 daqiqa (savol boshiga ~2.4 daqiqa) | Qolgan vaqtning ko'prog'ini shu yerga ajrating — qiyin savollar odatda oxirida joylashadi. |
| Oxirgi 2-3 daqiqa | — | Belgilangan (flagged) savollarga qayting, bo'sh qoldirilgan grid-in javoblarni tekshiring. |

*(Rough time budget: spend less time per question on the first ~15 (usually
straightforward — flag anything uncertain and move on), and noticeably more per question
on the last ~7 (harder items cluster toward the end of each module). Use the last 2-3
minutes to revisit flagged questions and double-check any blank grid-ins — an SAT grid-in
left blank is a guaranteed zero, a guess is not.)*

## Har bir mock testdan keyin

1. Javoblaringizni `answer-key.md` bilan tekshiring — nafaqat "to'g'ri/noto'g'ri", balki
   **har bir xato javobning sababini** o'qing (bu kitobning har bir javobi to'liq
   yechim bilan beriladi, shunchaki javob emas).
2. Qaysi domenda (Algebra, Advanced Math, Problem-Solving & Data Analysis, Geometry &
   Trigonometry) ko'proq xato qilganingizni kuzating — agar bitta domenda doimiy xato
   takrorlansa, tegishli Book 2 yoki Book 3 darsiga qayting.
3. Vaqtni kuzating: agar Module 2'ni tugata olmasangiz, bu ko'nikma emas, balki tezlik
   muammosi — shu holda avval tezlikka e'tibor bering, keyin qiyinroq mock'larga o'ting.

*(After each mock: check against the answer key and read WHY each wrong answer is wrong,
not just whether you got it right. Track which domain you're missing most in and revisit
the relevant Book 2/3 lesson. If you can't finish Module 2 in time, that's a speed problem,
not a knowledge gap — fix pacing before moving to the next mock.)*

## Tavsiya etilgan tartib

10 ta mock testni **ketma-ket, haftada 1-2 tadan** bajaring — hammasini bir hafta ichida
tugatish shoshqaloqlik va noto'g'ri xulosalarga olib keladi. Har bir mock orasida kamida
Book 1-3'dan kerakli darslarni qayta ko'rib chiqing.

*(Recommended pacing: take the 10 mocks over several weeks, 1-2 per week — taking all 10
in one week leads to rushed test-taking and misleading conclusions about where you stand.
Between mocks, revisit whatever Book 1-3 lesson your error pattern points to.)*
