- Stem: A construction crew has already laid 30 meters of drainage pipe. It lays 15 more meters each day, and the job requires a total of 150 meters. How many more days will the crew need to finish laying the pipe?

- Solution: The water forms a cone similar to the cup, with radius $9\cdot\frac{8}{12}=6$ and height 8, so $V=\frac13\pi(6^2)(8)=96\pi$. ## M2-Q17
- Domain: PSDA
- Difficulty: H
- Type: MC
- Stem: A bag holds 5 red tokens and $n$ blue tokens, where $n$ is a positive integer. Two tokens are drawn at random without replacement. The probability that the two tokens are the same color is $\frac{4}{9}$. What is the sum of all possible values of $n$?
- A: $4$
- B: $5$
- C: $9$
- D: $20$
- Answer: C
- Solution: $P(\text{same})=\frac{5\cdot4+n(n-1)}{(n+5)(n+4)}=\frac49$ gives $9n^2-9n+180=4n^2+36n+80$, so $5n^2-45n+100=0$ and $n^2-9n+20=0$, $n=4$ or $n=5$. Both work, so the sum is 9 (4 and 5 each find only one solution; 20 is their product).

## M2-Q18
- Domain: ALG
- Difficulty: H
- Type: GRID
- Stem: If $\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{6}{y}=4$ and $\dfrac{4}{x}-\dfrac{3}{y}=7$, what is the value of $x+y$?
- Answer: 7/2
- Solution: Let $u=\frac1x$ and $v=\frac1y$: $u+6v=4$ and $4u-3v=7$. Doubling the second and adding to the first gives $9u=18$, so $u=2$ and $v=\frac13$. Then $x=\frac12$, $y=3$, and $x+y=\frac72$.

## M2-Q20
- Domain: ADV
- Difficulty: H
- Type: MC
- Stem: What is the sum of all integer values of $k$ for which the equation $x^{2}-2kx+k+12=0$ has no real solutions?
- A: $6$
- B: $4$
- C: $0$
- D: $3$
- Answer: D
- Solution: No real solutions means a negative discriminant: $4k^2-4(k+12)<0$, so $k^2-k-12<0$ and $(k-4)(k+3)<0$, giving $-3<k<4$. The integers $-2,-1,0,1,2,3$ sum to 3 (4 includes the excluded endpoints $-3$ and $4$; 6 counts the integers; 0 assumes the interval is symmetric, $-3$ to $3$).

## M2-Q21
- Domain: GT
- Difficulty: H
- Type: GRID
- Stem: In the $xy$-plane, points $A(-1,2)$ and $B(7,k)$ are the endpoints of a diameter of a circle that is tangent to the $x$-axis. What is the value of $k$?
- Answer: 8
- Solution: The center is $\left(3,\frac{2+k}{2}\right)$ and the circle is tangent to the $x$-axis, so the radius equals the center's height: $\frac12AB=\frac{k+2}{2}$. Squaring, $64+(k-2)^2=(k+2)^2$, so $64=8k$ and $k=8$ (center $(3,5)$, radius 5).

## M2-Q22
- Domain: ADV
- Difficulty: H
- Type: MC
- Stem: The function $f$ is defined by $f(x)=x^{2}-6x+5$. What is the sum of all real solutions of the equation $f(f(x))=0$?
- A: $6$
- B: $12$
- C: $4$
- D: $18$
- Answer: B
- Solution: $f(f(x))=0$ means $f(x)=1$ or $f(x)=5$ (the zeros of $f$). $x^2-6x+4=0$ has two real solutions with sum 6, and $x^2-6x=0$ has solutions $0$ and $6$ with sum 6. The four solutions are distinct, so the total is 12 (6 uses only one of the two equations; 4 is the number of solutions; 18 counts one of the sums three times).
