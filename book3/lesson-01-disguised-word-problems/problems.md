# Book 3 — Lesson 1: Disguised Word Problems — Problem Sets

All problems are original. Every answer below is recomputed independently by `verify.py` (sympy), which prints OK/FAIL for each item, checks that exactly one choice is correct, and balances the correct letters. The digital SAT has a built-in Desmos graphing calculator, so a calculator is allowed on every problem. **Grid-in** = student-produced response: write a number (fractions and decimals are accepted; negative answers are allowed).

## Answer Key

Each entry: the answer, a short worked solution that uses the shortcut, and the trick used.

### Set 1 — Bridge: read the last sentence first

1. **C ($30$)** — The question asks for $s-a$, not $s$. From $a+s=120$ and $8a+5s=735$: $735-5(120)=3a=135$, so $a=45$ and $s=75$. The difference is $75-45=30$. (45 and 75 are the two counts; 15 is half the difference.)  *Trick: Read the last sentence first*

2. **D ($6$)** — The tip is not part of the fare: $21.50-3.00-3.50=15.00$ is the mileage charge, and $15\div2.50=6$. (7.2 forgets the tip, 7.4 forgets the starting fee, 8.6 divides the whole payment by the rate.)  *Trick: Remove what is not the fare*

3. **39** — For three consecutive numbers the middle one is the mean: $111\div3=37$. The three integers are 35, 37, 39, and the greatest is 39. (37 is the middle one.)  *Trick: Middle value = mean*

4. **A ($11$)** — ``Seven less than 5 times a number'' is $5n-7$, and ``3 times the sum'' is $3(n+5)$. So $5n-7=3n+15$, $2n=22$, $n=11$. (Writing $7-5n$ gives $-1$; $3n+5$ gives 6; $5n+7$ gives 4.)  *Trick: Translate sentence by sentence*

5. **40** — Profit \$780 needs $4.5c=990$, so $c=220$ cups. The question asks for the increase: $220-180=40$. (220 is the total, not the extra.)  *Trick: Read the last sentence first*

6. **B (\$14.00)** — Two extra hours cost $29-19=10$, so each hour is \$5 and the flat fee is $19-15=\$4$. Two hours cost $4+10=\$14$. (\$10 forgets the fee; \$24 is the price of a 4-hour rental; \$9.50 halves the 3-hour price.)  *Trick: Define variables with units*

7. **9** — Let Mei be $M$ now: $4M+6=2(M+6)$, so $2M=6$, $M=3$ and Jun is 12. The question asks for the age gap: $12-3=9$. (The gap never changes, so it is also $4M-M=3M$.)  *Trick: Read the last sentence first*

8. **D ($230$)** — Perimeter 66 gives $l+w=33$. With $l=2w+3$: $3w+3=33$, so $w=10$ and $l=23$. The area is $10\cdot23=230$. (10 and 23 are the sides; 33 is half the perimeter.)  *Trick: Read the last sentence first*

9. **C (\$2.00)** — The question asks for $n-p$, so subtract the second purchase from the first: $n-p=11.50-9.50=2.00$. No need for the separate prices (\$3.10 and \$1.10). Adding instead gives $5(n+p)=21$, so \$4.20 is the price of one of each.  *Trick: Subtract the equations*
